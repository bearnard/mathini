# Grade 7 — Natural Sciences & Technology: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10
**Note:** CAPS formally separates Natural Sciences and Technology from Grade 7; this file merges the main strands to match the requested combined syllabus view.

## Topics

### Energy transfer, heat and motion
- **Key concepts:** kinetic energy, potential energy, conduction, convection, radiation, insulation, efficiency
- **Skills / outcomes:** Interpret heat-transfer situations, compare energy stores, and investigate simple insulation ideas.
- **Assessment angles:** Energy-flow diagrams, heat-transfer comparisons, and short applied explanations.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Mechanical systems and control
- **Key concepts:** levers, gears, pulleys, linkages, motion conversion, mechanical advantage, systems and control
- **Skills / outcomes:** Identify mechanisms, predict movement changes, and build or evaluate simple models.
- **Assessment angles:** Mechanism diagrams, matching systems to functions, and control-system scenarios.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical systems task and term test.
- **SBA weighting:** SBA comes from practical demonstrations, design work, and written assessment.
