# Grade 7 — Natural Sciences & Technology: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10
**Note:** CAPS formally separates Natural Sciences and Technology from Grade 7; this file merges the main strands to match the requested combined syllabus view.

## Topics

### Matter, mixtures, acids and bases
- **Key concepts:** particle model, mixtures, solutions, acids, bases, indicators, periodic-table introduction
- **Skills / outcomes:** Classify substances, use indicators safely, and interpret simple evidence of chemical change.
- **Assessment angles:** pH-style tables, particle diagrams, and choosing suitable separation methods.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

### Processing materials and properties
- **Key concepts:** natural and processed materials, hardness, flexibility, shaping, joining, sustainability
- **Skills / outcomes:** Select materials for a purpose, describe processing steps, and evaluate finished products.
- **Assessment angles:** Material-selection questions, tool-safety items, and property comparisons.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Processing/design task and controlled test.
- **SBA weighting:** Projects and term tests contribute to the continuous assessment mark.
