# Grade 7 — Natural Sciences & Technology: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8
**Note:** CAPS formally separates Natural Sciences and Technology from Grade 7; this file merges the main strands to match the requested combined syllabus view.

## Topics

### Sun-Earth relationship and gravity
- **Key concepts:** seasons, Moon phases, tides, gravity, relative positions of Earth, Moon and Sun
- **Skills / outcomes:** Model seasonal change, interpret Moon-phase diagrams, and connect gravity to observed phenomena.
- **Assessment angles:** Orbit diagrams, tide questions, and sequences of phases.
- **Knowledge strand:** Planet Earth & Beyond

### Electrical systems and control
- **Key concepts:** series and parallel circuits, switches, inputs, processes, outputs, electrical safety
- **Skills / outcomes:** Build simple control systems, distinguish circuit types, and evaluate design solutions.
- **Assessment angles:** Circuit diagrams, fault-finding questions, and logic or switch scenarios.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project or systems task and end-of-year examination practice.
- **SBA weighting:** Formal tasks feed the school-based mark, with exam preparation becoming prominent.
