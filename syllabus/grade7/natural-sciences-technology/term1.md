# Grade 7 — Natural Sciences & Technology: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10
**Note:** CAPS formally separates Natural Sciences and Technology from Grade 7; this file merges the main strands to match the requested combined syllabus view.

## Topics

### Biosphere, biodiversity and reproduction
- **Key concepts:** biosphere, biodiversity, habitats, classification, sexual and asexual reproduction, pollination, seed dispersal
- **Skills / outcomes:** Classify living things, explain survival and reproduction strategies, and interpret life-cycle or reproduction diagrams.
- **Assessment angles:** Classifying organisms, labeled diagrams, and comparison questions on sexual vs asexual reproduction.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Design process and structures
- **Key concepts:** IDMEC, design brief, frame, shell and solid structures, tension, compression, stability
- **Skills / outcomes:** Investigate a problem, sketch a possible solution, and evaluate the strength of simple structures.
- **Assessment angles:** Force arrows, material-choice questions, and design-criteria interpretation.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Integrated investigation/design task and formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase SBA through projects, practical tasks, and written tests.
