# Grade 7 — English Home Language: Term 1

Source: CAPS English Home Language Senior Phase Gr 7-9, Grade 7 Terms 1-4; phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Novel or short story study
- **Key concepts:** character, plot, conflict, setting, theme, literary features
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify core literary elements and discuss how they shape meaning
- **Assessment angles:** matching character to action, setting and theme questions, plot sequencing

### Review and essay writing
- **Key concepts:** review format, narrative essay, reflective essay, planning, drafting
- **Skills / outcomes:** write a structured review and an extended essay suited to purpose and audience
- **Assessment angles:** format features, paragraph unity, conclusion choices

### Foundational grammar in context
- **Key concepts:** singular and plural, verb tenses, word order, direct and indirect speech
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply sentence-level accuracy while speaking and writing
- **Assessment angles:** tense correction, reported speech, word-order items, editing tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal oral reading or speaking begins, plus a major writing task such as a review or essay.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Grade 7 continues the integrated Intermediate Phase model but expects stronger independence in literary response and writing.
