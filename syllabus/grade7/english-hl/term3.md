# Grade 7 — English Home Language: Term 3

Source: CAPS English Home Language Senior Phase Gr 7-9, Grade 7 Terms 1-4; phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual literacy and persuasive texts
- **Key concepts:** advertisements, posters, persuasive language, target audience, layout
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse persuasive techniques and design or respond to visual texts
- **Assessment angles:** AIDA-style hooks, slogan questions, audience and purpose items

### Drama or continued novel study
- **Key concepts:** dialogue, stage action, conflict, theme, character response
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret dramatic features or deepen novel analysis through oral and written response
- **Assessment angles:** speaker identification, conflict and theme questions, short paragraph responses

### Project and language refinement
- **Key concepts:** project writing, active and passive voice, punctuation, clauses
- **Skills / outcomes:** sustain a longer project and edit language more deliberately
- **Assessment angles:** voice transformation, punctuation edits, project-planning questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Creative project or oral presentation, a writing task, and ongoing literature or language assessment.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 3 often centres on visual literacy and a larger project-style task.
