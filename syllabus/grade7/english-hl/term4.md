# Grade 7 — English Home Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Revision of all genres
- **Key concepts:** novel, poetry, drama, media texts, comprehension strategies
- **Skills / outcomes:** review core text types and respond more confidently under exam conditions
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-literature items, comprehension strategy questions, summary practice

### Exam writing practice
- **Key concepts:** essay types, transactional texts, planning, editing, timing
- **Skills / outcomes:** select suitable writing forms and complete them accurately in limited time
- **Assessment angles:** format recall, topic choice, error correction

### Grammar consolidation
- **Key concepts:** tenses, clauses, punctuation, vocabulary, language use in context
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply the year’s language knowledge across reading and writing tasks
- **Assessment angles:** language-in-context MCQs, editing, sentence correction

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of any remaining oral evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is primarily for consolidation, revision and end-of-year assessment.
