# Grade 7 — English Home Language: Term 2

Source: CAPS English Home Language Senior Phase Gr 7-9, Grade 7 Terms 1-4; phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Poetry and prescribed text work
- **Key concepts:** stanza, rhyme, imagery, tone, continued prescribed text study
- **Skills / outcomes:** read poems closely and connect poetry ideas to other texts studied in class
- **Assessment angles:** imagery questions, tone identification, line-reference items

### Formal and functional writing
- **Key concepts:** formal letter, email, dialogue, descriptive essay, register
- **Skills / outcomes:** use the correct format and tone for formal correspondence and descriptive writing
- **Assessment angles:** register selection, layout conventions, audience-purpose matching

### Grammar for cohesion
- **Key concepts:** adjectives, adverbs, conjunctions, sentence links
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve detail and flow in oral and written texts
- **Assessment angles:** sentence joining, modifier choice, coherence questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A controlled test covering comprehension, visual literacy and language structures, plus a formal writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
ATPs commonly use Term 2 for poetry analysis and more formal registers in writing.
