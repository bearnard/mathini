# Grade 7 — Social Sciences: Geography: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Map skills in the local area
- **Key concepts:** local maps, street maps, route finding, sketch maps, scale, latitude and longitude, places in the news
- **Skills / outcomes:** mapwork, atlas use, graph reading, case-study discussion, short explanatory writing
- **Assessment angles:** map labels, graph interpretation, case-study questions, short paragraphs and data-response items
- **Historical period / geographical region:** Local area and world reference maps

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Geography project/investigation plus mapwork or data task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; tasks contribute to the annual Social Sciences programme.
