# Grade 7 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 1

Source: CAPS Afrikaans Eerste Addisionele Taal Senior Phase Graad 7-9, Graad 7 Kwartaal 1-4; phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Short stories and reviews
- **Key concepts:** kortverhale, character, plot, setting, theme, resensie
- **Skills / outcomes:** read short stories, discuss key literary elements and write a simple review
- **Assessment angles:** plot and theme prompts, matching character to action, review conventions

### Extended responses
- **Key concepts:** narrative and descriptive writing, audience, drafting, paragraphing
- **Skills / outcomes:** write more developed responses with clearer organisation and detail
- **Assessment angles:** paragraph order, opening and closing choices, audience fit

### Word and sentence level grammar
- **Key concepts:** singular and plural, adjectives, verbs, present, past and future tense, homophones, idioms
- **Skills / outcomes:** use common word classes and tenses more accurately in context
- **Assessment angles:** meervoude, tense changes, idiom and vocabulary hooks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal oral work begins together with a major writing task such as a review or essay.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Grade 7 continues the integrated Intermediate Phase approach, but literary and writing responses become more explicit and structured.
