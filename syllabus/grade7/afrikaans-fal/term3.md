# Grade 7 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual literacy and persuasion
- **Key concepts:** advertisements, posters, AIDA, persuasive language, audience
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify persuasive devices and respond to visual texts critically
- **Assessment angles:** AIDA prompts, slogan analysis, target-audience questions

### Creative writing project
- **Key concepts:** drafting, revising, booklet or magazine task, presentation
- **Skills / outcomes:** sustain a longer writing project with teacher and peer feedback
- **Assessment angles:** project structure, planning stages, presentation prompts

### Language reinforcement
- **Key concepts:** comprehension, punctuation, sentence control, vocabulary
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve accuracy and confidence across spoken and written Afrikaans tasks
- **Assessment angles:** editing tasks, punctuation, vocabulary in context

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project-style writing or oral presentation, plus a formal language or comprehension task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
ATPs often use Term 3 for visual literacy and a larger process-writing activity.
