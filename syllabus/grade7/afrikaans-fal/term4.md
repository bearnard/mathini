# Grade 7 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 4

Source: CAPS Afrikaans Eerste Addisionele Taal Senior Phase Graad 7-9, Graad 7 Kwartaal 1-4; phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Revision of genres and skills
- **Key concepts:** stories, poems, visual texts, letters, comprehension
- **Skills / outcomes:** review the main Grade 7 genres and respond more confidently under assessment conditions
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-genre items, summary and retrieval questions

### Exam writing practice
- **Key concepts:** essay forms, formal and informal texts, editing checklist, timing
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply format and language conventions under time pressure
- **Assessment angles:** format recall, editing, topic suitability

### Language consolidation
- **Key concepts:** tenses, parts of speech, punctuation, vocabulary, sentence structure
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate the year’s language structures in context
- **Assessment angles:** MCQ-style grammar, error correction, sentence building

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of any outstanding oral evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is primarily for revision and preparation for the Senior Phase.
