# Grade 7 — Mathematics: Term 2

Source: CAPS Mathematics Senior Phase Gr 7-9, Time allocation per topic Grade 7 (p. 38) and 3.3.1 Clarification of content Grade 7 (pp. 39-70); phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Common Fractions
- **Key concepts:** equivalent fractions, comparing and ordering, operations with common fractions and mixed numbers
- **Skills / outcomes:** simplify, compare and calculate with fractions and solve sharing or grouping problems
- **Assessment angles:** common denominator errors, mixed-number operations, fraction of quantity questions
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Decimal Fractions
- **Key concepts:** place value, ordering, comparison and calculations with decimals
- **Skills / outcomes:** add, subtract, multiply and divide decimals and connect decimals to money and measures
- **Assessment angles:** decimal alignment, decimal ordering, decimal-operation fluency, word problems
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Functions and Relationships
- **Key concepts:** input-output relationships, tables, rules and simple number sentences
- **Skills / outcomes:** move between tables, flow diagrams and rules and describe relationships clearly
- **Assessment angles:** finding rules, missing values, inverse processes, table interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 3 hours

### Area and Perimeter of 2D Shapes
- **Key concepts:** formulae and strategies for rectangles and composite 2D figures
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate perimeter and area and apply formulae in practical contexts
- **Assessment angles:** perimeter vs area confusion, unit use, composite-shape breakdown
- **Approximate time:** About 7 hours

### Surface Area and Volume of 3D Objects
- **Key concepts:** nets, prisms, cubes and volume of right prisms
- **Skills / outcomes:** visualise solids from nets and calculate volume or surface measures in context
- **Assessment angles:** net recognition, counting faces, volume calculations, units cubed
- **Approximate time:** About 8 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** One formal test, one investigation and the mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
