# Grade 7 — Mathematics: Term 4

Source: CAPS Mathematics Senior Phase Gr 7-9, Time allocation per topic Grade 7 (p. 38) and 3.3.1 Clarification of content Grade 7 (pp. 39-70); phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Integers
- **Key concepts:** number line representation, ordering and the four operations with integers
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare signed numbers and calculate accurately with positive and negative values
- **Assessment angles:** sign rules, ordering on the number line, contextual integer questions
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Patterns, Functions and Algebra Consolidation
- **Key concepts:** numeric patterns, function rules, expressions and equations revisited at higher difficulty
- **Skills / outcomes:** connect symbolic work to patterns and solve algebraic problems from tables or words
- **Assessment angles:** finding unknowns, pattern rules, function tables, simplifying symbolic forms
- **Approximate time:** About 13 hours

### Data Handling
- **Key concepts:** collecting, organising, representing and interpreting data using tables and graphs
- **Skills / outcomes:** summarise data, read scales and draw conclusions from bar graphs, pie charts or tables
- **Assessment angles:** graph interpretation, data summary statements, mode or median use
- **Approximate time:** About 10.5 hours

### Probability
- **Key concepts:** simple experiments, equally likely outcomes and relative frequency
- **Skills / outcomes:** list outcomes, calculate simple probabilities and compare experimental to theoretical results
- **Assessment angles:** sample-space questions, fair-vs-unfair contexts, probability as fraction
- **Approximate time:** About 4.5 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment, investigation and end-of-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
