# Grade 7 — Mathematics: Term 1

Source: CAPS Mathematics Senior Phase Gr 7-9, Time allocation per topic Grade 7 (p. 38) and 3.3.1 Clarification of content Grade 7 (pp. 39-70); phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Whole Numbers
- **Key concepts:** place value, factors and multiples, prime factors of 3-digit numbers, HCF, LCM, ratio and rate in more complex contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate fluently, use factorisation strategies, compare quantities and solve financial or measurement problems
- **Assessment angles:** prime factor decomposition, HCF/LCM questions, ratio vs rate language, whole-number problem solving
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Exponents
- **Key concepts:** powers, squares, cubes and the basic laws of exponents
- **Skills / outcomes:** write and simplify exponential expressions and recognise equivalent forms
- **Assessment angles:** power notation, simplifying with exponent laws, square and cube patterns
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Construction of Geometric Figures
- **Key concepts:** accurate use of ruler, protractor and compass, construction of angles, triangles and quadrilaterals
- **Skills / outcomes:** construct shapes to instruction, measure and justify geometric properties
- **Assessment angles:** instrument use, angle construction, triangle construction accuracy, locus-style basics
- **Approximate time:** About 10 hours

### Geometry of 2D Shapes and Straight Lines
- **Key concepts:** properties of triangles, quadrilaterals and polygons, straight-line angle relationships, classification of shapes
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify, compare and classify 2D figures and use angle facts when lines intersect or run parallel
- **Assessment angles:** shape classification, missing-angle problems, reasoning from properties, line-angle relationships
- **Approximate time:** About 12 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** One formal test and one assignment.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
