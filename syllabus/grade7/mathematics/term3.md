# Grade 7 — Mathematics: Term 3

Source: CAPS Mathematics Senior Phase Gr 7-9, Time allocation per topic Grade 7 (p. 38) and 3.3.1 Clarification of content Grade 7 (pp. 39-70); phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Numeric and Geometric Patterns
- **Key concepts:** recognising rules, extending sequences, verbal and symbolic descriptions of patterns
- **Skills / outcomes:** extend patterns, describe general rules and identify how a pattern grows
- **Assessment angles:** next-term and missing-term items, rule descriptions, geometric pattern growth
- **Approximate time:** About 6 hours

### Functions, Algebraic Expressions and Equations
- **Key concepts:** input-output values, simple expressions, collecting like terms, simple equations
- **Skills / outcomes:** translate between words and symbols, simplify expressions and solve basic equations
- **Assessment angles:** verbal-to-symbol translation, substitution, balancing equations, simplifying expressions
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Graphs
- **Key concepts:** ordered pairs, coordinate grids and drawing or interpreting simple graphs
- **Skills / outcomes:** plot points, read coordinates and interpret relationships shown graphically
- **Assessment angles:** coordinate errors, graph reading, linking tables to graphs
- **Approximate time:** About 6 hours

### Transformation Geometry and 3D Objects
- **Key concepts:** translations, reflections, rotations, simple enlargements and properties of 3D objects
- **Skills / outcomes:** describe transformations precisely and classify solids by faces, edges and vertices
- **Assessment angles:** which transformation happened, congruence ideas, 3D object identification, nets
- **Approximate time:** About 18 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** One formal test plus an assignment or project task.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
