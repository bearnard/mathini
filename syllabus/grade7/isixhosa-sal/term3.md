# Grade 7 — isiXhosa Second Additional Language: Term 3

Source: CAPS isiXhosa Second Additional Language Senior Phase Gr 7-9, section 3.5.1 Ibanga lesi-7 Ikota 1-4 (pp. 37-46); phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** culture, environment, folktales, opinion giving, interviews
- **Skills / outcomes:** retell stories, compare cultural practices and give simple reasons for opinions
- **Assessment angles:** oral retell rubrics, opinion prompts, interview role plays

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** poems, folktales, posters, short factual texts, picture sequences
- **Skills / outcomes:** read a wider range of texts and identify mood, message and important details
- **Assessment angles:** poetry questions, picture-sequence tasks, poster interpretation

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** invitation, short report, directions, cultural paragraph, recount
- **Skills / outcomes:** present ideas in short paragraphs and functional texts linked to community and cultural topics
- **Assessment angles:** format checks, recount writing, peer feedback prompts

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** connectors, conjunctions, past and future tense, concords, punctuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** link ideas more smoothly and vary sentence patterns across tasks
- **Assessment angles:** connector tasks, editing items, tense and concord exercises

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A formal oral or practical writing task, plus a controlled language or comprehension test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
