# Grade 7 — isiXhosa Second Additional Language: Term 4

Source: CAPS isiXhosa Second Additional Language Senior Phase Gr 7-9, section 3.5.1 Ibanga lesi-7 Ikota 1-4 (pp. 37-46); phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** health, feelings, future plans, revision talks, discussion skills
- **Skills / outcomes:** give short prepared talks and revise familiar vocabulary through pair and group discussion
- **Assessment angles:** speech rubrics, vocabulary recall, listening quizzes

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** revision passages, comprehension texts, notices, stories, short summaries
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise reading skills across genres and select key information from short texts
- **Assessment angles:** summary selection, comprehension questions, matching tasks

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** personal recount, short review, message writing, revision paragraphs
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce clearer paragraphs and short reviews with relevant details and accurate format
- **Assessment angles:** writing rubrics, editing tasks, paragraph-planning prompts

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** revision of tenses, pronouns, question forms, concords, spelling
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate language structures for end-of-year assessment and Grade 8 readiness
- **Assessment angles:** grammar MCQs, sentence correction, proofreading tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Year-end examination and consolidation of oral and writing evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
