# Grade 7 — isiXhosa Second Additional Language: Term 1

Source: CAPS isiXhosa Second Additional Language Senior Phase Gr 7-9, section 3.5.1 Ibanga lesi-7 Ikota 1-4 (pp. 37-46); phase label corrected to Senior Phase (Gr 7-9). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** introductions, hobbies, daily routine, community vocabulary, asking for information
- **Skills / outcomes:** hold slightly longer dialogues and present short oral descriptions of self, school and interests
- **Assessment angles:** dialogue rubrics, oral question prompts, pronunciation checks

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** stories, advertisements, notices, short poems, visual literacy
- **Skills / outcomes:** read familiar texts with better fluency and answer basic comprehension and visual-literacy questions
- **Assessment angles:** ad analysis, sequencing tasks, retrieval questions

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** friendly letter, personal paragraph, dialogue, captions, short descriptions
- **Skills / outcomes:** write more connected sentences and simple paragraphs for real audiences and purposes
- **Assessment angles:** layout checks, guided paragraph writing, dialogue punctuation tasks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** present, past and future tense, plurals, possessives, vocabulary building, punctuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** use a wider range of forms and vocabulary in short oral and written tasks
- **Assessment angles:** tense-change items, plural forms, editing exercises

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** An oral interaction, a guided writing task and routine comprehension or language checks.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
