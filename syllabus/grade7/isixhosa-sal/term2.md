# Grade 7 — isiXhosa Second Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** shopping, transport, service encounters, giving directions, polite requests
- **Skills / outcomes:** take part in role plays about buying, asking for help and moving through familiar places
- **Assessment angles:** role-play rubrics, listening-for-detail items, map-direction tasks

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** news snippets, folk stories, simple informational texts, timetables, signs
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify key details and compare short fictional and factual texts
- **Assessment angles:** main-idea questions, matching tasks, fact-versus-fiction prompts

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** diary entry, short report, dialogue, instructions, messages
- **Skills / outcomes:** write short functional texts with clearer sequencing and audience awareness
- **Assessment angles:** format recognition, sentence-order tasks, guided report writing

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** negatives, question forms, pronouns, subject concords, adjectives
- **Skills / outcomes:** build more accurate questions and responses and improve sentence control
- **Assessment angles:** gap-fill items, question-building prompts, concord checks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal writing plus an oral task and a mid-year test or examination component.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
