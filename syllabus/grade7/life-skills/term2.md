# Grade 7 — Life Skills: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Personal and Social Well-being
- **Key concepts:** peer influence, substance abuse, online safety, communication, empathy
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse pressure from friends or media, practise assertive communication and plan safer online behaviour
- **Assessment angles:** problem-solution paragraphs, anti-drug poster hooks, digital-safety role play

### Physical Education
- **Key concepts:** sport-specific skills, passing and receiving, stamina, rules, fair competition
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply skills in modified matches and show leadership, discipline and fair play
- **Assessment angles:** small-game rubrics, skills ladders, officiating or rule-application prompts

### Creative Arts
- **Key concepts:** poster design, characterisation, song structure, movement motifs, audience
- **Skills / outcomes:** plan a themed poster or performance piece and shape message, mood and audience response
- **Assessment angles:** design briefs, short performance tasks, peer critiques on message and impact

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A formal PSW task, a PE skills assessment and a Creative Arts practical or presentation.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; PSW, PE and Creative Arts tasks feed the annual assessment programme.
