# Grade 7 — Life Skills: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Personal and Social Well-being
- **Key concepts:** diversity, human rights, gender stereotypes, career awareness, social change
- **Skills / outcomes:** debate inclusion, identify stereotypes and connect interests and strengths to future pathways
- **Assessment angles:** rights-based discussions, stereotype challenge items, career-interest mind maps

### Physical Education
- **Key concepts:** athletics, indigenous games, flexibility, endurance, teamwork
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve running and coordination, participate in indigenous games and track personal progress
- **Assessment angles:** timed events, movement analysis, indigenous-game leadership tasks

### Creative Arts
- **Key concepts:** South African heritage, mixed media, scene building, choreography, evaluation
- **Skills / outcomes:** research cultural forms and turn them into more polished artworks or performances
- **Assessment angles:** heritage presentation rubrics, process evidence, reflective evaluation questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** An integrated project or oral, a PE movement assessment and a Creative Arts performance or artwork.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; PSW, PE and Creative Arts tasks feed the annual assessment programme.
