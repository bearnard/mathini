# Grade 7 — Life Skills: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Personal and Social Well-being
- **Key concepts:** goal setting, exam stress, community responsibility, healthy routines, future planning
- **Skills / outcomes:** set realistic goals, manage stress and explain how responsible habits support learning and wellbeing
- **Assessment angles:** study-planner checks, stress-management scenarios, community-service reflections

### Physical Education
- **Key concepts:** personal fitness plans, game strategy, participation review, recreation
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply movement skills in game situations and evaluate personal improvement across the year
- **Assessment angles:** fitness-plan reviews, strategy talks, participation logs

### Creative Arts
- **Key concepts:** portfolio curation, rehearsal discipline, integrated showcase, self-evaluation
- **Skills / outcomes:** select work for a mini-portfolio and polish an integrated presentation for an audience
- **Assessment angles:** portfolio rubrics, showcase performance, self- and peer-assessment prompts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A year-end consolidation task, a PE participation record and a Creative Arts showcase or portfolio.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; PSW, PE and Creative Arts tasks feed the annual assessment programme.
