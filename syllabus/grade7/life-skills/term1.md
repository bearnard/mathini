# Grade 7 — Life Skills: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Personal and Social Well-being
- **Key concepts:** self-concept, values, puberty, emotions, decision-making
- **Skills / outcomes:** reflect on changing identity, manage emotions and make responsible choices in everyday situations
- **Assessment angles:** values-ranking activities, puberty myth-and-fact items, decision-tree questions

### Physical Education
- **Key concepts:** fitness baselines, movement patterns, coordination, cooperation, safe warm-ups
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete simple fitness checks, improve coordination and support others in cooperative tasks
- **Assessment angles:** baseline fitness records, paired challenges, warm-up design tasks

### Creative Arts
- **Key concepts:** observational drawing, texture, rhythm patterns, ensemble voice, improvisation
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine visual observation, perform rhythmic pieces and improvise short scenes with clearer structure
- **Assessment angles:** sketchbook rubrics, ensemble timing checks, improvisation cue cards

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A PSW reflection or poster task, a PE practical observation and a Creative Arts practical activity.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; PSW, PE and Creative Arts tasks feed the annual assessment programme.
