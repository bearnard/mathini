# Grade 9 — Mathematics: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Construction of Geometric Figures
- **Key concepts:** geometric constructions with standard tools and justification of results
- **Skills / outcomes:** construct accurately and connect construction steps to known properties
- **Assessment angles:** triangle and angle constructions, precision and notation
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Geometry of 2D Shapes and Straight Lines
- **Key concepts:** properties of triangles, quadrilaterals and polygons; angle relationships with intersecting and parallel lines
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve geometry problems using properties and provide reasons for each step
- **Assessment angles:** deductive geometry, line-angle facts, interior and exterior angle questions
- **Approximate time:** About 18 hours

### Theorem of Pythagoras
- **Key concepts:** right-triangle relationships and unknown side calculations
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply the theorem in geometric and practical contexts and interpret answers with units
- **Assessment angles:** selecting the correct side, application in diagrams, calculator rounding
- **Approximate time:** About 5 hours

### Area and Perimeter of 2D Shapes
- **Key concepts:** formulae and problem solving for compound 2D figures
- **Skills / outcomes:** break complex figures into simpler parts and calculate accurately
- **Assessment angles:** composite shapes, unit consistency, perimeter-area comparison
- **Approximate time:** About 5 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** One formal test, one investigation and the mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
