# Grade 9 — Mathematics: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Number Systems and Operations
- **Key concepts:** real number system overview, operations with whole numbers, integers, common fractions and decimal fractions
- **Skills / outcomes:** distinguish number types, apply order of operations and solve contextual numerical problems accurately
- **Assessment angles:** classifying numbers, rational vs irrational ideas, signed-number fluency, fraction-decimal calculation traps
- **Approximate time:** About 18 hours

### Exponents
- **Key concepts:** laws of exponents and equivalent exponential representations
- **Skills / outcomes:** simplify and compare exponential forms and solve exponent-based calculations
- **Assessment angles:** negative exponents, zero exponents, simplifying multi-step expressions
- **Approximate time:** About 5 hours

### Numeric and Geometric Patterns; Functions and Relationships
- **Key concepts:** pattern generalisation, rules, tables and function thinking
- **Skills / outcomes:** derive general rules, identify input-output relationships and justify pattern growth
- **Assessment angles:** pattern-to-rule translation, missing terms, recognising linear growth
- **Approximate time:** About 8.5 hours

### Algebraic Expressions and Equations
- **Key concepts:** simplifying, factorising and solving equations with greater symbolic sophistication
- **Skills / outcomes:** manipulate expressions, solve equations and interpret algebra in context
- **Assessment angles:** factorisation errors, solving multi-step equations, algebraic word problems
- **Approximate time:** About 8.5 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** One formal test and one assignment.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
