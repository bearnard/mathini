# Grade 9 — Mathematics: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Functions and Relationships
- **Key concepts:** formalising relations between variables and using multiple representations
- **Skills / outcomes:** connect tables, formulae and graphs and explain trends meaningfully
- **Assessment angles:** input-output reasoning, matching forms, context interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 5 hours

### Algebraic Expressions and Equations
- **Key concepts:** advanced simplification, factorisation and equation solving
- **Skills / outcomes:** work with symbolic forms confidently and solve non-routine algebraic problems
- **Assessment angles:** factorising harder expressions, solving symbolic equations, substitution
- **Approximate time:** About 18 hours

### Graphs
- **Key concepts:** drawing and interpreting linear and non-linear graphs on the Cartesian plane
- **Skills / outcomes:** plot accurately, interpret slope and shape informally, and answer graph-based questions
- **Assessment angles:** graph-reading, intercept ideas, linking formulae to plotted shape
- **Approximate time:** About 12 hours

### Surface Area and Volume of 3D Objects
- **Key concepts:** surface area and volume of common 3D forms and composite objects
- **Skills / outcomes:** choose correct formulae and interpret 3D measurement answers in context
- **Assessment angles:** surface area vs volume, net links, unit cubed or squared errors
- **Approximate time:** About 5 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** One formal test plus an assignment or project task.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
