# Grade 9 — Mathematics: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Transformation Geometry
- **Key concepts:** translations, reflections, rotations and enlargements on coordinate axes
- **Skills / outcomes:** describe transformations precisely and use coordinates to verify results
- **Assessment angles:** image-coordinate questions, enlargement factors, symmetry on the plane
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Geometry of 3D Objects
- **Key concepts:** properties, classification and spatial reasoning with solids and nets
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare solids, analyse nets and describe structural properties accurately
- **Assessment angles:** faces-edges-vertices counts, choosing correct net, classifying solids
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Data Handling
- **Key concepts:** collecting, representing, analysing and reporting data using appropriate summary measures
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare data sets critically and discuss bias, spread and representation choices
- **Assessment angles:** graph critique, mean/median/mode/range, reading tables and charts
- **Approximate time:** About 10.5 hours

### Probability
- **Key concepts:** simple and compound events, tree diagrams or tables and relative frequency
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate probabilities, compare outcomes and explain likelihood in context
- **Assessment angles:** compound-event questions, mutually exclusive thinking, reading tree diagrams
- **Approximate time:** About 4.5 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment, investigation and end-of-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
