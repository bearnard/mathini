# Grade 9 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** speeches, discussions, listening comprehension, interviews, fluency
- **Skills / outcomes:** present short speeches and engage more critically in discussions and listening tasks
- **Assessment angles:** speech rubrics, note-taking from listening, interview prompts

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** short stories, visual texts, poetry, theme, conflict, setting
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse literary and visual texts with clearer attention to theme, tone and supporting evidence
- **Assessment angles:** theme prompts, visual-analysis items, contextual questions

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** narrative and descriptive essays, personal reflection, friendly letter, planning
- **Skills / outcomes:** write more developed responses with clearer structure, detail and audience awareness
- **Assessment angles:** paragraph unity, tone questions, planning and drafting checks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** tenses, idiomatic vocabulary, concords, direct and indirect speech, punctuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** use a wider range of language structures accurately in context
- **Assessment angles:** tense conversion, idiom hooks, editing tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal oral work begins together with a major writing or comprehension task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
