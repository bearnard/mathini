# Grade 9 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** revision of speeches, listening practice, oral feedback, fluency
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate speaking and listening routines for end-of-year assessment
- **Assessment angles:** oral revision tasks, listening quizzes, fluency checks

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** revision across literature, visual texts, comprehension and summary
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply the year’s reading strategies in exam-style contexts and revision activities
- **Assessment angles:** summary questions, literature revision prompts, visual recall items

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** report, review, summary, exam essays, proofreading
- **Skills / outcomes:** write timed responses more confidently and revise key transactional formats
- **Assessment angles:** planning under pressure, format recall, proofreading tasks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** revision of all major grammar points, punctuation, sentence control
- **Skills / outcomes:** correct common errors and prepare for the move into FET language demands
- **Assessment angles:** grammar MCQs, sentence correction, editing items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of project or oral evidence from earlier terms.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
