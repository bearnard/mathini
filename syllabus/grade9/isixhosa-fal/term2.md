# Grade 9 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** prepared talks, interviews, summarising, group discussion, role play
- **Skills / outcomes:** summarise spoken information and communicate more confidently in formal and informal situations
- **Assessment angles:** summary-from-listening tasks, oral rubrics, discussion questions

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** novel or folklore study, media texts, characterisation, message, visual literacy
- **Skills / outcomes:** read longer texts with stronger focus on character growth, conflict and social themes
- **Assessment angles:** character evidence, plot sequencing, media-literacy prompts

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** formal email, review, report, dialogue, transactional writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** write more formal transactional texts and maintain correct register and format
- **Assessment angles:** register checks, report structure, review-language questions

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** connectors, passive voice, pronouns, question tags, concord
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve coherence and accuracy across longer written responses
- **Assessment angles:** connector items, passive voice prompts, concord checks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Controlled test, oral presentation and a formal writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
