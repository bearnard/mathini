# Grade 9 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** debate, prepared speech, oral response to literature, panel discussion
- **Skills / outcomes:** argue a point of view more clearly and respond to literature and social issues in spoken form
- **Assessment angles:** debate rubrics, oral-response prompts, listening evaluation

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** drama, poetry, short stories, message, tone, character motivation
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret prescribed or school-based literary texts with more independence and depth
- **Assessment angles:** tone questions, quotation prompts, contextual response items

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** discursive paragraph, formal letter, article, project writing, presentation
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete a longer project and switch effectively between reflective and formal writing tasks
- **Assessment angles:** project evidence, format recall, audience-purpose matching

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** complex sentences, conjunctions, punctuation, editing, vocabulary refinement
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine sentence structure and edit work carefully for clarity and correctness
- **Assessment angles:** error correction, sentence-combining tasks, vocabulary items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project submission or oral component, plus a formal language or writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
