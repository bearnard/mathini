# Grade 9 — Economic & Management Sciences: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 11–20

## Topics

### Entrepreneurship and financial planning
- **Key concepts:** successful business traits, business opportunities, budgeting, financial planning, continuation of financial literacy, small-business decisions
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret case studies, classify transactions, record journals and ledgers, compare business roles, justify simple decisions
- **Assessment angles:** definitions, source-document questions, journal entries, short case studies and controlled-test items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project or assignment plus ongoing practical accounting tasks.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; EMS tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
