# Grade 9 — English Home Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Poetry and drama study
- **Key concepts:** literary devices, characterisation, theme, dialogue, scene analysis
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse drama and poetry more formally and support responses with evidence
- **Assessment angles:** device identification, quotation-to-theme matching, contextual questions

### Letters and reviews
- **Key concepts:** friendly letter, formal letter, review, process writing, register
- **Skills / outcomes:** adapt tone and format for personal and formal communication
- **Assessment angles:** register shifts, layout conventions, review criteria

### Sentence structure and cohesion
- **Key concepts:** pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, simple, compound and complex sentences, tense consistency
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve cohesion and grammatical accuracy in extended responses
- **Assessment angles:** sentence-combining, pronoun reference, tense consistency items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal essay or transactional writing, plus a test covering comprehension, literature and language.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 typically deepens literature work and introduces more mature formal writing expectations.
