# Grade 9 — English Home Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Advertising and visual design
- **Key concepts:** advertisements, posters, tone, emotive language, AIDA, target audience
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret persuasive techniques and explain how visual and verbal choices influence readers
- **Assessment angles:** AIDA prompts, slogan analysis, emotive-language items

### Essay and transactional writing
- **Key concepts:** narrative, descriptive, reflective, argumentative essays, poster copy, process writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** select suitable essay forms and craft transactional responses for purpose and audience
- **Assessment angles:** essay-type recognition, planning stages, audience-purpose matching

### Grammar revision and accuracy
- **Key concepts:** nouns, adjectives, verbs, spelling, punctuation, direct and indirect speech
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate prior grammar while editing and extending own writing
- **Assessment angles:** editing passages, speech conversion, punctuation choices

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Baseline diagnostic work, a formal writing task, and a first-term control test or oral task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Grade 9 closes the Senior Phase, so ATPs often emphasise persuasive reading, controlled writing and revision of core grammar.
