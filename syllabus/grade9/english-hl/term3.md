# Grade 9 — English Home Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Oral literature and narrative traditions
- **Key concepts:** folktales, myths, oral presentation, interview, instruction
- **Skills / outcomes:** explain features of oral traditions and present information clearly to listeners
- **Assessment angles:** genre features, sequencing, oral-response prompts

### Reports and instructional texts
- **Key concepts:** report writing, narrative control, instructional writing, audience awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** write functional texts that organise information clearly and accurately
- **Assessment angles:** text-type features, ordering information, purpose questions

### Advanced language in context
- **Key concepts:** active and passive voice, reported speech, figurative language, complex sentence patterns
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify and use more advanced grammar and stylistic choices in context
- **Assessment angles:** voice transformation, figurative language, sentence transformation

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Oral presentation, formal writing task and controlled test as exam preparation builds.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 3 commonly mixes oral work, functional writing and stronger language-in-context assessment.
