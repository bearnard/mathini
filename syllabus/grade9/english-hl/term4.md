# Grade 9 — English Home Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Synoptic revision
- **Key concepts:** all genres, comprehension strategies, summary, retrieval of evidence
- **Skills / outcomes:** review the full year’s genres and respond strategically to exam-style questions
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-paper items, summary skills, evidence selection

### Examination writing practice
- **Key concepts:** letters, essays, planning, editing, timed responses
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce accurate extended and transactional pieces under time pressure
- **Assessment angles:** format recall, topic suitability, common editing errors

### Grammar and literature consolidation
- **Key concepts:** integrated language revision, literary themes, vocabulary, punctuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** draw together language and literature knowledge for final assessment
- **Assessment angles:** integrated MCQ hooks, short contextual questions, grammar revision

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of outstanding oral evidence if required.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is dominated by revision and exam readiness for progression into the FET phase.
