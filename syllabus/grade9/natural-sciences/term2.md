# Grade 9 — Natural Sciences: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Atoms, elements and compounds
- **Key concepts:** atomic structure, molecules, elements, compounds, mixtures, symbols, formulae, periodic table
- **Skills / outcomes:** Build particle models, distinguish matter types, and interpret formulae and symbols.
- **Assessment angles:** Particle drawings, formula questions, and periodic-table interpretation.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

### Chemical reactions, acids, bases and salts
- **Key concepts:** reactants, products, conservation of mass, pH, acids, bases, neutralisation, salts
- **Skills / outcomes:** Observe reactions, classify changes, use indicator information, and write simple word equations.
- **Assessment angles:** pH tables, signs of reaction, and reaction-summary questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Chemistry practical and mid-year test or exam task.
- **SBA weighting:** Written and practical tasks feed the school-based mark before the mid-year assessment.
