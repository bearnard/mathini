# Grade 9 — Technology: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Hydraulics, pneumatics and Pascal’s principle
- **Key concepts:** fluid pressure, syringes, pneumatic systems, hydraulic systems, force multiplication, Pascal’s principle
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare hydraulic and pneumatic systems, explain pressure transfer, and solve simple mechanical advantage problems.
- **Assessment angles:** System diagrams, Pascal-principle applications, and short calculation items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

### Gears and pulleys
- **Key concepts:** fixed and movable pulleys, block and tackle, spur gears, bevel gears, worm gears, rack and pinion
- **Skills / outcomes:** Predict direction and speed changes and choose mechanisms for a practical purpose.
- **Assessment angles:** Gear-train diagrams, pulley identification, and mechanism-matching questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Mechanical systems task and controlled test.
- **SBA weighting:** Theory and practical design work both feed the SBA mark.
