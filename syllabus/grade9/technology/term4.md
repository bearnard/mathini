# Grade 9 — Technology: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Processing of metals, food and plastics
- **Key concepts:** corrosion prevention, galvanising, electroplating, food preservation, plastics, recycling, processing choices
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare processing methods, select suitable materials, and evaluate environmental impact.
- **Assessment angles:** Case studies, process-flow diagrams, and sustainability questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

### Technology in society and exam consolidation
- **Key concepts:** systems integration, sustainability, safety, communication of designs, review of PAT work
- **Skills / outcomes:** Synthesise knowledge across the year and present clear exam-style answers.
- **Assessment angles:** Mixed diagrams, structured responses, and environmental/economic trade-off questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** End-of-year examination and PAT consolidation.
- **SBA weighting:** Internal PAT and test evidence remain important, while the final exam has major promotional weight.
