# Grade 9 — Technology: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Graphic communication and orthographic drawing
- **Key concepts:** first-angle orthographic projection, front/top/side views, scale, line types, dimension awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** Draw 2D views from 3D objects and interpret basic technical drawings accurately.
- **Assessment angles:** Projection tasks, line-convention questions, and scale interpretation.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

### Structures and accessible design
- **Key concepts:** static and dynamic forces, bending, torsion, material properties, stairs, wheelchair ramp, IDMEC
- **Skills / outcomes:** Develop a design brief, build a scale model, and evaluate accessibility and safety.
- **Assessment angles:** Force analysis, design-criteria questions, and model-evaluation prompts.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PAT 1 structure/design task and formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** PATs and tests are the major contributors to the internal technology mark.
