# Grade 9 — Technology: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Electrical circuits and Ohm’s law
- **Key concepts:** current, voltage, resistance, series and parallel circuits, V=IR, resistor colour codes
- **Skills / outcomes:** Calculate unknown electrical quantities, interpret circuit behaviour, and test simple circuits.
- **Assessment angles:** Circuit calculations, colour-code items, and fault-finding questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

### Electronic control and PAT 2
- **Key concepts:** diodes, LEDs, LDRs, thermistors, transistors, AND/OR switching, control devices
- **Skills / outcomes:** Design an electronic solution, assemble components, and evaluate device performance.
- **Assessment angles:** Component-symbol matching, sensor scenarios, and design-justification prompts.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PAT 2 electronic-system task and formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** PAT evidence is central, supported by calculation-based theory assessment.
