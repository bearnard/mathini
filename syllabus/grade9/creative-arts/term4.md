# Grade 9 — Creative Arts: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Visual Arts
- **Key concepts:** portfolio moderation, exhibition curation, presentation, arts careers
- **Skills / outcomes:** select and present portfolio work with clearer critical reflection and professional finish
- **Assessment angles:** portfolio rubrics, curation plans, reflection paragraphs

### Music
- **Key concepts:** performance showcase, aural revision, score reading, music careers
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare a final performance and revise theory and listening skills for summative tasks
- **Assessment angles:** aural recall, score-reading questions, final performance rubrics

### Drama
- **Key concepts:** final production, technical theatre, reflection, evaluation
- **Skills / outcomes:** stage a short final production and evaluate acting, design and teamwork choices
- **Assessment angles:** production rubrics, backstage role checks, reflective responses

### Dance
- **Key concepts:** final choreography, appreciation, refinement, community performance
- **Skills / outcomes:** perform a polished routine and discuss how dance communicates ideas to an audience
- **Assessment angles:** final practical assessment, appreciation questions, peer feedback

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A final practical examination or showcase plus end-of-year theory or literacy assessment.
- **SBA weighting:** CAPS balances practical work with written arts-literacy tasks; no single term percentage is fixed in the ATP.
