# Grade 9 — Creative Arts: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual Arts
- **Key concepts:** identity and environment themes, mixed media, symbolism, critique
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce a personal themed artwork and discuss choices of media, symbols and composition
- **Assessment angles:** artist statements, critique prompts, themed-project rubrics

### Music
- **Key concepts:** musical form, arrangement, composition, technology awareness, performance practice
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse short forms, arrange simple material and rehearse with greater independence
- **Assessment angles:** form-analysis items, arrangement checklists, rehearsal observations

### Drama
- **Key concepts:** script writing, character motivation, rehearsal process, collaboration, audience impact
- **Skills / outcomes:** write or adapt scenes and rehearse performances that communicate mood and message clearly
- **Assessment angles:** script drafts, performance rubrics, peer review questions

### Dance
- **Key concepts:** group choreography, transitions, expressive intent, rehearsal discipline, evaluation
- **Skills / outcomes:** link sections into a coherent group dance and evaluate performance strengths and gaps
- **Assessment angles:** transition scoring, rehearsal logs, self-evaluation prompts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A process project, a performance presentation and a controlled theory or reflection task.
- **SBA weighting:** CAPS balances practical work with written arts-literacy tasks; no single term percentage is fixed in the ATP.
