# Grade 9 — Creative Arts: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual Arts
- **Key concepts:** advanced drawing, shading, perspective, proportion, 2D and 3D planning
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply drawing skills more accurately and plan an artwork with stronger composition and depth
- **Assessment angles:** perspective drills, sketchbook evidence, studio rubrics

### Music
- **Key concepts:** improvisation, scales or tonal patterns, phrasing, ensemble accuracy, notation review
- **Skills / outcomes:** improvise within simple structures and improve technical control in performance
- **Assessment angles:** improvisation prompts, rhythm dictation, performance rubrics

### Drama
- **Key concepts:** devised drama, role, mood, conflict, stage space, physical theatre
- **Skills / outcomes:** create short devised scenes from social themes and use body and voice with more control
- **Assessment angles:** devised-scene rubrics, theme interpretation, terminology checks

### Dance
- **Key concepts:** technique, alignment, travelling steps, dynamics, safe conditioning
- **Skills / outcomes:** perform longer movement phrases with clearer alignment, control and expressive contrast
- **Assessment angles:** technique checklists, phrase memory, dynamic variation prompts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A diagnostic practical, an arts-literacy or theory check and a short performance or studio task.
- **SBA weighting:** CAPS balances practical work with written arts-literacy tasks; no single term percentage is fixed in the ATP.
