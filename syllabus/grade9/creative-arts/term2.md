# Grade 9 — Creative Arts: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual Arts
- **Key concepts:** design process, research, South African artists, print or craft techniques
- **Skills / outcomes:** investigate artists and produce a planned artwork informed by research and experimentation
- **Assessment angles:** research notes, visual-analysis items, practical project rubrics

### Music
- **Key concepts:** African and world music, listening analysis, harmony, texture, group work
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare styles, recognise instruments and apply listening insights in ensemble work
- **Assessment angles:** style comparison tables, listening questions, ensemble balance rubrics

### Drama
- **Key concepts:** South African theatre, script interpretation, voice, gesture, stage directions
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret scripts with stronger character choices and historical or social awareness
- **Assessment angles:** script-analysis prompts, staged-reading rubrics, drama history questions

### Dance
- **Key concepts:** social and cultural dance, choreographic intent, canon, repetition, formation
- **Skills / outcomes:** develop choreography that shows pattern, contrast and cultural understanding
- **Assessment angles:** choreography journals, formation diagrams, practical performance rubrics

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A practical artwork or choreography, a written arts-literacy response and an ensemble or drama activity.
- **SBA weighting:** CAPS balances practical work with written arts-literacy tasks; no single term percentage is fixed in the ATP.
