# Grade 9 — Social Sciences: History: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 21–30

## Topics

### Apartheid and resistance from 1948
- **Key concepts:** human rights, racism, apartheid laws, Sophiatown, Bantustans, Defiance Campaign, Freedom Charter, Women’s March, oral history
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, sequencing events, identifying cause and consequence, paragraph writing
- **Assessment angles:** timeline tasks, short source-based questions, paragraph responses, simple evidence-and-inference items
- **Historical period / geographical region:** South Africa, 1948–1960s

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Oral history and research project plus source-based classwork.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; tasks contribute to the annual Social Sciences programme.
