# Grade 9 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Journalism and media texts
- **Key concepts:** news reports, magazine articles, interviews, listening comprehension
- **Skills / outcomes:** read and discuss media texts and identify key information and stance
- **Assessment angles:** headline questions, fact and opinion items, main-point retrieval

### Formal correspondence
- **Key concepts:** formal letter, newspaper report, magazine article, register, audience
- **Skills / outcomes:** write with a more formal tone and clearer purpose for public communication
- **Assessment angles:** register choice, layout, audience-purpose matching

### Grammar for reporting
- **Key concepts:** direct and indirect speech, conjunctions, idioms, language editing
- **Skills / outcomes:** report speech and connect ideas more accurately in written Afrikaans
- **Assessment angles:** speech conversion, idiom hooks, conjunction items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** June control test plus a formal writing task and oral work.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 usually aligns closely with mid-year assessment and more formal media-related writing.
