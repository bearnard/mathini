# Grade 9 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Literature study and response
- **Key concepts:** prescribed texts, literary features, oral presentation, comprehension
- **Skills / outcomes:** engage more deeply with theme, character and message in prescribed texts
- **Assessment angles:** theme support, character evidence, short contextual questions

### Creative and personal writing
- **Key concepts:** narrative, descriptive, reflective essay, review, informal letter
- **Skills / outcomes:** write longer personal and imaginative pieces with improved control
- **Assessment angles:** essay planning, review conventions, opening and closing choices

### Sentence structure and voice
- **Key concepts:** sentence patterns, verb forms, active and passive voice, editing
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve grammatical accuracy in more complex sentence structures
- **Assessment angles:** voice transformation, verb-form items, editing tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal oral or writing task together with ongoing exam preparation.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 3 usually blends literature, extended writing and revision of advanced language structures.
