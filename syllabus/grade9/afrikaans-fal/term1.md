# Grade 9 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Advertising and visual persuasion
- **Key concepts:** advertisements, posters, comic strips, tone, tempo, emotive language
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret how visual and verbal elements are used to persuade in Afrikaans texts
- **Assessment angles:** tone and emotion hooks, layout questions, target-audience items

### Transactional writing set
- **Key concepts:** agenda, minutes, report, narrative, descriptive and reflective essay
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce practical and extended texts with suitable sequence and format
- **Assessment angles:** format conventions, sequencing, text-type recognition

### Language structures in context
- **Key concepts:** meervoude, adjectives, tenses, punctuation, poetry response, youth novel or drama
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply grammar while reading literature and composing responses
- **Assessment angles:** tense changes, adjective choice, punctuation and short contextual items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Informal tasks with a first-term control test and formal writing component.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Grade 9 Afrikaans FAL commonly starts with advertising, transactional formats and stronger literature integration.
