# Grade 9 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Synoptic revision
- **Key concepts:** literature themes, media texts, comprehension strategies, summary
- **Skills / outcomes:** review the full year’s work and respond strategically to exam tasks
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-genre revision items, summary technique, retrieval of evidence

### Exam writing practice
- **Key concepts:** final essays, transactional texts, planning, proofreading
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce accurate responses under timed conditions
- **Assessment angles:** format recall, editing traps, task-purpose matching

### Grammar consolidation
- **Key concepts:** review of all grammar and language use, punctuation, vocabulary
- **Skills / outcomes:** enter the FET phase with stronger language confidence
- **Assessment angles:** MCQ grammar, sentence correction, vocabulary in context

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination plus completion of remaining oral or writing evidence if needed.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is mainly revision and preparation for the transition into Grade 10 FET expectations.
