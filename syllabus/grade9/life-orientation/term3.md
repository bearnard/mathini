# Grade 9 — Life Orientation: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Democracy and human rights
- **Key concepts:** citizenship, the Constitution, rights, duties, participation
- **Skills / outcomes:** explain how democratic structures work and how citizens can participate responsibly
- **Assessment angles:** short-answer questions, structure-matching tasks, source analysis

### Diversity and social justice
- **Key concepts:** discrimination, stereotyping, gender equity, inclusion, nation-building
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse unfair treatment and suggest ways to build more inclusive school and community spaces
- **Assessment angles:** case studies, debate prompts, rights campaign tasks

### Conflict resolution and advocacy
- **Key concepts:** negotiation, mediation, respectful dialogue, leadership, activism
- **Skills / outcomes:** use negotiation strategies and plan responses to social problems that affect young people
- **Assessment angles:** dialogue-writing tasks, advocacy poster prompts, reflection questions

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** athletics, indigenous games, fitness maintenance, leadership
- **Skills / outcomes:** show leadership and consistency in physical activity while applying technique and rules
- **Assessment angles:** leadership observation, timed events, participation logs

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A democracy or rights investigation, a controlled test, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase Life Orientation uses 80% SBA and 20% PET across the year.
