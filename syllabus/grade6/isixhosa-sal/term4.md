# Grade 6 — isiXhosa Second Additional Language: Term 4

Source: CAPS isiXhosa Gr 4-6 (Home Language s3.2.1 pp. 22-23; First Additional Language s3.2.1 pp. 23-25), Grade 6 plans; phase label corrected to Intermediate Phase (Gr 4-6). Note: the Intermediate Phase has no isiXhosa 'Second Additional Language' in CAPS (audit), so the SAL label is unverified.

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** health, feelings, celebrations, revision dialogues, short presentations
- **Skills / outcomes:** give short prepared talks and respond to revision questions about familiar topics
- **Assessment angles:** oral presentation rubrics, listening quizzes, vocabulary recall

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** mixed comprehension texts, notices, stories, revision passages, simple summaries
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply reading strategies across familiar genres and identify key ideas for revision
- **Assessment angles:** comprehension questions, summary selection, matching exercises

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** personal paragraph, simple review, messages, revision tasks, picture writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce a short connected piece with clearer organisation and relevant vocabulary
- **Assessment angles:** guided writing rubrics, editing tasks, picture-description prompts

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** revision of pronouns, tenses, concords, punctuation, spelling
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate the year’s language structures for end-of-year assessment
- **Assessment angles:** MCQ-style grammar items, editing exercises, sentence correction

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Year-end examination and consolidation of oral and writing evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
