# Grade 6 — isiXhosa Second Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** daily routine, school day, food, shopping, directions, polite requests
- **Skills / outcomes:** take part in short conversations about routines, food choices and movement around the community
- **Assessment angles:** role-play prompts, sequencing tasks, listening-for-detail questions

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** simple stories, menus, advertisements, instructions, timetables
- **Skills / outcomes:** read familiar everyday texts and identify main ideas and useful information
- **Assessment angles:** menu questions, ad interpretation, instruction matching

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** diary lines, shopping list, short instructions, simple descriptions, messages
- **Skills / outcomes:** write short functional texts linked to school, home and shopping contexts
- **Assessment angles:** list-making tasks, guided diary writing, message format checks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** question forms, negatives, adjectives, possessives, present and past tense
- **Skills / outcomes:** expand sentence control and ask or answer simple questions accurately
- **Assessment angles:** question-building tasks, negative-form items, word-choice exercises

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal writing plus an oral task and a mid-year test or examination component.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
