# Grade 6 — isiXhosa Second Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** greetings, introductions, family vocabulary, classroom language, simple questions
- **Skills / outcomes:** greet confidently, introduce self and others, and respond to short oral prompts about family and school
- **Assessment angles:** dialogue completion, pronunciation checks, oral recall prompts

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** short dialogues, labels, notices, pictures, familiar vocabulary
- **Skills / outcomes:** read short texts aloud, match pictures to words and identify key details in simple texts
- **Assessment angles:** picture matching, true-or-false items, main-detail questions

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** simple sentences, personal details, family paragraph, captions, lists
- **Skills / outcomes:** write short sentences about self and family and present basic personal information clearly
- **Assessment angles:** sentence-order tasks, guided paragraph writing, fill-in forms

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** personal pronouns, subject concords, singular and plural, present tense, punctuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** use basic sentence patterns more accurately in speaking and writing
- **Assessment angles:** gap-fill grammar, editing items, punctuation checks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** An oral interaction, a guided writing task and routine comprehension or language checks.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
