# Grade 6 — isiXhosa Second Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** weather, transport, animals, culture, folktales, giving opinions
- **Skills / outcomes:** retell familiar stories, talk about seasons or transport and give simple supported opinions
- **Assessment angles:** retelling rubrics, opinion prompts, listening sequence tasks

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** folktales, posters, short factual texts, poems, visual cues
- **Skills / outcomes:** read simple literary and factual texts and identify who, where and what happened
- **Assessment angles:** genre recognition, retrieval questions, matching text to image

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** invitations, directions, short recounts, picture paragraphs, cultural themes
- **Skills / outcomes:** write a short recount or invitation and describe familiar events or places in connected sentences
- **Assessment angles:** format checks, sentence sequencing, guided recount writing

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** future tense, conjunctions, noun classes, connectors, question words
- **Skills / outcomes:** link ideas more clearly and vary sentence openings and tenses
- **Assessment angles:** connector tasks, tense change items, noun-class prompts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A formal oral or practical writing task, plus a controlled language or comprehension test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
