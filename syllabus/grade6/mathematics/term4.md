# Grade 6 — Mathematics: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Mass and Time
- **Key concepts:** grams and kilograms, analogue and digital scales, 12-hour and 24-hour time, calendars, stopwatches, time intervals and time zones
- **Skills / outcomes:** measure and compare mass, convert g and kg, read and write time in different formats, and calculate elapsed time and dates
- **Assessment angles:** 24-hour conversions, elapsed-time calculations, interpreting calendars and clocks, reading scale instruments
- **Approximate time:** About 15 hours

### Capacity, Volume and Integrated Problem Solving
- **Key concepts:** millilitres, litres and kilolitres, practical measuring tools, conversions, four operations in financial and measurement contexts, ratio and rate, fraction sharing
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve multi-step real-life problems, convert units confidently and select appropriate operations in context
- **Assessment angles:** capacity conversions, mixed-operation word problems, rate vs ratio wording, fraction sharing in context
- **Approximate time:** About 12 hours

### Data Handling
- **Key concepts:** tally tables, simple questionnaires, pictographs, bar graphs, double bar graphs, pie charts, mode, median, conclusions and predictions
- **Skills / outcomes:** collect, organise, represent and interpret data, compare categories and summarise findings in short written form
- **Assessment angles:** reading scales, choosing suitable graphs, mode or median questions, conclusion statements supported by data
- **Approximate time:** About 12 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal test on Terms 3 and 4 work, with foundational ideas from Terms 1 and 2 revisited.
- **SBA weighting:** School-based assessment only in this phase; term marks are built from formal tasks recorded by the school.
