# Grade 6 — Mathematics: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Length
- **Key concepts:** estimating and measuring, use of rulers and tape measures, mm-cm-m-km conversions, fractional and decimal forms of measures
- **Skills / outcomes:** choose sensible units, measure accurately, convert units and solve length problems in context
- **Assessment angles:** unit-conversion questions, perimeter from measured lengths, reading scales, decimal and fraction forms of measurement
- **Approximate time:** About 6 hours

### 2D Shapes, Angles, Symmetry and Transformations
- **Key concepts:** properties of polygons, angle types, lines of symmetry, tessellations, rotations, translations, reflections, enlargements and reductions
- **Skills / outcomes:** classify and compare shapes, recognise and name angles, draw lines of symmetry, describe transformations and build tessellated patterns
- **Assessment angles:** shape-property questions, angle recognition, symmetry counts, identifying transformations, congruent vs similar reasoning
- **Approximate time:** About 21 hours

### 3D Objects, Area, Perimeter and Volume
- **Key concepts:** prisms, cubes, pyramids, nets, faces, vertices and edges, perimeter, area on grids, volume of rectangular prisms by packing and formula reasoning
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify and describe solids, construct simple models, calculate perimeter and area, and reason about capacity or volume
- **Assessment angles:** net-to-solid matching, counting faces/edges/vertices, area-perimeter contrasts, grid-area tasks, volume of rectangular prisms
- **Approximate time:** About 15 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project covering a combination of Term 1–3 topics and a formal Term 3 test.
- **SBA weighting:** School-based assessment only in this phase; term marks are built from formal tasks recorded by the school.
