# Grade 6 — Mathematics: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Number Sentences and Patterns
- **Key concepts:** number sentences, trial and improvement, numeric patterns, geometric patterns, input-output values, flow diagrams, tables, equivalent rule descriptions
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete and create patterns, determine rules, move between verbal, tabular and symbolic forms, and solve simple missing-value problems
- **Assessment angles:** missing input/output values, rule-writing, extending patterns, flow-diagram reversals, geometric pattern growth
- **Approximate time:** About 3–4 weeks

### Common Fractions
- **Key concepts:** comparing and ordering fractions, equivalent fractions, mixed numbers, addition and subtraction where one denominator is a multiple of another, fractions of whole numbers, percentages of whole numbers
- **Skills / outcomes:** simplify and compare fractions, convert mixed/improper forms when needed, calculate with fractions, and solve sharing or percentage contexts
- **Assessment angles:** fraction ordering, denominator errors, mixed-number regrouping, fraction of a quantity, percentage linked to fraction form
- **Approximate time:** About 4 weeks

### Decimal Fractions
- **Key concepts:** tenths and hundredths, place value to two decimal places, comparing and ordering decimals, addition and subtraction of decimals, multiplying by 10 and 100, fraction-decimal-percentage equivalence
- **Skills / outcomes:** align decimal points correctly, convert between representations, and solve money or measurement problems using decimals
- **Assessment angles:** decimal place-value traps, common fraction to decimal conversion, ordering decimals, context questions with money and measures
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Investigation on one Term 2 topic plus a test covering Terms 1 and 2 content.
- **SBA weighting:** School-based assessment only in this phase; term marks are built from formal tasks recorded by the school.
