# Grade 6 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 2

Source: CAPS Afrikaans Eerste Addisionele Taal Intermediere Fase Graad 4-6, Graad 6 Kwartaal 1-4 teaching plans; phase label corrected to Intermediate Phase (Gr 4-6).

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Oral presentations and sequencing
- **Key concepts:** events in order, oral presentation, retelling, instructions
- **Skills / outcomes:** speak more confidently about familiar topics and organise ideas in sequence
- **Assessment angles:** sequencing cues, short oral prompts, key-detail recall

### Reading stories and everyday texts
- **Key concepts:** fables, news items, short comprehension texts, main idea
- **Skills / outcomes:** read short factual and fictional texts and identify important information
- **Assessment angles:** main-idea questions, fact recall, vocabulary in context

### Diary and informal writing
- **Key concepts:** diary entry, descriptions, informal letters, adjectives, pronouns, negative form
- **Skills / outcomes:** write short personal texts with more detail and improving grammar control
- **Assessment angles:** format questions, adjective and pronoun choice, negative-form items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal writing plus an oral task and a mid-year test or examination component.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 usually extends writing beyond single paragraphs and uses more everyday functional texts.
