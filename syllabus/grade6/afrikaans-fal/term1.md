# Grade 6 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 1

Source: CAPS Afrikaans Eerste Addisionele Taal Intermediere Fase Graad 4-6, Graad 6 Kwartaal 1-4 teaching plans; phase label corrected to Intermediate Phase (Gr 4-6).

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Stories, poems and instructions
- **Key concepts:** luister en praat, stories, gedigte, instruksies, mondelinge begrip
- **Skills / outcomes:** listen to and retell short Afrikaans texts, follow instructions, and answer simple oral questions
- **Assessment angles:** sequencing, key-word recall, matching instruction to action

### Early writing forms
- **Key concepts:** paragraphs, friendly letters, dialogue, sentence building, audience
- **Skills / outcomes:** write short connected pieces with clear topic sentences and simple format control
- **Assessment angles:** format recognition, sentence ordering, suitable openings and closings

### Core grammar and punctuation
- **Key concepts:** nouns, verbs, simple sentences, punctuation, spelling, word order
- **Skills / outcomes:** use basic Afrikaans grammar accurately in short spoken and written tasks
- **Assessment angles:** editing items, punctuation, simple tense and word-order questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** An oral activity, foundational writing task and routine comprehension or language checks.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Intermediate Phase Afrikaans FAL integrates Listening & Speaking, Reading & Viewing, Writing & Presenting, and Language Structures. Vocabulary growth and oral confidence are central.
