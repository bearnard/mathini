# Grade 6 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Role play and opinion giving
- **Key concepts:** role play, opinions, retelling, interaction, listening
- **Skills / outcomes:** respond orally to familiar scenarios and give short supported opinions
- **Assessment angles:** dialogue choices, opinion prompts, listening-for-detail items

### Fiction and nonfiction reading
- **Key concepts:** stories, information texts, comprehension, comparison
- **Skills / outcomes:** distinguish between fictional and factual texts and answer basic comprehension questions
- **Assessment angles:** genre recognition, retrieval questions, comparison prompts

### Functional writing and grammar growth
- **Key concepts:** invitation, directions, simple report, prepositions, conjunctions, verb tenses, questions
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce simple functional texts and use a wider range of sentence patterns
- **Assessment angles:** format features, conjunction and preposition items, question formation

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A formal oral or practical writing task, plus a controlled language or comprehension test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 3 often mixes oral interaction with more functional writing such as invitations and directions.
