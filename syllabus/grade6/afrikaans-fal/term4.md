# Grade 6 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Summaries and oral feedback
- **Key concepts:** summary, feedback, discussion, comparison of texts
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify key ideas and report them briefly in Afrikaans
- **Assessment angles:** main-point selection, summary order, oral-recall prompts

### Poetry and review writing
- **Key concepts:** poetry response, review, creative paragraph, text comparison
- **Skills / outcomes:** respond to short literary texts and write simple evaluative comments
- **Assessment angles:** poetry mood questions, review language, comparing texts

### Revision of language structures
- **Key concepts:** nouns, verbs, adjectives, punctuation, tenses, sentence patterns
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise the year’s main language structures for end-of-year assessment
- **Assessment angles:** editing tasks, MCQ-style grammar, sentence correction

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Year-end examination and consolidation of oral and writing evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is mainly for revision, confidence-building and preparation for Grade 7.
