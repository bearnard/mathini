# Grade 6 — English Home Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Deeper literature response
- **Key concepts:** theme, mood, character motivation, review, interview, book response
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse motives and themes more closely and justify opinions with textual support
- **Assessment angles:** review-style questions, theme statements, character evidence, sequencing

### Extended writing and speaking
- **Key concepts:** book reviews, narrative essays, interviews, oral response, audience awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** present information orally and in writing with stronger organisation and detail
- **Assessment angles:** oral presentation hooks, audience-purpose matching, opening and closing conventions

### Complex sentence work
- **Key concepts:** phrases, clauses, compound sentences, complex sentences, conjunctions
- **Skills / outcomes:** combine ideas effectively and improve sentence variety in written work
- **Assessment angles:** join and split sentences, clause identification, error correction

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A formal oral or project-style response, a writing task such as a review or essay, and a controlled test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
The ATP usually increases independence in reading response and expects learners to apply grammar through authentic writing tasks.
