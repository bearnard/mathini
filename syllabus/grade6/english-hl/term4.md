# Grade 6 — English Home Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Integrated revision across genres
- **Key concepts:** poetry, fiction, media texts, comprehension, summary, oral recall
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise strategies for reading, viewing and responding across the year’s genres
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-genre revision questions, summary skills, retrieval of evidence

### Exam-oriented writing practice
- **Key concepts:** essay planning, transactional texts, editing checklist, time management
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce complete pieces under time limits and edit for clarity and accuracy
- **Assessment angles:** planning under pressure, format recall, common grammar traps

### Language consolidation
- **Key concepts:** parts of speech, tense, direct and indirect speech, punctuation, spelling
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply the year’s language structures accurately in context
- **Assessment angles:** error spotting, sentence correction, multiple-choice grammar items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Year-end examination, consolidation of oral evidence where needed, and shorter revision-based writing tasks.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is mainly consolidation and preparation for the final assessment programme.
