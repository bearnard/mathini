# Grade 6 — English Home Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Literary genres in rotation
- **Key concepts:** novel or novelette, play, poetry, theme, character, viewpoint
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare literary texts and respond orally to poems and scenes
- **Assessment angles:** theme questions, speaker matching, figurative language, short contextual responses

### Creative and transactional writing
- **Key concepts:** poems, dialogues, play scripts, messages, drafting and redrafting
- **Skills / outcomes:** write for different audiences and purposes using the full writing process
- **Assessment angles:** text-type conventions, register, planning notes, paragraph coherence

### Language extension
- **Key concepts:** vocabulary building, idioms, synonyms, antonyms, tense control, punctuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** expand vocabulary and apply grammar accurately while editing own work
- **Assessment angles:** language-in-context items, word choice, punctuation and spelling checks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Completion of the ongoing oral task, a writing task, and a mid-year test or examination component.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 broadens the range of texts and expects more comparison across genres while literature and language work remain integrated.
