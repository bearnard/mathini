# Grade 6 — English Home Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Current-issues media texts
- **Key concepts:** newspaper articles, radio reports, fact and opinion, inference, evidence
- **Skills / outcomes:** listen critically, identify main ideas, and discuss current issues with reasons
- **Assessment angles:** comprehension questions, bias/inference items, viewpoint and evidence matching

### Fiction and visual literacy
- **Key concepts:** speculative fiction, advertisements, skimming, scanning, visualising
- **Skills / outcomes:** read literary and visual texts and explain message, setting and plot
- **Assessment angles:** advert purpose, target audience, plot/theme questions, vocabulary in context

### Process writing and core grammar
- **Key concepts:** summaries, narrative and descriptive essays, friendly letters, dialogue, nouns, pronouns, conjunctions, adverbs
- **Skills / outcomes:** plan, draft, revise and edit writing with appropriate paragraphing and grammar
- **Assessment angles:** editing items, punctuation, sentence transformation, text-type conventions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Oral reading aloud begins; an extended writing task and routine comprehension/language activities.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Intermediate Phase English HL integrates Listening & Speaking, Reading & Viewing, Writing & Presenting, and Language Structures. ATPs emphasise daily reading and vocabulary growth.
