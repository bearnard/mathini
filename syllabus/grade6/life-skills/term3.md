# Grade 6 — Life Skills: Term 3

Source: CAPS Life Skills Grades 4-6 (PSW pp. 23-26, PE pp. 30-32, Creative Arts pp. 41-50).

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Personal and Social Well-being
- **Key concepts:** caring for animals; caring for people (needs, views, kindness); nation-building, cultural heritage, national symbols and days; gender stereotyping, sexism and abuse
- **Skills / outcomes:** Work through the CAPS topics listed for this term.
- **Assessment angles:** scenario questions, project (formal task)

### Physical Education
- **Key concepts:** rhythmic patterns of movement (aerobics, galloping, skipping and similar)
- **Skills / outcomes:** Work through the CAPS topics listed for this term.
- **Assessment angles:** movement observation checklists

### Creative Arts
- **Key concepts:** music: conflict dialogues with movement and music, treble clef notation, 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4 time, South African songs from two traditions; visual arts: 2D images of people and objects (portraits, shells, shoes), 3D clay modelling
- **Skills / outcomes:** Work through the CAPS topics listed for this term.
- **Assessment angles:** practical performance and artwork rubrics

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PSW, PE and Creative Arts tasks as set out in the CAPS programme of assessment (PSW: formal test Term 2, project Term 3, examination Term 4).
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage here; see the CAPS Life Skills programme of assessment (Grades 4-6).
