# Grade 6 — Life Skills: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Personal and Social Well-being
- **Key concepts:** diversity, culture, religion, rights and responsibilities, substance abuse awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare different identities respectfully and explain why harmful substances and stereotypes damage communities
- **Assessment angles:** discussion prompts on diversity, matching rights to responsibilities, anti-substance-abuse campaigns

### Physical Education
- **Key concepts:** athletics, relays, indigenous games, flexibility, reaction time
- **Skills / outcomes:** take part in running, jumping and indigenous games while showing safe technique and cooperation
- **Assessment angles:** relay timing, movement-sequence scoring, indigenous-game demonstrations

### Creative Arts
- **Key concepts:** South African cultural art, mixed media, choral verse, singing, movement
- **Skills / outcomes:** create culturally informed artworks and rehearse a short integrated music, drama or dance piece
- **Assessment angles:** cultural-theme rubrics, oral reflection prompts, ensemble performance tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** An integrated project or oral, a PE movement assessment and a Creative Arts performance or artwork.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; PSW, PE and Creative Arts tasks feed the annual assessment programme.
