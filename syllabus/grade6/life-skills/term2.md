# Grade 6 — Life Skills: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 6–7)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Personal and Social Well-being
- **Key concepts:** friendships, conflict resolution, personal safety, responsibilities, study habits
- **Skills / outcomes:** discuss relationship problems, solve conflicts fairly and identify trusted adults and safe choices
- **Assessment angles:** case studies on conflict, role-play prompts, safety poster or checklist tasks

### Physical Education
- **Key concepts:** invasion games, catching and passing, agility, teamwork, fair play
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply basic sport skills in small-sided games and improve fitness through repeated drills
- **Assessment angles:** peer-assessed passing drills, mini-game tactics, fair-play observations

### Creative Arts
- **Key concepts:** texture, pattern, printmaking, storytelling, puppetry, rhythm patterns
- **Skills / outcomes:** experiment with print or collage techniques and present short stories through voice, movement or puppets
- **Assessment angles:** process journals, practical artwork checks, storytelling performance rubrics

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A formal PSW task, a PE skills assessment and a Creative Arts practical or presentation.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; PSW, PE and Creative Arts tasks feed the annual assessment programme.
