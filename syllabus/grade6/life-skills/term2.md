# Grade 6 — Life Skills: Term 2

Source: CAPS Life Skills Grades 4-6 (PSW pp. 23-26, PE pp. 30-32, Creative Arts pp. 41-50).

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Personal and Social Well-being
- **Key concepts:** self management: responsibilities and an activity plan; bullying (reasons, where to find help); cultural rites of passage (birth, baptism, wedding, death); dignity of the person in South African religions
- **Skills / outcomes:** Work through the CAPS topics listed for this term.
- **Assessment angles:** scenario questions, short reflections; formal test on Terms 1 and 2 content

### Physical Education
- **Key concepts:** physical fitness programme (agility, power, speed, flexibility, endurance, circuits)
- **Skills / outcomes:** Work through the CAPS topics listed for this term.
- **Assessment angles:** fitness programme records, observation checklists

### Creative Arts
- **Key concepts:** dance: elements of dance, binary and ternary form, cultural dance with drum, appreciate two South African dance types; visual arts: lettering or radiating pattern (2D), relief mandala (3D)
- **Skills / outcomes:** Work through the CAPS topics listed for this term.
- **Assessment angles:** practical performance and artwork rubrics

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PSW, PE and Creative Arts tasks as set out in the CAPS programme of assessment (PSW: formal test Term 2, project Term 3, examination Term 4).
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage here; see the CAPS Life Skills programme of assessment (Grades 4-6).
