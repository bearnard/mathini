Source: CAPS Social Sciences Grades 4-6 (History, Grade 6 Terms 1-4, pp. 42-45); audit /audit/caps-alignment.md (Grade 6 History).

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)

## Topics

### Medicine through time
- **Key concepts:** indigenous healing in South Africa (holistic healing, herbs, training of healers), modern Western discoveries (Jenner, Pasteur, Koch, Fleming, first heart transplant), link between holistic and Western healing today
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, sequencing events, identifying cause and consequence, paragraph writing
- **Assessment angles:** timeline tasks, short source-based questions, paragraph responses, simple evidence-and-inference items
- **Historical period / geographical region:** South Africa, Europe and the modern world

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Controlled test or year-end summative task on the term content.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; tasks contribute to the annual Social Sciences programme.
