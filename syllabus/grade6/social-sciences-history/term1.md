# Grade 6 — Social Sciences: History: Term 1

Source: CAPS Social Sciences Grades 4-6 (History, Grade 6 Terms 1-4, pp. 42-45); audit /audit/caps-alignment.md (Grade 6 History).

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### An African kingdom long ago in southern Africa: Mapungubwe
- **Key concepts:** changes in societies in the Limpopo Valley 900–1300 AD; K2 and Schroda; Mapungubwe as the first state 1220–1300 (king, stone palace, Mapungubwe Hill, first town, social classes, golden rhinoceros, Indian Ocean trade, World Heritage Site); change and continuity in East Coast trade with inland settlements; Great Zimbabwe
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, sequencing events, identifying cause and consequence, paragraph writing
- **Assessment angles:** timeline tasks, short source-based questions, paragraph responses, simple evidence-and-inference items
- **Historical period / geographical region:** Limpopo Valley and southern Africa, 900–1300 AD

### A European explorer in Asia: Marco Polo
- **Key concepts:** Marco Polo and his influence on later European exploration
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, sequencing events, identifying cause and consequence, paragraph writing
- **Assessment angles:** timeline tasks, short source-based questions, paragraph responses, simple evidence-and-inference items
- **Historical period / geographical region:** Europe and Asia, 13th century
