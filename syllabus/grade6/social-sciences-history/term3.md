## Topics

### How people govern themselves: democracy and citizenship
- **Key concepts:** first democratic government 1994, parties and voting, the Constitution, Parliament, rules and laws, the justice system; rights and responsibilities of citizens, Constitutional Court, case studies (Fatima Meer, Pius Langa); children's rights and the Children's Charter of South Africa; national symbols since 1994 (Coat of Arms, flag, anthem); research project: biography of a South African who built democracy
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, sequencing events, identifying cause and consequence, paragraph writing
- **Assessment angles:** timeline tasks, short source-based questions, paragraph responses, simple evidence-and-inference items
- **Historical period / geographical region:** South Africa, 1994 to present

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Source-based task or controlled test on the term theme.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; tasks contribute to the annual Social Sciences programme.
