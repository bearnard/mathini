## Topics

### Explorers from Europe find southern Africa
- **Key concepts:** reasons for European exploration (the Renaissance, new ideas and inventions such as gunpowder, compass and caravel, spreading Christianity, trade and profit); European trade route to the East via southern Africa: Dias and the Khoikhoi at Mossel Bay 1488, Da Gama, VOC journeys, life of a sailor on a VOC ship
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, sequencing events, identifying cause and consequence, paragraph writing
- **Assessment angles:** timeline tasks, short source-based questions, paragraph responses, simple evidence-and-inference items
- **Historical period / geographical region:** Europe and southern Africa, 15th–17th centuries

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Source-based task or controlled test on the term theme.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; tasks contribute to the annual Social Sciences programme.
