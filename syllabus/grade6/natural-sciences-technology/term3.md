# Grade 6 — Natural Sciences & Technology: Term 3

Source: CAPS Natural Sciences & Technology Intermediate Phase Gr 4-6, Grade 6 Terms 1-4 teaching plans (pp. 47-64); phase label corrected to Intermediate Phase (Gr 4-6). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Electric circuits and components
- **Key concepts:** cells, wires, switches, bulbs, conductors, insulators, open circuit, closed circuit, series circuit
- **Skills / outcomes:** Assemble a simple circuit, predict whether a circuit will work, and troubleshoot broken connections.
- **Assessment angles:** Circuit-symbol diagrams, component matching, and reasons why a bulb does or does not light.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Electrical safety and systems for control
- **Key concepts:** mains electricity, safety rules, safe handling, simple control systems, useful vs dangerous electricity
- **Skills / outcomes:** Relate classroom circuits to everyday electrical use, identify safe and unsafe practices, and explain control in simple systems.
- **Assessment angles:** Safety scenarios, circuit-use examples, and short explanation questions about conductors and insulators.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change, Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical circuit task and term test.
- **SBA weighting:** School-based assessment relies on practical work, observation, and written testing.
