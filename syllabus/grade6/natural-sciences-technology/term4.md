# Grade 6 — Natural Sciences & Technology: Term 4

Source: CAPS Natural Sciences & Technology Intermediate Phase Gr 4-6, Grade 6 Terms 1-4 teaching plans (pp. 47-64); phase label corrected to Intermediate Phase (Gr 4-6). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Solar system and planetary movement
- **Key concepts:** Sun, planets, order of planets, orbit, rotation, revolution, gravity, day and night
- **Skills / outcomes:** Model the solar system, compare planetary features, and explain day/night and the year.
- **Assessment angles:** Ordering planets, labeling solar-system diagrams, and simple orbit or rotation explanations.
- **Knowledge strand:** Planet Earth & Beyond

### Observing Earth from space
- **Key concepts:** Moon phases, telescopes, Earth-Sun-Moon relationship, space observation, astronomy tools
- **Skills / outcomes:** Use models to explain phases, interpret basic observations, and connect technology to astronomy.
- **Assessment angles:** Moon-phase diagrams, matching instruments to their uses, and short explanatory items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Planet Earth & Beyond

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project or model task and end-of-term test.
- **SBA weighting:** Continuous assessment with a strong emphasis on diagrams, projects, and concept recall.
