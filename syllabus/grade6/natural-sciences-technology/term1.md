# Grade 6 — Natural Sciences & Technology: Term 1

Source: CAPS Natural Sciences & Technology Intermediate Phase Gr 4-6, Grade 6 Terms 1-4 teaching plans (pp. 47-64); phase label corrected to Intermediate Phase (Gr 4-6). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Photosynthesis and plant food
- **Key concepts:** chlorophyll, sunlight, water, carbon dioxide, glucose, oxygen, leaves as food factories
- **Skills / outcomes:** Explain the inputs and outputs of photosynthesis, interpret simple classroom investigations, and connect plant food to ecosystem survival.
- **Assessment angles:** Label a leaf or plant diagram, compare plants kept in light and dark, and explain why green plants are producers.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Nutrients, food chains and ecosystems
- **Key concepts:** balanced diet, food groups, producers, consumers, decomposers, food chains, food webs, habitats
- **Skills / outcomes:** Classify organisms by feeding role, build food chains and food webs, and explain interdependence in an ecosystem.
- **Assessment angles:** Food-web diagrams, trophic-level questions, and “what happens if…” ecosystem disturbance items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical investigation and class test.
- **SBA weighting:** Continuous assessment; practical tasks and tests dominate the term mark.
