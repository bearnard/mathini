# Grade 6 — Natural Sciences & Technology: Term 2

Source: CAPS Natural Sciences & Technology Intermediate Phase Gr 4-6, Grade 6 Terms 1-4 teaching plans (pp. 47-64); phase label corrected to Intermediate Phase (Gr 4-6). Topic content not re-audited here.

**CAPS phase:** Intermediate Phase (Gr 4–6)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### States of matter, mixtures and solutions
- **Key concepts:** solids, liquids, gases, particles, mixtures, solutions, solute, solvent, dissolving
- **Skills / outcomes:** Differentiate states of matter, distinguish mixtures from solutions, and record observations using correct scientific vocabulary.
- **Assessment angles:** Particle-model sketches, sorting examples into mixtures or solutions, and short explanations of dissolving.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

### Separating mixtures and water purification
- **Key concepts:** filtration, sieving, settling, decanting, evaporation, fair tests, rates of dissolving, clean water
- **Skills / outcomes:** Choose suitable separation methods, plan a fair test, and design a simple filtration or purification system.
- **Assessment angles:** Apparatus diagrams, investigation variables, and real-world water-pollution or purification questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical investigation and mid-term test.
- **SBA weighting:** Continuous assessment with investigations, written tasks, and a controlled test.
