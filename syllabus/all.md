# **What to study for your Grade 5 November Assessments**

## **English**

### **Section A**

**1.1 Reading Comprehension (25 marks)**

* You will receive a text and questions.  
* Read the text carefully and answer the questions:  
  * Contextual questions (understanding)  
  * Opinion  
  * Vocabulary  
* Remember to answer in **full sentences**.  
* Incorrect Grammar, Punctuation, and Spelling will be marked down by ½ a mark.

**1.2 Visual Comprehension (10 marks)**

* Answer questions based on an image.

**1.3 Summary (10 marks)**

* Skim and Scan the text for five important facts.  
* Include a topic sentence and a conclusion.

**1.4 Listening Skill (10 marks)**

* Listen carefully to the text which your teacher will read to you and answer the questions based on it.

**Study Tip:** Make a schedule. It's helpful to getting things done properly and in a timely manner.

### **2\) Language (30 marks)**

*(Spelling is important)*

* **Identification and function in the text:**  
  * Common nouns, pronouns, proper nouns  
  * Relative clauses and relative pronouns  
  * Adjectives of comparison (e.g., easy, easier, easiest) and adjectival phrases  
  * Verbs and Adverbs (How? When? Where?)  
  * Adverbial phrases \- Time, Place, Manner, and Degree  
  * Articles: Definite and Indefinite with a noun  
  * Prepositions  
  * Direct speech \- correct punctuation and Capital Letters  
  * Reported speech \- Five rules\!\!\!

### **Section B \- Transactional Text (10 marks)**

1. Plan using a mind map \- ideas, keywords.  
2. Draft copy and edit.  
3. Final neat copy.

**You will be given three choices:**

* **A Dialogue:** "Life in the Animal Kingdom" \- A conversation between Lion and Elephant. Which one will be the better King?  
* **A Book Review:** About a book that you have read recently. Include information about author, publisher, characters, plot, and your opinion.  
* **Design a Brochure/Flyer:** Provides information about *Chimp Eden*. Use the following headings:  
  * The name of the centre  
  * The location  
  * The purpose/aims  
  * Three interesting facts about the place  
  * Reasons to visit  
  * *(Additional information will be provided about Chimp Eden)*
