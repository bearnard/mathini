# Grade 10 — English Home Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Poetry and short stories
- **Key concepts:** imagery, diction, tone, comparison, narrative voice, symbolism
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse shorter prescribed texts with more independence
- **Assessment angles:** poetry device items, unseen-style questions, short-story technique prompts

### Imaginative and transactional writing
- **Key concepts:** reflective essay, imaginative writing, report, interview, statement, curriculum vitae
- **Skills / outcomes:** shift between creative and practical writing forms with stronger control of format
- **Assessment angles:** format recognition, audience and register, editing for clarity

### Oral communication and language practice
- **Key concepts:** prepared speech, listening, language revision, editing
- **Skills / outcomes:** deliver and respond to oral tasks while maintaining language accuracy
- **Assessment angles:** speech hooks, listening details, language correction items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Oral task, formal writing task, and a controlled test as preparation for end-of-year demands.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 3 generally broadens the literature set and adds more independent oral and writing performance.
