# Grade 10 — English Home Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Introduction to FET literature study
- **Key concepts:** novel, drama, short story, poetry, plot, setting, theme, literary terms
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify major literary genres and begin sustained study of prescribed texts
- **Assessment angles:** genre identification, literary-term questions, contextual literature prompts

### Essay and shorter transactional writing
- **Key concepts:** narrative essay, descriptive essay, reflective writing, friendly letter, article, review
- **Skills / outcomes:** use the FET writing process to produce longer and shorter texts for specific purposes
- **Assessment angles:** essay-type selection, format features, paragraph development

### Language in context
- **Key concepts:** comprehension, summary, grammar revision, editing, oral baseline
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply reading and editing skills expected for Paper 1 and classroom tasks
- **Assessment angles:** summary rules, comprehension inference, language-editing items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Baseline diagnostic work, a transactional writing task, an oral activity and a language or comprehension test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
FET English HL is organised around Paper 1 (language in context), Paper 2 (literature) and Paper 3 (writing). Prescribed texts vary by DBE cycle and school.
