# Grade 10 — English Home Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Novel or drama in depth
- **Key concepts:** character development, conflict, theme, dramatic technique, evidence
- **Skills / outcomes:** build longer evidence-based responses to prescribed literature
- **Assessment angles:** quotation use, theme development, contextual literature questions

### Argument and formal communication
- **Key concepts:** discursive essay, argumentative essay, formal letter, speech, newspaper article
- **Skills / outcomes:** write more formally and sustain an argument with logical support
- **Assessment angles:** register, tone, thesis statements, purpose and audience questions

### Mid-year Paper 1 preparation
- **Key concepts:** summary, visual literacy, language structures, editing, comprehension
- **Skills / outcomes:** move between comprehension, summary and language tasks more fluently
- **Assessment angles:** summary marking hooks, visual text items, grammar in context

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal writing, oral work, and the mid-year examination covering language, literature and writing.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 usually deepens one major literary text and prepares learners for the first full examination cycle.
