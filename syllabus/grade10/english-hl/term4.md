# Grade 10 — English Home Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Revision of Papers 1–3
- **Key concepts:** comprehension, summary, literature revision, essay planning, transactional recall
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate paper structure and sharpen exam techniques
- **Assessment angles:** paper-format questions, summary technique, literature revision items

### Analytical and exam writing
- **Key concepts:** essay planning, editing, extended responses, timed writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete polished responses within examination time limits
- **Assessment angles:** time management, planning notes, common error traps

### Full-year literature consolidation
- **Key concepts:** novel, drama, poetry, short stories, quotation revision
- **Skills / outcomes:** synthesise work from the year into exam-ready literature responses
- **Assessment angles:** integrated literature questions, theme tracking, contextual recall

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination with paper-style tasks across language, literature and writing, plus any remaining oral evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is revision-heavy and mirrors the paper structure used throughout the FET phase.
