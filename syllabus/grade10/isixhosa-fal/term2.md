# Grade 10 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** interviews, oral presentations, summarising spoken texts, register awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** listen for bias or key information and respond with more formal oral language when needed
- **Assessment angles:** presentation rubrics, listening notes, register-matching tasks

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** novel or short stories, media texts, character, theme, bias and purpose
- **Skills / outcomes:** read longer prose and media texts while discussing theme, conflict and audience effect
- **Assessment angles:** character evidence, bias questions, theme prompts

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** email, review, report, dialogue, transactional writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** write transactional texts with clearer structure, tone and audience awareness
- **Assessment angles:** email layout, review language, report organisation

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** direct and indirect speech, concord, pronouns, conjunctions, editing
- **Skills / outcomes:** report information accurately and improve coherence across connected paragraphs
- **Assessment angles:** speech conversion, conjunction choices, pronoun and concord tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Controlled test, oral presentation and a formal writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
