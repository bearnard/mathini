# Grade 10 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** prepared reading, discussion, speech, listening comprehension, oral baseline
- **Skills / outcomes:** build confidence with FET oral routines and respond accurately to spoken texts and discussion prompts
- **Assessment angles:** oral rubrics, listening recall, discussion questions

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** introduction to literature genres, visual literacy, comprehension, summary, prescribed texts
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify major literary genres and begin sustained study of prescribed texts and media material
- **Assessment angles:** genre identification, summary hooks, contextual questions

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** narrative essay, descriptive essay, friendly or formal letter, process writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce longer and shorter texts for specific purposes using the full writing process
- **Assessment angles:** essay-type selection, format features, paragraph development

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** grammar review, editing, vocabulary growth, sentence construction, punctuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise foundational grammar while applying it to realistic texts and Paper 1-style activities
- **Assessment angles:** editing items, tense shifts, word-order questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Baseline or diagnostic work, a writing task, oral activity and a language or comprehension test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
