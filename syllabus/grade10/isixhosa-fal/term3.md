# Grade 10 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** prepared speech, debate, role play, discussion of social issues
- **Skills / outcomes:** present arguments and support opinions more clearly in formal oral tasks
- **Assessment angles:** speech rubrics, debate prompts, oral response checklists

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** drama, poetry, short stories, message, tone, contextual reading
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret literary texts more analytically and support responses with evidence
- **Assessment angles:** tone questions, quotation prompts, contextual response items

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** discursive essay, formal letter, article, project writing, presentation
- **Skills / outcomes:** switch between creative, reflective and formal writing while completing a sustained project
- **Assessment angles:** project planning, format recall, audience-purpose matching

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** complex sentences, punctuation, editing, vocabulary refinement, style
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine sentence variety and edit work more accurately in context
- **Assessment angles:** sentence-combining tasks, proofreading, vocabulary items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project submission and oral component, plus a formal language or writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
