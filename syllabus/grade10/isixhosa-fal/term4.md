# Grade 10 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** revision of oral tasks, listening comprehension, reading fluency, exam practice
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply the year’s speaking and listening strategies in revision and exam-style contexts
- **Assessment angles:** oral recall, listening quizzes, fluency checks

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** revision across literature, comprehension, visual literacy, summary, prescribed texts
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate Paper 1 and Paper 2 reading demands for final assessment
- **Assessment angles:** summary questions, literature revision prompts, visual-text items

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** exam essays, transactional recall, summary writing, proofreading
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete writing tasks accurately under timed conditions and revise key formats
- **Assessment angles:** planning under pressure, format recall, proofreading tasks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** revision of grammar, punctuation, sentence control, editing
- **Skills / outcomes:** correct common language errors and prepare for the next FET grade
- **Assessment angles:** grammar MCQs, sentence correction, editing exercises

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of any remaining oral evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
