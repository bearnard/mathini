# Grade 10 — Mathematics: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Analytical Geometry
- **Key concepts:** distance, gradient and midpoint formulae on the Cartesian plane
- **Skills / outcomes:** derive and apply formulas to coordinates, identify parallel or perpendicular lines and solve coordinate problems
- **Assessment angles:** midpoint vs distance confusion, gradient sign errors, coordinate proofs
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Finance and Growth
- **Key concepts:** simple and compound growth, interest, hire purchase, inflation, population growth and foreign exchange contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** choose appropriate growth formulae, substitute correctly and interpret answers in real-life financial settings
- **Assessment angles:** simple vs compound growth, interest contexts, exchange-rate interpretation, contextual formula choice
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Statistics
- **Key concepts:** grouped data, quartiles, percentiles, five-number summary and box-and-whisker plots
- **Skills / outcomes:** summarise data, identify central tendency and spread, and comment meaningfully on distributions
- **Assessment angles:** median interval, quartile reading, box-plot interpretation, grouped-data mean estimate
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Euclidean Geometry, Trigonometry and Measurement
- **Key concepts:** geometry riders, two-dimensional trigonometric problems, surface area and volume of spheres, cones and pyramids
- **Skills / outcomes:** integrate geometry and measurement ideas to solve longer multi-step problems
- **Assessment angles:** composite solids, geometry-proof structure, trig in context, choosing correct measurements
- **Approximate time:** About 4 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Two formal tests covering Term 3 topics in proportion to teaching time.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
