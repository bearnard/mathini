# Grade 10 — Mathematics: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Probability
- **Key concepts:** probability models, Venn diagrams, unions, intersections, mutually exclusive and complementary events
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate probabilities symbolically and from contextual information and record event relationships clearly
- **Assessment angles:** union vs intersection, Venn-diagram setup, complementary events, relative frequency vs theory
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Revision and Examination Technique
- **Key concepts:** paper structure, topic integration, method communication and exam pacing across Papers 1 and 2
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise strategically, diagnose weak areas and practise full-paper responses under time limits
- **Assessment angles:** paper-topic weighting, common exam traps, multi-topic integration and checking routines
- **Approximate time:** About 7 weeks including exams

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final formal test plus the end-of-year examination (Paper 1 and Paper 2).
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
