# Grade 10 — Mathematics: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Algebraic Expressions
- **Key concepts:** factorisation including grouping and cubes, algebraic fractions, rational and irrational numbers, rounding surds
- **Skills / outcomes:** simplify expressions accurately, work with algebraic fractions and justify factorisation choices
- **Assessment angles:** factorisation patterns, simplifying algebraic fractions, rational vs irrational classification, surd estimates
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Exponents and Number Patterns
- **Key concepts:** laws of exponents with integer and rational exponents, linear number patterns and general terms
- **Skills / outcomes:** simplify exponential forms, solve simple exponent equations and describe pattern rules
- **Assessment angles:** negative or fractional exponents, nth-term reasoning, equivalent exponential forms
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Equations and Inequalities
- **Key concepts:** linear, quadratic and simultaneous equations, literal equations, interval notation and linear inequalities
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve equations systematically, represent inequality solutions and interpret answers in context
- **Assessment angles:** quadratic factorisation, simultaneous-equation setups, interval notation, inequality sign errors
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Trigonometry
- **Key concepts:** trigonometric ratios from 0° to 360°, reciprocal ratios, special angles, right-triangle problem solving and simple trig equations
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate unknown lengths or angles, read diagrams correctly and solve introductory trig equations
- **Assessment angles:** special-angle values, quadrant reasoning, right-triangle applications, simple trig equations
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Investigation or project plus a formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
