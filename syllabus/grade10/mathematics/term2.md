# Grade 10 — Mathematics: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Functions
- **Key concepts:** concept of function, tables, graphs and formulae, linear, quadratic, hyperbolic and exponential graphs, parameter changes, basic trig graphs
- **Skills / outcomes:** convert between representations, sketch prescribed graphs and interpret domain, range, intercepts and turning points
- **Assessment angles:** matching graphs to equations, parameter effects, asymptotes, turning points and intercepts
- **Approximate time:** About 5 weeks

### Euclidean Geometry
- **Key concepts:** similarity and congruence revision, midpoint theorem, special quadrilaterals and formal geometric reasoning
- **Skills / outcomes:** prove and justify geometric properties and solve riders based on quadrilateral theorems
- **Assessment angles:** similarity/congruence reasoning, midpoint theorem, quadrilateral proofs, properties of diagonals
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Examination Preparation and Consolidation
- **Key concepts:** integration of algebra, trigonometry, functions and geometry for mid-year assessment
- **Skills / outcomes:** manage multi-topic questions, select efficient methods and communicate reasoning clearly
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-topic exam questions, calculator discipline, method marks and justification
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or test and the mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
