# Grade 10 — Economics: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Economics basics, scarcity and macroeconomic flows
- **Key concepts:** economics as a discipline, scarcity, opportunity cost, production, consumption, exchange, circular flow, GDP and GNI, business cycles
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret graphs and tables, apply calculations, explain policy choices, analyse case studies, write structured responses
- **Assessment angles:** data-response questions, graph interpretation, calculations, policy paragraphs and short essays

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment and controlled test 1.
- **SBA weighting:** 25% Programme of Assessment / 75% examinations in FET Economics.
