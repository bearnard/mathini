# Grade 10 — Life Orientation: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Development of the self in society
- **Key concepts:** self-concept, self-esteem, self-motivation, values, responsible decisions
- **Skills / outcomes:** reflect on identity and use decision-making models to handle pressure, goals and relationships
- **Assessment angles:** decision-making case studies, values-reflection prompts, short tests on concepts

### Wellbeing and study skills
- **Key concepts:** stress management, healthy lifestyle, time management, study methods, support systems
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply study skills and health routines that improve resilience and academic performance
- **Assessment angles:** study-plan tasks, lifestyle audits, reflection paragraphs

### Citizenship in everyday life
- **Key concepts:** respect, communication, diversity, accountability, empathy
- **Skills / outcomes:** connect personal behaviour to the expectations of responsible citizenship and healthy relationships
- **Assessment angles:** scenario MCQs, discussion prompts, problem-solution writing

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** fitness plans, warm-ups, endurance, flexibility, participation
- **Skills / outcomes:** follow a personal fitness routine and take part responsibly in physical activity
- **Assessment angles:** fitness-plan checklists, participation rubrics, improvement logs

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A written task or assignment, a short test, and ongoing Physical Education Task (PET) evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** FET Life Orientation uses 75% SBA and 25% PET across the year.
