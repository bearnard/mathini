# Grade 10: Life Orientation, Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10-12)
**Source:** CAPS FET Life Orientation Grades 10-12, Annual Teaching Plan, Grade 10 (DBE). Corrected 2026-11-16; the earlier version of this file did not match CAPS.

## Topics

### Development of the self in society (Weeks 1-3, 3 hours)
- **Content:** strategies to enhance self-awareness, self-esteem and self-development (factors including the media; building confidence in self and others; respecting the uniqueness of self and others); power, power relations, masculinity, femininity and gender (differences between a man and a woman, stereotypes, gender differences in physical activity; the influence of gender inequality on relationships and wellbeing: sexual abuse, teenage pregnancy, violence, STIs including HIV and AIDS); the value of exercise programmes that promote fitness, and the link between physical and mental health.

### Careers and career choices (Weeks 4-6, 3 hours)
- **Content:** knowledge about self in relation to subjects, career fields and study choices (interests, abilities, talents, strengths); the difference between career field, occupation, career and job; NCS subject requirements and the steps in choosing and decision-making; life domains (being, becoming, community); socio-economic factors (community needs, finances, affordability, stereotyping, accessibility, the impact of income tax on the final salary package).

### Democracy and human rights (Weeks 7-10, 4 hours)
- **Content:** diversity, discrimination and violations of human rights in contexts such as race, religion, culture, language, gender, age, rural or urban, xenophobia, human trafficking and HIV and AIDS status; the Bill of Rights and international conventions and instruments (Convention on the Rights of the Child, African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of Children, CEDAW); bias, prejudice and discrimination and their impact; challenging prejudice; contemporary events and campaigns, nation-building and protection agencies.

### Physical Education (10 hours across the term)
- **Content:** participation in activities that promote physical fitness; movement performance; safety issues in fitness exercises.

## Assessment
- **Formal assessment:** a written task and the Physical Education Task (PET). The sequence of topics within the term is not fixed.
