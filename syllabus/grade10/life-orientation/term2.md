# Grade 10 — Life Orientation: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Social and environmental responsibility
- **Key concepts:** social issues, environmental issues, community responsibility, sustainable choices
- **Skills / outcomes:** investigate current social or environmental challenges and propose practical interventions
- **Assessment angles:** source-based questions, mini-investigations, campaign ideas

### Risk behaviour and media influence
- **Key concepts:** substance abuse, peer pressure, media messages, abuse, coping strategies
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse risky influences and choose healthier responses grounded in evidence and support networks
- **Assessment angles:** case studies, media-analysis prompts, refusal-skill tasks

### Community engagement
- **Key concepts:** service learning, empathy, volunteering, accountability, collaboration
- **Skills / outcomes:** plan or evaluate a small community-oriented response to a local problem
- **Assessment angles:** project plans, reflection tasks, discussion questions

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** game strategies, coordination, fitness maintenance, teamwork
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply movement and cooperation skills in group activities and monitor personal progress
- **Assessment angles:** skill assessments, team-game rubrics, participation records

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A project, investigation or case study, a written task or test, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** FET Life Orientation uses 75% SBA and 25% PET across the year.
