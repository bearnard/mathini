# Grade 10: Life Orientation, Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10-12)
**Source:** CAPS FET Life Orientation Grades 10-12, Annual Teaching Plan, Grade 10 (DBE). Corrected 2026-11-16; the earlier version of this file did not match CAPS.

## Topics

### Study skills (Weeks 1-3, 3 hours)
- **Content:** listening, reading, comprehension, concentration, memory, organisation and time management; study methods (note-taking, mind-mapping, selecting important concepts and content, assignment and essay construction, making comparisons); critical, creative and problem-solving skills; the process of assessment (internal and external); the annual study plan.

### Social and environmental responsibility (Weeks 4-7, 4 hours)
- **Content:** social and environmental justice; social issues (crime, poverty, food security, food production, violence, HIV and AIDS, safety, security, unequal access to basic resources, lack of basic services such as water and health services); the harmful effects of these issues on personal and community health; social, constructive and critical thinking skills for civic life; youth service development (youth and civic organisations, community services or projects, volunteerism); a group project on a contemporary social issue.

### Physical Education (7 hours)
- **Content:** movement activities that promote skills in playground, community and indigenous games; safety issues in those games.

## Assessment
- **Formal assessment:** mid-year examination (Weeks 8-10) and the Physical Education Task (PET).
