# Grade 10 — Life Orientation: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Careers and career choices
- **Key concepts:** career fields, subject packages, qualifications, interests, labour-market awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** link school subjects and personal strengths to post-school study or work pathways
- **Assessment angles:** career-matching tasks, subject-choice justifications, oral presentations

### World of work documents
- **Key concepts:** CV basics, application forms, personal profiles, evidence files, professionalism
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete simple career documents and present themselves appropriately for opportunities
- **Assessment angles:** form-completion tasks, CV layout checks, document-editing items

### Lifelong learning and planning
- **Key concepts:** goal review, post-school options, TVET and university pathways, motivation
- **Skills / outcomes:** build a realistic plan for the next phase of learning and work exploration
- **Assessment angles:** planning templates, information-search tasks, reflection paragraphs

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** personal fitness review, recreation, active lifestyle targets, participation
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate physical progress and set future activity goals linked to healthy living
- **Assessment angles:** fitness reflections, participation logs, target-setting tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A career portfolio or application task, final SBA moderation, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** FET Life Orientation uses 75% SBA and 25% PET across the year.
