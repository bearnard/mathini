# Grade 10: Life Orientation, Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10-12)
**Source:** CAPS FET Life Orientation Grades 10-12, Annual Teaching Plan, Grade 10 (DBE). Corrected 2026-11-16; the earlier version of this file did not match CAPS.

## Topics

### Careers and career choices (Weeks 1-3, 3 hours)
- **Content:** trends and demands in the job market (emerging demands, changing patterns of careers, scarce skills; growth and decline of occupations); SAQA, the NQF and recognition of prior learning; the need for lifelong learning (the ability to change, re-train and keep developing); formal, informal and non-formal learning.

### Democracy and human rights (Weeks 4-6, 3 hours)
- **Content:** living in a multi-religious society (ethical traditions and religious laws of the major religions in South Africa: Judaism, Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, Baha'i Faith and African religion; indigenous belief systems); coverage of sport and ways to redress biases (gender, race, stereotyping and sporting codes; unfair practices such as drug-taking, match-fixing, subjective umpiring and maladministration; analysis and critical evaluation).

### Physical Education (6 hours)
- **Content:** activities that promote skills in traditional and non-traditional sport; safety issues.

## Assessment
- **Formal assessment:** end-of-year examination (Weeks 7-10) and the Physical Education Task (PET).
