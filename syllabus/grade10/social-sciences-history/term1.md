# Grade 10 — History: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Worlds around 1600 and historical skills
- **Key concepts:** source analysis, paragraph writing, Songhai Empire, trans-Saharan trade, Timbuktu, Ming China or Mughal India
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, chronology, comparing interpretations, paragraph and essay writing
- **Assessment angles:** source-based questions, causation essays, interpretation of cartoons/photos/maps, chronology prompts
- **Historical period / geographical region:** World around 1600: West Africa and Asia

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or source-based task plus essay/source-based test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET History.
