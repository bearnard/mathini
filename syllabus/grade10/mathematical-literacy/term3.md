# Grade 10 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Finance: Income, Expenditure, Profit, Loss and Budgets
- **Key concepts:** income-and-expenditure statements, budgets and profit-loss reasoning
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete, compare and interpret financial records and budget choices
- **Assessment angles:** budget shortfall or surplus, profit vs loss, interpreting financial tables
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Measurement: Perimeter, Area and Volume
- **Key concepts:** measure and calculate perimeter, area and volume in everyday objects or spaces
- **Skills / outcomes:** select suitable measures and solve household or consumer scenarios
- **Assessment angles:** area vs perimeter, area of rooms or surfaces, volume in containers
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Maps, Plans and Models
- **Key concepts:** reading plans, sketching simple models and interpreting physical representations
- **Skills / outcomes:** translate between real objects and their representations and answer practical spatial questions
- **Assessment angles:** plan views, model interpretation, spatial reasoning from drawings
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or investigation and a control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
