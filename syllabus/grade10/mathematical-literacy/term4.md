# Grade 10 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Finance: Interest, Banking and Taxation
- **Key concepts:** simple interest contexts, banking fees and introductory tax-related calculations
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare banking options and interpret common financial transactions or documents
- **Assessment angles:** bank statements, transaction-fee comparisons, interest calculations, tax wording
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Data Handling
- **Key concepts:** collecting, organising, representing and interpreting data in tables and graphs
- **Skills / outcomes:** read graphs critically, summarise data and draw evidence-based conclusions
- **Assessment angles:** mean/median/mode in context, graph misreading, conclusion statements
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Revision and Exam Integration
- **Key concepts:** integrated real-life problem solving across all Mathematical Literacy topics
- **Skills / outcomes:** combine finance, measurement, data and map skills in full examination-style scenarios
- **Assessment angles:** multi-topic case studies, exam pacing and selecting relevant information
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks including exams

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or investigation and the end-of-year examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
