# Grade 10 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Finance: Documents and Tariff Systems
- **Key concepts:** budgets, bills, tariffs, income and expenditure records and financial decision-making
- **Skills / outcomes:** read financial documents, calculate charges and compare tariff options in context
- **Assessment angles:** reading bills, tariff-step calculations, identifying the better option
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Measurement
- **Key concepts:** measuring length, weight, volume and temperature in practical settings
- **Skills / outcomes:** estimate, measure and convert accurately in household or workplace contexts
- **Assessment angles:** unit conversion, reading scales, practical-measure errors
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Maps, Plans and Scale
- **Key concepts:** scale, map work and basic plan interpretation
- **Skills / outcomes:** use scale to calculate actual distances and interpret map or plan features
- **Assessment angles:** scale-factor questions, route planning, map-key interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Probability
- **Key concepts:** chance events in familiar contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** list outcomes and calculate simple probabilities linked to real situations
- **Assessment angles:** equally likely outcomes, language of chance, simple probability comparisons
- **Approximate time:** About 1 week plus revision

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or investigation and the mid-year examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
