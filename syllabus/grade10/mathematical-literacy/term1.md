# Grade 10 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Numbers and Calculations in Context
- **Key concepts:** whole numbers, decimals, fractions, percentages, ratio, rate and calculator use in authentic contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate accurately in everyday contexts and interpret the reasonableness of answers
- **Assessment angles:** best-buy style questions, percentage change, ratio and rate wording, calculator interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Patterns, Relationships and Representations
- **Key concepts:** tables, simple formulae, input-output thinking and representation of relationships
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret patterns, complete tables and describe relationships in words, numbers and graphs
- **Assessment angles:** missing values, graph-to-table translation, trend interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Measurement: Conversions and Time
- **Key concepts:** unit conversions, timetables, elapsed time and practical measurement contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** convert units confidently and solve time or measurement problems drawn from daily life
- **Assessment angles:** time-format conversions, unit choice, practical measurement errors, timetable reading
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or investigation and a control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
