# Grade 10 — Accounting: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Adjustments and financial statements
- **Key concepts:** depreciation, accruals, prepayments, bad debts, final accounts, statement of comprehensive income, statement of financial position
- **Skills / outcomes:** process year-end adjustments and prepare the main financial statements of a sole trader from trial-balance information
- **Assessment angles:** adjustment journals, statement layouts, selecting correct figures for income statement and balance sheet items

### Analysis and interpretation
- **Key concepts:** profitability, liquidity, solvency, basic financial indicators, interpretation of statements
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate ratios, comment on financial health and link figures to decisions by owners and creditors
- **Assessment angles:** ratio calculations, explanatory comments, improvement recommendations based on figures

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Case study on sole-trader financial statements and a control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
This term consolidates Grade 10 financial reporting. Learners should move beyond preparation into interpretation and short written comments backed by figures.
