# Grade 10 — Accounting: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Cost accounting basics
- **Key concepts:** manufacturing environment, direct materials, direct labour, factory overheads, unit cost, cost concepts
- **Skills / outcomes:** distinguish between trading and manufacturing costs and calculate simple production costs
- **Assessment angles:** classification questions, cost-item matching, manufacturing-context calculations

### Budgeting and examination consolidation
- **Key concepts:** cash budget, projected income statement, revision of Grade 10 bookkeeping and statements
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare simple budgets, interpret budget figures and revise across the full year for paper practice
- **Assessment angles:** budget variance hooks, cash-flow reasoning, mixed end-of-year paper questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination (two internal papers) after budgeting consolidation.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
Term 4 is shorter and strongly exam-driven. Budgeting is introduced at a basic level, then the ATP shifts into revision and final-paper preparation.
