# Grade 10 — Accounting: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Bookkeeping foundations and ethics
- **Key concepts:** informal and formal bookkeeping, internal control, code of ethics, GAAP basics, accounting equation, source documents
- **Skills / outcomes:** assess prior knowledge, explain basic accounting language, classify assets/liabilities/equity, relate internal control to cash, stock and fixed assets
- **Assessment angles:** definitions, ethics scenarios, control weaknesses, accounting-equation effects of transactions

### Sole trader cash and credit processing
- **Key concepts:** CRJ, CPJ, PCJ, DJ, DAJ, CJ, CAJ, GJ, general ledger, debtors ledger, creditors ledger, trial balance
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete journals, post to ledgers, balance accounts, prepare trial balances and analyse combined cash and credit transactions
- **Assessment angles:** source-document to journal questions, posting sequences, error correction, trial-balance totals

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Presentation and control test on term 1 bookkeeping foundations.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
Based on CAPS and the 2023/24 DBE ATP. Grade 10 starts with sole-trader bookkeeping under the perpetual inventory system, while ethics, GAAP and internal control are integrated throughout the term.
