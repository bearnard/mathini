# Grade 10 — Accounting: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Combined transactions and reconciliations
- **Key concepts:** combined cash and credit transactions, debtors reconciliation, creditors reconciliation, control accounts, subsidiary ledgers
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete combined bookkeeping cycles, reconcile control accounts with lists, identify omissions and correct errors
- **Assessment angles:** reconciliation adjustments, matching lists to control accounts, error-spotting items

### Payroll and year-end procedures
- **Key concepts:** salaries and wages, deductions, employer contributions, SJ, WJ, pre-adjustment trial balance, post-adjustment trial balance, final accounts
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate payroll entries, record salary journals, process year-end adjustments and prepare final accounts for a sole trader
- **Assessment angles:** pay-slip calculations, adjustment entries, final-account formats, ethics in payroll and labour practices

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project on bookkeeping/final accounts and the June examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
Term 2 extends Grade 10 into full accounting-cycle work. Salary processing and adjustment work are useful bridges into financial statements and exam-style questions.
