# Grade 10 — Business Studies: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Creative thinking and opportunity analysis
- **Key concepts:** creative thinking, problem-solving cycle, business opportunities, internal and external market research, related factors
- **Skills / outcomes:** generate ideas, solve business problems and evaluate whether an opportunity is realistic
- **Assessment angles:** problem-solving steps, opportunity screening, short case-study evaluations

### Business information and planning
- **Key concepts:** presentation of business information, verbal and non-verbal presentation, support materials, business plan structure, viability
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare information for presentations and draft basic business plans with market, operations and finance components
- **Assessment angles:** presentation-method questions, business-plan sections, application of planning content to scenarios

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project and control test on term 3 entrepreneurial content.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
This term shifts into entrepreneurship and planning. Learners should be able to move from an idea to a structured action-oriented plan.
