# Grade 10 — Business Studies: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Business sectors and socio-economic issues
- **Key concepts:** primary, secondary and tertiary sectors, formal and informal sectors, public and private sector, inequality, poverty, inclusivity, unemployment, HIV/AIDS, gambling, crime
- **Skills / outcomes:** classify sectors, explain socio-economic pressures and evaluate how issues affect businesses and communities
- **Assessment angles:** sector-comparison items, impact questions, recommendation-style responses

### Social responsibility and forms of ownership
- **Key concepts:** social responsibility, CSR, sole proprietor, partnership, non-profit company, profit companies, co-operatives
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare forms of ownership, advise on suitable ownership choices and suggest responsible business responses to social problems
- **Assessment angles:** advantages/disadvantages tables, ownership comparisons, scenario-based recommendations

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Presentation plus the June examination on terms 1 and 2 work.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
Term 2 combines contextual social-issue work with foundational ownership content. Learners should practise using business terminology in full-sentence responses.
