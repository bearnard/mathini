# Grade 10 — Business Studies: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Business environments and functions
- **Key concepts:** micro environment, vision, mission, goals, organisational culture, structure, eight business functions, management and leadership
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify internal business features, explain the purpose of functions and relate organograms and resources to business performance
- **Assessment angles:** define-and-explain items, scenario-based function identification, short structured responses

### Quality and external environments
- **Key concepts:** quality, quality control, quality assurance, market environment, macro environment, opportunities, threats, interrelationship of environments
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply quality ideas to business functions, explain market and macro pressures and compare the three environments
- **Assessment angles:** case studies on competition and threats, matching quality indicators to functions, paragraph questions on environmental links

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Case study and control test on term 1 business environments content.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
Based on CAPS and the 2023/24 DBE ATP. Grade 10 begins with strong terminology work; exam questions often move quickly from definitions into scenarios.
