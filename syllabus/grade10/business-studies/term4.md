# Grade 10 — Business Studies: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Relationship and team performance
- **Key concepts:** interpersonal relationships, prejudice, values, diversity, team criteria, collaborative performance, conflict in teams
- **Skills / outcomes:** explain factors that influence teamwork and suggest ways to improve relationships and group output
- **Assessment angles:** teamwork scenarios, conflict-resolution recommendations, professionalism prompts

### Revision and final-paper preparation
- **Key concepts:** revision of terms 1 to 4, business environments, operations, ventures and roles
- **Skills / outcomes:** integrate content across the year and prepare for paper 1 and paper 2 style questions
- **Assessment angles:** mixed short questions, case studies, paragraph answers and extended responses

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination with two papers after revision.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
Term 4 is heavily revision-based. The ATP keeps a short content strand on team performance before switching to examination preparation.
