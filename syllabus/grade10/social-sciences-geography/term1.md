# Grade 10 — Geography: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### The atmosphere
- **Key concepts:** structure of the atmosphere, insolation, greenhouse effect, global warming, condensation, rainfall types, synoptic weather maps, GIS basics
- **Skills / outcomes:** mapwork, GIS awareness, graph and data interpretation, fieldwork or case-study analysis
- **Assessment angles:** mapwork items, data-response questions, climate/settlement/resource essays, interpretation of graphs and images
- **Historical period / geographical region:** Global atmosphere with African climate examples

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Data-handling task and test.
- **SBA weighting:** 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Geography.
