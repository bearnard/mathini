# Grade 10 — Life Sciences: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Plant tissues and organs
- **Key concepts:** meristematic tissue, permanent tissue, epidermis, parenchyma, xylem, phloem, root, stem, leaf
- **Skills / outcomes:** Identify tissues in sections, relate structure to function, and explain transpiration and translocation.
- **Assessment angles:** Cross-section diagrams, tissue-function matching, and transport-process questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Animal tissues and support systems
- **Key concepts:** epithelial, connective, muscle and nerve tissue, axial skeleton, appendicular skeleton, support and movement
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare tissue types and explain how body structures support movement and protection.
- **Assessment angles:** Tissue identification, skeleton-label questions, and structure-function comparisons.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Transport systems in mammals
- **Key concepts:** heart, blood vessels, pulmonary circulation, systemic circulation, cardiac cycle
- **Skills / outcomes:** Trace blood flow through the body and interpret diagrams of the circulatory system.
- **Assessment angles:** Heart diagrams, blood-flow sequences, and system-comparison questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment and mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
