# Grade 10 — Life Sciences: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Chemistry of life and enzymes
- **Key concepts:** water, minerals, carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, nucleic acids, vitamins, enzymes, scientific method
- **Skills / outcomes:** Use biological vocabulary accurately, interpret simple investigations, and explain factors affecting enzyme action.
- **Assessment angles:** Molecule comparisons, practical-variable questions, and enzyme graphs.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Cells and transport
- **Key concepts:** light and electron microscopes, organelles, plant and animal cells, diffusion, osmosis, active transport
- **Skills / outcomes:** Label cell diagrams, calculate magnification, and explain transport across membranes.
- **Assessment angles:** Microscope questions, organelle-function items, and osmosis scenarios.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Mitosis and cancer
- **Key concepts:** cell cycle, interphase, mitosis phases, cytokinesis, uncontrolled cell division, cancer basics
- **Skills / outcomes:** Sequence mitosis correctly, interpret cell-division diagrams, and explain the importance of mitosis.
- **Assessment angles:** Phase identification, comparing mitosis stages, and short application questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical task and formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
