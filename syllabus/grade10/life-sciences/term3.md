# Grade 10 — Life Sciences: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Biosphere to ecosystems
- **Key concepts:** biosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, biomes, abiotic and biotic factors, food webs, nutrient cycles
- **Skills / outcomes:** Explain ecosystem interactions, compare South African biomes, and interpret ecological diagrams.
- **Assessment angles:** Food-web items, biome comparisons, and nutrient-cycle questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Biodiversity and classification
- **Key concepts:** history of classification, binomial system, five kingdoms, diagnostic features
- **Skills / outcomes:** Use classification systems correctly and compare major groups of living organisms.
- **Assessment angles:** Classification tables, kingdom characteristics, and naming conventions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### History of life on Earth
- **Key concepts:** geological timescale, eras, Cambrian explosion, fossil formation, mass extinctions
- **Skills / outcomes:** Sequence major events in Earth’s history and explain the value of fossils as evidence.
- **Assessment angles:** Timeline tasks, fossil evidence questions, and extinction-case prompts.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical task and formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
