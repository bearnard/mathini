# Grade 10 — Physical Sciences: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–4

## Topics

### Conservation of mechanical energy
- **Key concepts:** conservation law, transfer between kinetic and potential energy, ideal vs real systems
- **Skills / outcomes:** Solve multi-step energy problems and explain when energy is transformed rather than lost.
- **Assessment angles:** Problem-solving items, energy-bar reasoning, and identifying common errors.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Revision and examination practice
- **Key concepts:** integration of physics and chemistry topics, formula use, practical data handling, exam strategy
- **Skills / outcomes:** Consolidate calculations, interpret experiments, and prepare for full-paper questions.
- **Assessment angles:** Mixed calculations, practical write-ups, and terminology recall.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials, Energy & Change

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
