# Grade 10 — Physical Sciences: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Chemical change and stoichiometry
- **Key concepts:** balanced equations, mole concept, concentration, empirical formula, percentage composition, theoretical yield
- **Skills / outcomes:** Balance equations, convert between mass and moles, and solve stoichiometry problems.
- **Assessment angles:** Mole calculations, reaction tables, and word-to-symbol equation items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

### Vectors, motion and mechanical energy
- **Key concepts:** scalars, vectors, displacement, velocity, acceleration, motion graphs, kinetic energy, gravitational potential energy
- **Skills / outcomes:** Analyse motion graphs, solve one-dimensional kinematics problems, and calculate energy changes.
- **Assessment angles:** Graph interpretation, equation substitution, and energy comparisons.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical investigation and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
