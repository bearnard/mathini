# Grade 10 — Physical Sciences: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Matter, states and phase change
- **Key concepts:** particle model, states of matter, mixtures, heating curves, cooling curves, kinetic molecular theory
- **Skills / outcomes:** Explain phase changes microscopically, interpret heating curves, and classify matter correctly.
- **Assessment angles:** Particle diagrams, heating-curve questions, and classification tables.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

### Atoms, periodic table and bonding
- **Key concepts:** atomic structure, isotopes, electron configuration, periodic trends, ionic, covalent and metallic bonding
- **Skills / outcomes:** Draw atomic models, predict bonding type, and interpret periodic-table information.
- **Assessment angles:** Electron arrangements, Lewis structures, and trend-comparison questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or practical task and mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
