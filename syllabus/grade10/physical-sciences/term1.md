# Grade 10 — Physical Sciences: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Waves, sound and light
- **Key concepts:** transverse waves, longitudinal waves, wavelength, frequency, wave speed, sound, electromagnetic radiation
- **Skills / outcomes:** Calculate wave properties, distinguish wave types, and interpret wave or spectrum diagrams.
- **Assessment angles:** Wave calculations, EM-spectrum labeling, and comparisons of pitch or loudness.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Electrostatics and electric circuits
- **Key concepts:** charge, conservation of charge, potential difference, current, resistance, circuit concepts
- **Skills / outcomes:** Reason about charging, analyse simple circuits, and apply basic electrical relationships.
- **Assessment angles:** Charge questions, circuit diagrams, and short formula-based items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical investigation and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
