# Grade 10 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Revision across Papers 1–3
- **Key concepts:** comprehension, summary, literature, essay planning, transactional recall
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate the paper structure used across the FET phase
- **Assessment angles:** paper-format recognition, summary practice, literature revision

### Exam writing and proofreading
- **Key concepts:** essay planning, editing, time management, format control
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete writing tasks accurately under timed conditions
- **Assessment angles:** editing traps, planning quality, format questions

### Prescribed-text consolidation
- **Key concepts:** poetry, prose, drama, revision of key themes and characters
- **Skills / outcomes:** synthesise literature learning for final assessment
- **Assessment angles:** quotation recall, theme linkage, contextual prompts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of any remaining oral evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is revision-heavy and mirrors the assessment structure used in later FET grades.
