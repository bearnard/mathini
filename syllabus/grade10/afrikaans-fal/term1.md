# Grade 10 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### FET orientation and core skills
- **Key concepts:** listening, reading comprehension, sentence construction, introduction to literature genres
- **Skills / outcomes:** build confidence with FET Afrikaans FAL routines across reading, writing and oral work
- **Assessment angles:** comprehension recall, summary hooks, genre identification

### Friendly and everyday writing
- **Key concepts:** friendly letter, short paragraph, diary-style writing, process writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce shorter transactional and personal texts with correct basic format
- **Assessment angles:** layout, audience, opening and closing conventions

### Grammar review in context
- **Key concepts:** word order, tenses, punctuation, editing, vocabulary growth
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise foundational grammar while applying it to realistic texts
- **Assessment angles:** editing items, tense shifts, sentence-order questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Baseline or diagnostic work, a writing task, oral activity and a language or comprehension test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
FET Afrikaans FAL is organised around Paper 1 (language in context), Paper 2 (literature) and Paper 3 (writing). Prescribed texts vary by DBE cycle and school.
