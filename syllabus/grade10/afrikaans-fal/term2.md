# Grade 10 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Oral presentations and prose study
- **Key concepts:** oral presentation, prose or drama study, character, conflict, theme
- **Skills / outcomes:** speak more formally and respond to longer prescribed texts
- **Assessment angles:** oral criteria, character and theme questions, contextual prompts

### Functional and reflective writing
- **Key concepts:** diary entries, instructions, reviews, narrative response, audience and purpose
- **Skills / outcomes:** shift between everyday and more formal writing purposes
- **Assessment angles:** text-type features, sequencing, review language

### Mid-year Paper 1 preparation
- **Key concepts:** summary, visual literacy, language structures, editing, comprehension
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare for mid-year assessment with stronger Paper 1 routines
- **Assessment angles:** summary rules, visual-text hooks, grammar in context

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal writing, oral work and the mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 usually broadens both literature and writing while leading into the first full exam cycle.
