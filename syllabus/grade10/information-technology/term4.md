# Grade 10 — Information Technology: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Social implications and cyber safety
- **Key concepts:** ethics, privacy, security, cybercrime, digital citizenship, health impacts, environmental considerations
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate social and ethical effects of technology and justify safe computing choices
- **Assessment angles:** short scenario responses, cyber-safety recommendations, ethics paragraphs

### Revision and year-end examination preparation
- **Key concepts:** full-year practical and theory consolidation, algorithm revision, programming fluency, exam strategy
- **Skills / outcomes:** review across theory and practical strands and prepare for year-end papers
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-paper revision, timed code tracing and integrated theory questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Year-end practical and theory examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** For Grades 10–11, 2023 ATP mediation indicates SBA converts to 40% and examinations to 60%; PAT work is built into the year programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is mainly consolidation. Use it to strengthen exam technique in both theory and programming rather than introducing too much new content.
