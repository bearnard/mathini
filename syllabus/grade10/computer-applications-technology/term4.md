# Grade 10 — Computer Applications Technology: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Spreadsheets, information management and PAT completion
- **Key concepts:** charts and graphs, data interpretation, report writing, summarising information, plagiarism, references, PAT phase 2
- **Skills / outcomes:** turn data into graphs and reports, process information critically and complete PAT evidence accurately
- **Assessment angles:** graph selection, report-structure questions, PAT moderation checkpoints

### Document integration and exam consolidation
- **Key concepts:** documents, word processing integration, social implications, troubleshooting, case studies, practical and theory paper preparation
- **Skills / outcomes:** integrate text and graphics, solve common software problems and revise for the final practical and theory examinations
- **Assessment angles:** integrated practical tasks, computer-crime examples, exam-style case studies

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PAT completion and end-of-year practical plus theory examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** CAT uses task-based SBA; Grade 12 commonly uses 25% SBA + 25% PAT + 50% exams, while Grades 10–11 follow the school POA with PAT-style practical work.

## Notes
Term 4 is short and assessment-heavy. The ATP expects finalisation of the practical assessment task before end-of-year examination papers.
