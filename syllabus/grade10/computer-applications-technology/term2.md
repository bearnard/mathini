# Grade 10 — Computer Applications Technology: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Systems, networks and digital responsibility
- **Key concepts:** extended hardware concepts, software categories, device management, networks, network aims, copyright, licensing, software piracy, ethical use
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify devices and software, explain why networks are used and discuss safe and legal technology use
- **Assessment angles:** hardware/software comparisons, network basics, copyright and licensing scenarios

### Word processing, spreadsheets and information management
- **Key concepts:** advanced word processing, tables, headers and footers, spreadsheets, cell references, formulae, ranges, data vs information, problem-solving steps
- **Skills / outcomes:** format longer documents, create spreadsheets with formulae and begin structured information-management tasks
- **Assessment angles:** spreadsheet formula questions, practical document edits, control-test style integrated application tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical test and mid-year/control-test style assessment.
- **SBA weighting:** CAT uses task-based SBA; Grade 12 commonly uses 25% SBA + 25% PAT + 50% exams, while Grades 10–11 follow the school POA with PAT-style practical work.

## Notes
Term 2 broadens CAT beyond word processing into spreadsheets and information management. Practical fluency matters as much as theory recall.
