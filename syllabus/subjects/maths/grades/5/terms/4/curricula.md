# **Grade 5 Mathematics Syllabus: Term 4 (Western Cape / CAPS)**

**Curriculum Alignment:** CAPS (Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement) & WCED Annual Teaching Plan (ATP).

**Focus:** Term 4 is a short term focused on finishing specific measurement/geometry topics and revising the entire year's work for the final assessment.

## **1\. New Content & Consolidation Topics**

### **A. Whole Numbers (Revision & Consolidation)**

* **Counting & Ordering:**  
  * Working with numbers up to **999,999** (6 digits).  
  * Comparing numbers ($\<$, $\>$, $=$).  
  * Representing numbers in expanded notation (e.g., $400,000 \+ 20,000 \+ 500 \+ 9$).  
* **Place Value:** Recognizing the value of digits in large numbers.  
* **Rounding Off:** Rounding to the nearest **5, 10, 100, and 1,000**.  
* **Calculations:**  
  * Addition and Subtraction (Column method up to 5 digits).  
  * Multiplication (3-digit $\\times$ 2-digit).  
  * Division (3-digit $\\div$ 2-digit).

### **B. Measurement: Mass**

* **Units:** Understanding grams ($g$) and kilograms ($kg$).  
* **Conversions:** \* Converting $g$ to $kg$ and vice versa (e.g., $2,500g \= 2\\frac{1}{2}kg$ or $2.5kg$).  
* **Instruments:** Reading analogue and digital scales.  
* **Problem Solving:** Calculations involving mass (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of mass units).

### **C. Properties of 3D Objects**

* **Identification:** Distinguishing between Prisms, Pyramids, Cylinders, Cones, and Spheres.  
* **Characteristics:**  
  * **Faces:** Flat or curved surfaces.  
  * **Edges:** Where two faces meet.  
  * **Vertices:** Corners where edges meet.  
* **Sorting:** Classifying shapes based on the shape of their faces (e.g., "This prism has rectangular faces").

### **D. Measurement: Perimeter, Area, and Volume**

* **Perimeter:** \* Measurement of the distance *around* a 2D shape.  
  * Measured in $mm$, $cm$, and $m$.  
* **Area:** \* Measurement of the surface covered.  
  * Usually calculated in Grade 5 by counting square units (grids).  
* **Volume/Capacity:** \* The amount of space an object occupies.  
  * Understanding the concept by packing cubes into boxes.

### **E. Probability (Data Handling)**

* **Experiments:** Simple trials (flipping coins, rolling dice, spinning spinners).  
* **Language of Chance:**  
  * Certain  
  * Likely  
  * Unlikely  
  * Impossible  
  * Even chance (50/50)

### **F. Geometric Patterns**

* Identifying, describing, and extending patterns in drawings and tessellating shapes.

## **2\. End-of-Year Controlled Test Framework**

The Term 4 final mark is primarily based on the End-of-Year Controlled Test. This test covers work from **Terms 1, 2, 3, and 4**.

### **Suggested Weighting per Topic Area**

| Content Area | Approximate Weighting (%) | Key Concepts to Revise |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Numbers, Operations & Relationships** | **\~50%** | Whole numbers, Long Division, Multiplication, Common Fractions, Decimals. |
| **Patterns, Functions & Algebra** | **\~10%** | Geometric patterns, Number sentences, Flow diagrams. |
| **Space & Shape (Geometry)** | **\~15%** | 2D shapes, 3D objects, Symmetry, Transformations, Viewing objects. |
| **Measurement** | **\~15%** | Length, Mass, Capacity, Time, Temperature, Perimeter/Area. |
| **Data Handling** | **\~10%** | Tally tables, Bar graphs, Pictographs, Probability. |

## **3\. Study Tips for Term 4**

1. **Don't Ignore Term 1-3:** The biggest mistake students make is only studying Term 4 work. The final exam includes Fractions (Term 2/3) and Time (Term 3).  
2. **Practice Long Division:** This is often the highest mark-losing section. Practice the "DMSB" method (Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Bring down) daily.  
3. **Learn Definitions:** Ensure the student knows the difference between a *prism* and a *pyramid*, and between *area* and *perimeter*.  
4. **Mental Maths:** Keep practicing times tables (up to $12 \\times 12$) to ensure speed during the exam.
