# **Grade 6 Mathematics Syllabus: Term 1 (Western Cape / CAPS)**

**Curriculum Alignment:** CAPS (Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement) & WCED Annual Teaching Plan (ATP).

**Focus:** Term 1 focuses on establishing a strong foundation in whole numbers and introducing more complex operations and fractions.

## **1. Whole Numbers**

### **A. Counting, Ordering & Representing**
*   Numbers up to **9 digits** (hundred millions).
*   Place value and expanded notation.
*   Comparing numbers (<, >, =).
*   Rounding off to nearest 5, 10, 100, 1000, 100 000.
*   Prime numbers up to 100.

### **B. Operations**
*   **Addition & Subtraction:** Column method with at least 6-digit numbers.
*   **Multiplication:** 4-digit by 3-digit numbers.
*   **Division:** Long division (4-digit by 2-digit).
*   **Properties:** Commutative, associative, distributive.

## **2. Common Fractions**

*   Comparing and ordering fractions.
*   Equivalent fractions.
*   Addition and subtraction of fractions with same and different denominators.
*   Fractions of whole numbers.

## **3. Decimal Fractions**

*   Recognizing and ordering decimal fractions.
*   Place value to at least 2 decimal places.
*   Calculations with decimals.

## **4. Patterns, Functions & Algebra**

*   Numeric and geometric patterns.
*   Input and output values (flow diagrams).

## **5. Space & Shape (Geometry)**

*   Properties of 2D shapes.
*   Properties of 3D objects.
