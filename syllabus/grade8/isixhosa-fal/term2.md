# Grade 8 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** oral presentations, interviews, everyday transactions, summarising spoken texts
- **Skills / outcomes:** present short oral responses and follow or summarise familiar spoken texts
- **Assessment angles:** presentation rubrics, listening-for-detail items, role-play tasks

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** folklore, youth prose, media texts, comprehension, theme and character
- **Skills / outcomes:** read longer prose and discuss character, conflict and message with more confidence
- **Assessment angles:** theme questions, character evidence, media-text prompts

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** email, review, report, informal paragraphs, process writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** write shorter transactional texts with the correct tone, format and audience awareness
- **Assessment angles:** email layout, review language, sequencing and editing tasks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** pronouns, direct and indirect speech, question forms, conjunctions, concord
- **Skills / outcomes:** report information more accurately and link ideas in connected sentences
- **Assessment angles:** speech-conversion items, conjunction choices, pronoun and concord checks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Controlled test, oral presentation and a formal writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
