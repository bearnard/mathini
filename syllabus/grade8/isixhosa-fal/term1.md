# Grade 8 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** introductions, daily life discussions, prepared reading, listening comprehension, classroom interaction
- **Skills / outcomes:** listen for key details, take part in short discussions and build confidence in spoken isiXhosa
- **Assessment angles:** oral-reading rubrics, listening recall, dialogue prompts

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** visual texts, advertisements, short stories, poems, comprehension strategies
- **Skills / outcomes:** read varied short texts and explain how words and visuals shape meaning
- **Assessment angles:** visual-literacy questions, plot sequencing, vocabulary-in-context items

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** narrative paragraph, descriptive writing, friendly letter, dialogue, drafting
- **Skills / outcomes:** write connected pieces for familiar purposes using planning, drafting and redrafting
- **Assessment angles:** format checks, paragraph cohesion, opening and closing choices

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** nouns, concords, adjectives, simple tenses, punctuation, spelling
- **Skills / outcomes:** strengthen sentence control while applying grammar through reading and writing tasks
- **Assessment angles:** editing items, tense shifts, concord questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal oral work begins together with a major writing or comprehension task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
