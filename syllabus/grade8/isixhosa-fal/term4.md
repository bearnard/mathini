# Grade 8 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** revision of oral tasks, listening comprehension, prepared response, fluency
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply the year’s speaking and listening strategies in revision and exam-style contexts
- **Assessment angles:** oral recall, listening quizzes, short prepared-response tasks

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** revision across stories, poems, media texts, comprehension, summary
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise reading and viewing strategies across the year’s genres
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-genre questions, summary skills, evidence retrieval

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** summary, report, review, exam writing, proofreading
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce concise transactional pieces and revise process-writing skills for final assessment
- **Assessment angles:** format recall, selecting key detail, editing checklists

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** revision of grammar, concords, tenses, punctuation, spelling
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate major language structures for the final examination
- **Assessment angles:** grammar MCQs, sentence improvement, proofreading items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of project or oral evidence from earlier terms.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
