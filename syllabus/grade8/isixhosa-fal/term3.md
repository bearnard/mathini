# Grade 8 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** prepared speech, discussion, role play, oral response to literature
- **Skills / outcomes:** speak more independently about texts and familiar issues and respond appropriately in group tasks
- **Assessment angles:** speech rubrics, discussion prompts, oral-response checklists

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** drama scenes, short stories, poetry, visual support, literary response
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret literary features in shorter set texts and scenes and explain personal responses
- **Assessment angles:** speaker identification, conflict prompts, poetry mood questions

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** descriptive essay, narrative writing, formal letter, project writing, presentation
- **Skills / outcomes:** move between creative and practical writing and complete a small project with evidence of revision
- **Assessment angles:** project planning, format recall, paragraph development

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** adverbs, prepositions, clauses, punctuation, editing, vocabulary development
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve sentence variety and edit writing more accurately in context
- **Assessment angles:** error correction, clause tasks, vocabulary questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project submission or oral component, plus a formal language or writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
