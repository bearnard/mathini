# Grade 8 — Life Orientation: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Social and environmental responsibility
- **Key concepts:** environmental health, pollution, water, waste, conservation
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify local environmental issues and suggest practical ways learners can protect their surroundings
- **Assessment angles:** poster prompts, source-based questions, community-action ideas

### Health and risk behaviour
- **Key concepts:** substance abuse, unsafe situations, personal safety, resilience
- **Skills / outcomes:** discuss common risks, practise refusal skills and choose safer responses in pressured situations
- **Assessment angles:** what-would-you-do items, anti-substance-abuse messages, safety-plan tasks

### Community responsibility
- **Key concepts:** services, reporting channels, shared spaces, empathy, volunteering
- **Skills / outcomes:** show how communities support wellbeing and explain how young people can contribute positively
- **Assessment angles:** service-matching questions, mini-project prompts, discussion tasks

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** net or wall games, agility, endurance, coordination, team cooperation
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve movement control and apply skills in small-group activities
- **Assessment angles:** skills ladders, mini-game rubrics, self-assessment logs

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A formal health or environmental responsibility task, a written test or project, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase Life Orientation uses 80% SBA and 20% PET across the year.
