# Grade 8 — Life Orientation: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Careers and career choices
- **Key concepts:** interests, abilities, occupations, work roles, aspiration mapping
- **Skills / outcomes:** connect strengths and interests to possible career fields and community roles
- **Assessment angles:** career-interest inventories, matching tasks, oral presentations on occupations

### Learning styles and exam readiness
- **Key concepts:** revision plans, memory strategies, note-making, stress management
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare for assessments using structured revision and time-management routines
- **Assessment angles:** study-plan checks, revision strategy questions, reflective writing

### Responsible decision-making
- **Key concepts:** goal setting, consequences, influence, resilience, healthy routines
- **Skills / outcomes:** make reasoned choices about schoolwork, friendships and future planning
- **Assessment angles:** decision-tree items, scenario MCQs, action-plan tasks

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** games application, personal fitness, participation review, recreation
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply the year’s movement skills and review personal improvement in physical activity
- **Assessment angles:** participation records, game strategy talks, fitness reflections

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A careers or study-planning task, year-end SBA consolidation, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase Life Orientation uses 80% SBA and 20% PET across the year.
