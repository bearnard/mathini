# Grade 8 — Life Orientation: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Development of the self in society
- **Key concepts:** self-image, self-concept, self-motivation, strengths, support systems
- **Skills / outcomes:** reflect on identity, recognise influences on self-esteem and set personal goals for the year
- **Assessment angles:** self-profile prompts, scenario questions, goal-setting checklists

### Relationships and emotional wellbeing
- **Key concepts:** peer pressure, bullying, abuse, communication, help-seeking
- **Skills / outcomes:** practise assertive responses, identify warning signs of bullying or abuse and explain where to get help
- **Assessment angles:** case studies, role-play prompts, discussion questions on support networks

### Study skills and personal organisation
- **Key concepts:** time management, homework routines, concentration, planning
- **Skills / outcomes:** use simple study strategies and organise schoolwork more independently
- **Assessment angles:** study timetable tasks, matching strategy to problem, reflection paragraphs

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** warm-ups, basic fitness, invasion-game skills, safety and fair play
- **Skills / outcomes:** participate in fitness and game activities while following rules, teamwork and safety routines
- **Assessment angles:** participation rubrics, skill drills, fitness-record tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A source-based task or project, a class test or assignment, and ongoing Physical Education Task (PET) evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase Life Orientation uses 80% SBA and 20% PET across the year.
