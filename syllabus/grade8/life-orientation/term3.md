# Grade 8 — Life Orientation: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Democracy and human rights
- **Key concepts:** constitutional rights, responsibilities, equality, dignity, respect
- **Skills / outcomes:** link rights to responsibilities and explain how everyday actions protect or violate others’ rights
- **Assessment angles:** rights-and-responsibilities matching, case studies, short written responses

### Diversity and nation-building
- **Key concepts:** culture, prejudice, stereotyping, inclusion, ubuntu
- **Skills / outcomes:** discuss diversity respectfully and challenge prejudice using examples from school and community life
- **Assessment angles:** debate hooks, scenario analysis, poster or pledge tasks

### Conflict management and communication
- **Key concepts:** mediation, assertiveness, listening, compromise, problem solving
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply constructive ways to resolve disagreements and maintain healthy relationships
- **Assessment angles:** dialogue completion, role-play rubrics, reflection questions

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** athletics, relays, indigenous games, flexibility, teamwork
- **Skills / outcomes:** participate in movement challenges and demonstrate cooperation and safe technique
- **Assessment angles:** timed events, indigenous-game demonstrations, teamwork observation

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A democracy or rights investigation, a controlled test, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase Life Orientation uses 80% SBA and 20% PET across the year.
