# Grade 8 — Creative Arts: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual Arts
- **Key concepts:** elements of art, observational drawing, line, shape, colour, texture, 2D composition
- **Skills / outcomes:** draw from observation and use the elements of art deliberately in a studio task
- **Assessment angles:** sketchbook exercises, labelled-image questions, practical artwork rubrics

### Music
- **Key concepts:** beat, rhythm, metre, pitch, dynamics, notation basics, listening skills
- **Skills / outcomes:** sing or play short patterns and identify basic musical elements in listening activities
- **Assessment angles:** clap-back tasks, notation MCQs, short solo or group performances

### Drama
- **Key concepts:** body warm-up, voice projection, tableaux, improvisation, character
- **Skills / outcomes:** use voice and body in short improvised scenes and communicate simple dramatic ideas
- **Assessment angles:** role-play rubrics, freeze-frame analysis, drama terminology checks

### Dance
- **Key concepts:** locomotor and non-locomotor movement, posture, space, time, safe stretching
- **Skills / outcomes:** repeat simple movement phrases and respond accurately to counts and cues
- **Assessment angles:** sequence memory tasks, beat response, safe-technique checklists

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A diagnostic practical, an arts-literacy or theory check and a short performance or studio task.
- **SBA weighting:** CAPS balances practical work with written arts-literacy tasks; no single term percentage is fixed in the ATP.
