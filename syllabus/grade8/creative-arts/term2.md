# Grade 8 — Creative Arts: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual Arts
- **Key concepts:** principles of design, pattern, printmaking, collage, African visual references
- **Skills / outcomes:** plan and produce a design-based artwork using pattern, repetition and contrast
- **Assessment angles:** design briefs, process notes, practical assessment rubrics

### Music
- **Key concepts:** South African music styles, melody, texture, ensemble balance, call-and-response
- **Skills / outcomes:** perform short ensemble pieces and compare local styles through listening and discussion
- **Assessment angles:** style-identification questions, ensemble rehearsal checks, listening logs

### Drama
- **Key concepts:** storytelling, script excerpts, improvisation, focus, tension, audience awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** shape short scenes from stories and rehearse clearer beginnings, middles and endings
- **Assessment angles:** scene-building rubrics, cue-card prompts, reflective discussion questions

### Dance
- **Key concepts:** motif development, folk or social dance, rhythm, group patterns, coordination
- **Skills / outcomes:** develop short dance motifs and work accurately with partners or groups
- **Assessment angles:** pattern recall, choreography journals, group performance rubrics

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A practical artwork or choreography, a written arts-literacy response and an ensemble or drama activity.
- **SBA weighting:** CAPS balances practical work with written arts-literacy tasks; no single term percentage is fixed in the ATP.
