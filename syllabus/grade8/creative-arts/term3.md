# Grade 8 — Creative Arts: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual Arts
- **Key concepts:** South African and African art, symbolism, mixed media, visual literacy
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret artworks from different contexts and create a themed mixed-media response
- **Assessment angles:** artist-research prompts, visual-literacy questions, themed artwork rubrics

### Music
- **Key concepts:** binary and ternary form, composition basics, ostinato, listening analysis
- **Skills / outcomes:** compose simple melodic or rhythmic ideas and identify musical form in set examples
- **Assessment angles:** form-recognition items, composition checklists, listening short answers

### Drama
- **Key concepts:** characterisation, conflict, staging, scripted dialogue, rehearsal
- **Skills / outcomes:** develop characters and stage short scripted or devised scenes with improved focus
- **Assessment angles:** character hot-seating, scene rubrics, rehearsal reflection tasks

### Dance
- **Key concepts:** space, energy, choreographic devices, cultural dance study, performance quality
- **Skills / outcomes:** link movement phrases into a longer choreographed sequence and discuss style choices
- **Assessment angles:** movement-analysis prompts, choreography rubrics, peer feedback

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A process project, a performance presentation and a controlled theory or reflection task.
- **SBA weighting:** CAPS balances practical work with written arts-literacy tasks; no single term percentage is fixed in the ATP.
