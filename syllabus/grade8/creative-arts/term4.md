# Grade 8 — Creative Arts: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Visual Arts
- **Key concepts:** portfolio selection, exhibition planning, finishing techniques, reflection
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare artworks for display and explain artistic choices using appropriate terms
- **Assessment angles:** portfolio moderation, artist-statement writing, display planning

### Music
- **Key concepts:** performance revision, genre comparison, aural recall, stage etiquette
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine a prepared item and review theory through listening and performance practice
- **Assessment angles:** aural quizzes, performance rubrics, genre-matching questions

### Drama
- **Key concepts:** scripted performance, rehearsal discipline, audience etiquette, evaluation
- **Skills / outcomes:** polish a short performance and evaluate how dramatic techniques shaped meaning
- **Assessment angles:** performance rubrics, cue accuracy, reflective questions

### Dance
- **Key concepts:** final routine, timing, expression, appreciation, careers awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** perform a complete dance sequence and discuss dance roles in school and community contexts
- **Assessment angles:** final practical checks, appreciation prompts, choreography reflection

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A final practical examination or showcase plus end-of-year theory or literacy assessment.
- **SBA weighting:** CAPS balances practical work with written arts-literacy tasks; no single term percentage is fixed in the ATP.
