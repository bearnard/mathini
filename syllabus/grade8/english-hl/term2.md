# Grade 8 — English Home Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Media and literary texts
- **Key concepts:** news reports, articles, folklore, drama, plot, character, conflict
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare media and literary texts and identify how information or stories are shaped
- **Assessment angles:** fact and opinion, headline technique, conflict and theme questions

### Formal correspondence and reviews
- **Key concepts:** formal letter, informal letter, email, review, register, audience
- **Skills / outcomes:** write for formal and semi-formal contexts with the correct tone and structure
- **Assessment angles:** format conventions, register selection, review-language choices

### Language for report and dialogue
- **Key concepts:** perfect and future tense, conjunctions, direct and indirect speech, active and passive voice
- **Skills / outcomes:** report speech accurately and build more complex sentences
- **Assessment angles:** speech conversion, tense sequence, clause-link questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal writing, oral presentation or dramatization, and reading comprehension or language assessment.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 usually expands the range of media and literary texts and introduces more formal writing tasks.
