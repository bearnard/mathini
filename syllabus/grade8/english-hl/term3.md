# Grade 8 — English Home Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Creative writing project
- **Key concepts:** research, drafting, peer review, presentation, audience awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** plan and complete a longer project with evidence of revision and reflection
- **Assessment angles:** project structure, source use, presentation hooks, drafting stages

### Short stories and narrative technique
- **Key concepts:** narrator, sequencing, conflict, climax, characterisation
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse short stories and use narrative technique in own writing
- **Assessment angles:** narrative structure, narrator viewpoint, character evidence

### Essay writing and style
- **Key concepts:** narrative essay, reflective essay, descriptive detail, sentence variety, stylistic devices
- **Skills / outcomes:** craft longer pieces with clearer structure and stronger descriptive control
- **Assessment angles:** introduction choices, paragraph coherence, stylistic device recognition

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Creative project submission and oral presentation, plus a formal writing or language task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
ATPs often use Term 3 for a larger process-writing project supported by reading and oral work.
