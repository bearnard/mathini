# Grade 8 — English Home Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Brochures and visual literacy
- **Key concepts:** brochure format, audience, purpose, layout, skimming, scanning, inference
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse brochures and design an informative or persuasive brochure using the writing process
- **Assessment angles:** layout recognition, purpose questions, audience matching, inference from visuals

### Poetry and close reading
- **Key concepts:** stanza, rhyme, imagery, tone, theme, reading aloud
- **Skills / outcomes:** read poems critically and explain how language choices shape meaning
- **Assessment angles:** poetic device questions, tone shifts, line-reference responses

### Language structures and bias
- **Key concepts:** nouns, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, tenses, punctuation, bias, prejudice, stereotypes
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify language structures and discuss loaded language in texts
- **Assessment angles:** grammar-in-context MCQs, stereotype and bias hooks, sentence correction

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Baseline activities, a formal oral or reading task, and a brochure or other writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Senior Phase English HL still integrates the four skills, but texts become denser and analytical language is expected.
