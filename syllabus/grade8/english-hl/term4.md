# Grade 8 — English Home Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Revision and oral completion
- **Key concepts:** portfolio completion, revision of genres, exam strategies
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate the year’s skill set and complete any remaining oral evidence
- **Assessment angles:** revision drills, mixed-genre questions, oral reflection prompts

### Examination writing
- **Key concepts:** essay, transactional writing, planning, editing, timing
- **Skills / outcomes:** choose suitable text forms and produce accurate responses under exam conditions
- **Assessment angles:** time-management questions, text-type recall, editing under pressure

### Language and literature consolidation
- **Key concepts:** comprehension, literature response, grammar revision, summary
- **Skills / outcomes:** move flexibly between language, literature and writing demands
- **Assessment angles:** integrated paper-style questions, summary practice, grammar revision items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Year-end examination together with oral or project evidence finalised from earlier in the year.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is mainly revision and examination preparation.
