# Grade 8 — Natural Sciences: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Classification of matter and periodic table
- **Key concepts:** elements, compounds, mixtures, metals, non-metals, solutions, periodic-table basics
- **Skills / outcomes:** Classify substances, read simple periodic-table information, and distinguish mixtures from compounds.
- **Assessment angles:** Sorting tasks, particle diagrams, and interpreting element information.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

### Chemical reactions, acids and bases
- **Key concepts:** indicators, neutralisation, physical vs chemical change, evidence of reaction, separation methods
- **Skills / outcomes:** Observe and record reactions, identify acids/bases, and choose suitable methods for separating mixtures.
- **Assessment angles:** Indicator-colour questions, reaction evidence, and apparatus diagrams.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical chemistry task and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Practical work and formal testing form the main SBA evidence for the term.
