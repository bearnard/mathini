# Grade 8 — Natural Sciences: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Photosynthesis and respiration
- **Key concepts:** inputs and outputs, stored food, oxygen, carbon dioxide, energy release, relationship between the two processes
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare photosynthesis and respiration, interpret simple experiments, and explain why both processes matter in ecosystems.
- **Assessment angles:** Comparison tables, labeled diagrams, and short explanation questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Ecosystems and micro-organisms
- **Key concepts:** biotic and abiotic factors, food webs, conservation, bacteria, fungi, viruses, useful and harmful microbes
- **Skills / outcomes:** Trace energy flow, classify micro-organisms, and discuss human impact on ecosystem balance.
- **Assessment angles:** Food-web disruption items, classification questions, and scenario-based conservation prompts.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical investigation and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase school-based assessment through practical tasks and written tests.
