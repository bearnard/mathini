# Grade 8 — Natural Sciences: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Forces, motion and measurement
- **Key concepts:** speed, distance, time, friction, gravitational force, magnetic force, electrostatic force
- **Skills / outcomes:** Use simple formulas, interpret motion situations, and investigate the effects of friction.
- **Assessment angles:** Speed calculations, force identification, and simple graph or table questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Forms and transfer of energy
- **Key concepts:** heat, light, sound, chemical, electrical, mechanical energy, conduction, convection, radiation, conservation of energy
- **Skills / outcomes:** Trace energy transfers, compare heat-transfer processes, and reason about everyday energy systems.
- **Assessment angles:** Energy-chain diagrams, heat-transfer comparisons, and applied scenario questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical task and end-of-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Term tasks contribute to SBA, with the year-end examination carrying major promotional weight.
