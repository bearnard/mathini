# Grade 8 — Natural Sciences: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Biogeochemical cycles
- **Key concepts:** water cycle, carbon cycle, nitrogen cycle, recycling of matter, decomposers, atmosphere interactions
- **Skills / outcomes:** Track matter through systems, explain the role of living things in cycles, and interpret cycle diagrams.
- **Assessment angles:** Cycle labels, cause-and-effect questions, and comparing the main stages of each cycle.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Solar system and gravity
- **Key concepts:** Sun, planets, moons, comets, asteroids, gravity, scale of the solar system
- **Skills / outcomes:** Order solar-system objects, compare planetary properties, and explain the role of gravity.
- **Assessment angles:** Labeled diagrams, matching objects to descriptions, and short explanation questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Planet Earth & Beyond

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Research task or investigation and mid-year test.
- **SBA weighting:** School-based tasks contribute to the term mark; exam-style questions increase toward mid-year.
