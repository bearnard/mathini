**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 31–40

## Topics

### Transport and trade
- **Key concepts:** reasons for trade, modes of transport, South African harbours, imports and exports, road versus rail, urban commuter transport
- **Skills / outcomes:** mapwork, atlas use, graph reading, case-study discussion, short explanatory writing
- **Assessment angles:** map labels, graph interpretation, case-study questions, short paragraphs and data-response items
- **Historical period / geographical region:** South Africa and the world

- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in CAPS; tasks contribute to the annual Social Sciences programme.
