# Grade 8 — Mathematics: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Functions and Relationships
- **Key concepts:** tables, rules and representations of relationships between variables
- **Skills / outcomes:** read, interpret and generate rules from numeric or contextual data
- **Assessment angles:** finding rules, matching tables to expressions, constant vs changing relationships
- **Approximate time:** About 6 hours

### Algebraic Equations
- **Key concepts:** linear equations revisited for fluency and application
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve equations accurately and explain each transformation used
- **Assessment angles:** balancing errors, substitution checks, equation wording
- **Approximate time:** About 3 hours

### Graphs
- **Key concepts:** plotting ordered pairs and interpreting linear and non-linear graphs
- **Skills / outcomes:** draw graphs from tables or formulae and describe trends such as increasing, decreasing or constant
- **Assessment angles:** coordinate plotting, identifying intercepts informally, trend descriptions
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Transformation Geometry
- **Key concepts:** translations, reflections, rotations and enlargements on the coordinate plane
- **Skills / outcomes:** describe image movement precisely and use coordinates to verify transformations
- **Assessment angles:** coordinate-image questions, enlargement factor, congruent vs similar
- **Approximate time:** About 6 hours

### Geometry of 3D Objects
- **Key concepts:** properties, classification, nets and comparison of 3D solids
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse solids by faces, edges and vertices and connect models to nets
- **Assessment angles:** net matching, solid classification, Euler-style reasoning
- **Approximate time:** About 7 hours

### Probability
- **Key concepts:** equally likely outcomes, simple experiments and relative frequency
- **Skills / outcomes:** determine probabilities from sample spaces and compare theory with experiment
- **Assessment angles:** listing outcomes, relative-frequency comparison, simple event language
- **Approximate time:** About 4.5 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment, investigation and end-of-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
