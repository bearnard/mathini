# Grade 8 — Mathematics: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Algebraic Expressions
- **Key concepts:** expanding, factorising and simplifying expressions with increasing symbolic fluency
- **Skills / outcomes:** manipulate algebraic forms accurately and explain steps clearly
- **Assessment angles:** sign errors, bracket expansion, factorisation recognition
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Algebraic Equations
- **Key concepts:** solving linear equations and related symbolic problems
- **Skills / outcomes:** balance equations, isolate variables and check solutions by substitution
- **Assessment angles:** inverse-operation mistakes, checking solutions, word-to-equation translation
- **Approximate time:** About 3 hours

### Construction of Geometric Figures
- **Key concepts:** accurate constructions using standard instruments and geometric conventions
- **Skills / outcomes:** construct triangles, angles and quadrilaterals from given data
- **Assessment angles:** construction accuracy, instrument use, following constraints precisely
- **Approximate time:** About 8 hours

### Geometry of 2D Shapes and Straight Lines
- **Key concepts:** properties of polygons, angle relationships, parallel lines and geometric reasoning
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve missing-angle problems and justify statements using shape properties
- **Assessment angles:** parallel-line angle facts, interior-angle totals, geometric vocabulary
- **Approximate time:** About 17 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** One formal test, one investigation and the mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
