# Grade 8 — Mathematics: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Whole Numbers and Integers
- **Key concepts:** operations with whole numbers and integers, properties of number operations, ordering and place value
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate efficiently, apply sign rules and solve contextual problems involving positive and negative numbers
- **Assessment angles:** integer sign errors, order of operations, contextual gains/losses and temperature-style problems
- **Approximate time:** About 15 hours

### Exponents
- **Key concepts:** laws of exponents, squares, cubes and equivalent exponential forms
- **Skills / outcomes:** simplify exponential expressions and solve straightforward exponent-based problems
- **Assessment angles:** negative exponent misconceptions, simplifying powers, linking notation to value
- **Approximate time:** About 9 hours

### Numeric and Geometric Patterns; Functions and Relationships
- **Key concepts:** pattern rules, tables, input-output values and relation descriptions
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify rules, represent relationships in more than one way and justify pattern growth
- **Assessment angles:** missing-value questions, table-rule translation, pattern generalisation
- **Approximate time:** About 7.5 hours

### Algebraic Expressions and Equations
- **Key concepts:** like terms, substitution, simplification, expansion and solving linear equations
- **Skills / outcomes:** translate words into algebra, simplify correctly and solve equations step by step
- **Assessment angles:** collecting like terms, distributive law, substitution, equation-solving errors
- **Approximate time:** About 7.5 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** One formal test and one assignment.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
