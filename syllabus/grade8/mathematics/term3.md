# Grade 8 — Mathematics: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Common and Decimal Fractions
- **Key concepts:** operations with fractions and decimals, conversions and context-based calculations
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate accurately and switch flexibly between fraction and decimal forms
- **Assessment angles:** fraction-decimal conversions, operation errors, interpreting calculator output
- **Approximate time:** About 13 hours

### Theorem of Pythagoras
- **Key concepts:** right-angled triangles, squares on sides and unknown lengths
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify when Pythagoras applies and solve for unknown sides
- **Assessment angles:** choosing the hypotenuse, square-root accuracy, real-life length problems
- **Approximate time:** About 5 hours

### Area, Perimeter, Surface Area and Volume
- **Key concepts:** formulae for 2D measures and 3D measures of common solids
- **Skills / outcomes:** select correct formulae, substitute values correctly and interpret answers with units
- **Assessment angles:** unit conversion, surface area vs volume, composite measurement tasks
- **Approximate time:** About 10 hours

### Data Handling
- **Key concepts:** collecting, organising, representing and interpreting grouped and ungrouped data
- **Skills / outcomes:** draw graphs, summarise data and critique what different representations show or hide
- **Assessment angles:** histograms vs bar graphs, central tendency, graph-scale interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 10.5 hours

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** One formal test plus an assignment or project task.
- **SBA weighting:** Senior Phase CAPS: SBA 40% across the year and end-of-year examination 60%.
