# Grade 8 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Creative writing project
- **Key concepts:** research, planning, drafting, presentation, response to themes
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete a sustained creative project with evidence of revision and audience awareness
- **Assessment angles:** project planning, source use, presentation criteria

### Short stories and drama
- **Key concepts:** short stories, drama, continued poetry, literary response
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret literary features in shorter set texts and scenes
- **Assessment angles:** speaker identification, conflict and theme prompts, short contextual questions

### Transactional writing and language refinement
- **Key concepts:** formal letters, descriptive and narrative writing, conjunctions, punctuation, reported speech
- **Skills / outcomes:** move between creative and practical writing with better grammatical control
- **Assessment angles:** format recall, conjunction choices, editing items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project submission and oral component, plus a formal language or writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 3 often carries the largest process-writing task of the year.
