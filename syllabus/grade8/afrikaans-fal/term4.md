# Grade 8 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Revision and exam preparation
- **Key concepts:** practice papers, listening and speaking review, summary, comprehension
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply the year’s reading and writing strategies in exam-style contexts
- **Assessment angles:** summary items, comprehension strategy questions, listening recall

### Review and report writing
- **Key concepts:** reports, summaries, reviews, format control
- **Skills / outcomes:** write concise, well-structured transactional pieces for known purposes
- **Assessment angles:** text-type conventions, selecting relevant detail, editing

### Language consolidation
- **Key concepts:** revision of all major grammar points, punctuation, sentence control
- **Skills / outcomes:** correct common errors and prepare for the final examination
- **Assessment angles:** MCQ grammar, error correction, sentence improvement

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of project or oral evidence from earlier terms.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 consolidates visual literacy, literature and writing for end-of-year assessment.
