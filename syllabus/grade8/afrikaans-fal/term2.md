# Grade 8 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Electronic communication and reviews
- **Key concepts:** emails, reviews, formality, purpose, audience
- **Skills / outcomes:** write shorter transactional texts with the correct tone and structure
- **Assessment angles:** email layout, review language, register questions

### Folklore or youth novel study
- **Key concepts:** plot, character, conflict, magazine article, dialogue on social issues
- **Skills / outcomes:** read longer prose texts and discuss character and conflict more fully
- **Assessment angles:** plot sequencing, character evidence, dialogue conventions

### Grammar for reporting and coherence
- **Key concepts:** sentence structure, direct and indirect speech, concord, pronouns
- **Skills / outcomes:** report information more accurately and link ideas in connected sentences
- **Assessment angles:** speech conversion, pronoun choice, concord items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Controlled test, oral presentation and a formal writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 generally combines literary reading with more formal communication tasks and mid-year assessment.
