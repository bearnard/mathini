# Grade 8 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 8–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual literacy and advertisements
- **Key concepts:** flyers, advertisements, layout, emotive language, target audience
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse visual texts and explain how language and design persuade readers
- **Assessment angles:** audience-purpose matching, slogan hooks, layout questions

### Poetry and early literature study
- **Key concepts:** poetry structure, rhyme, stanza, theme, introductory literary response
- **Skills / outcomes:** read poems more closely and begin discussing literary effect in Afrikaans
- **Assessment angles:** imagery, tone, line-reference items

### Narrative writing and grammar
- **Key concepts:** narrative or reflective essay, nouns, adjectives, interjections, tenses, punctuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** write longer pieces and improve control of core language structures
- **Assessment angles:** essay openings, tense shifts, punctuation and editing questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal writing, oral work and comprehension or language activities, often after a baseline check.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Senior Phase Afrikaans FAL increases text complexity while keeping language structures closely tied to reading and writing tasks.
