# Grade 8 — Technology: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Making and evaluating prototypes
- **Key concepts:** prototyping, measuring, joining, tool use, safe working practice, evaluation criteria
- **Skills / outcomes:** Construct a model, test and improve a prototype, and record the making process clearly.
- **Assessment angles:** Process sequencing, tool-choice items, and evaluating a finished product against criteria.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

### Simple machines revision
- **Key concepts:** levers, inclined planes, wedges, wheel and axle, force multiplication
- **Skills / outcomes:** Relate simple machines to everyday devices and explain how they reduce effort.
- **Assessment angles:** Identifying machine types and explaining how movement or force changes.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PAT 2 make/evaluate component and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** The practical prototype and written evaluation are key pieces of SBA evidence.
