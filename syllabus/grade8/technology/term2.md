# Grade 8 — Technology: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Electrical systems and circuit safety
- **Key concepts:** series and parallel circuits, cells, switches, conductors, fuses, circuit breakers, earthing
- **Skills / outcomes:** Build simple circuits, compare layouts, and apply safe electrical practices.
- **Assessment angles:** Circuit diagrams, safety scenarios, and component-identification items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

### Logic and PAT design work
- **Key concepts:** AND logic, OR logic, truth tables, control systems, panic-button design scenario
- **Skills / outcomes:** Represent logic states, draw design ideas, and communicate a workable control solution.
- **Assessment angles:** Truth tables, system-input questions, and labeled design drawings.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PAT 2 investigation/design task and a term test.
- **SBA weighting:** PATs dominate the practical SBA evidence, supported by theory questions.
