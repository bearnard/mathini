# Grade 8 — Technology: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** Senior
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Mechanical systems and motion
- **Key concepts:** wedge, wheel and axle, gears, cams, cranks, motion change, mechanical advantage
- **Skills / outcomes:** Identify mechanisms, explain how motion changes, and sketch solutions using suitable systems.
- **Assessment angles:** Mechanism diagrams, application questions, and reasoning about mechanical advantage.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

### Structures and design brief
- **Key concepts:** shell, frame and solid structures, tension, compression, dead load, live load, IDMEC
- **Skills / outcomes:** Investigate a structural problem, produce design sketches, and evaluate stability and strength.
- **Assessment angles:** Force arrows, material-choice questions, and interpreting a design brief.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PAT 1 (investigate and design) plus a formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** PATs and tests form the internal technology mark through the year.
