# Grade 8 — Technology: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** Senior Phase (Gr 7–9)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Indigenous technology and processing
- **Key concepts:** preserving metals, galvanising, painting, electroplating, food preservation, local knowledge systems
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare indigenous and modern methods, justify process choices, and discuss sustainability.
- **Assessment angles:** Process comparisons, corrosion-prevention questions, and short case studies.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

### Systems consolidation and exam preparation
- **Key concepts:** mechanical, structural and electrical vocabulary, design communication, revision of PAT concepts
- **Skills / outcomes:** Integrate knowledge across terms, interpret mixed diagrams, and prepare concise exam answers.
- **Assessment angles:** Mixed-system diagrams, short structured responses, and PAT reflection prompts.
- **Knowledge strand:** Engineering & Technology

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** End-of-year examination and PAT evidence consolidation.
- **SBA weighting:** Internal PAT evidence remains important, while the year-end examination adds promotional weight.
