# Grade 12 — Physical Sciences: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Electric circuits and internal resistance
- **Key concepts:** EMF, internal resistance, terminal potential difference, complex series/parallel circuits
- **Skills / outcomes:** Solve circuit problems with internal resistance and explain why terminal voltage changes under load.
- **Assessment angles:** Circuit calculations, comparison of loaded vs unloaded cells, and resistor-network questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Electrodynamics and optical phenomena
- **Key concepts:** AC and DC generators, motors, electromagnetic induction, RMS values, photoelectric effect, absorption and emission spectra
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare generators and motors, apply electromagnetic principles, and explain evidence for the particle nature of light.
- **Assessment angles:** Machine diagrams, RMS calculations, and photoelectric-effect questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Electrochemical reactions
- **Key concepts:** galvanic cells, electrolytic cells, anode, cathode, salt bridge, electroplating, refining of copper
- **Skills / outcomes:** Interpret electrochemical-cell diagrams, calculate cell EMF, and explain industrial applications.
- **Assessment angles:** Cell labeling, half-reaction reasoning, and electrolysis applications.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** SBA practical and trial examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET sciences.
