# Grade 12 — Physical Sciences: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–4

## Topics

### Paper 1 consolidation
- **Key concepts:** mechanics, waves, electricity, electrodynamics, graph skills, formula fluency
- **Skills / outcomes:** Review core Physics concepts, practise timed calculations, and improve graph interpretation.
- **Assessment angles:** Mixed mechanics/electricity questions and exam-style graph analysis.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Paper 2 consolidation
- **Key concepts:** organic chemistry, reaction rates, equilibrium, acids and bases, electrochemistry, structured responses
- **Skills / outcomes:** Integrate chemistry topics and present concise, accurate explanations in examination conditions.
- **Assessment angles:** Mixed chemistry calculations, data-response items, and extended reasoning questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** NSC final examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 promotion is typically 25% SBA / 75% external examination.
