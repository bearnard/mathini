# Grade 12 — Physical Sciences: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Momentum and impulse
- **Key concepts:** momentum, impulse-momentum theorem, conservation of linear momentum, elastic and inelastic collisions
- **Skills / outcomes:** Solve one-dimensional collision problems and connect impulse ideas to safety applications.
- **Assessment angles:** Collision calculations, momentum-vector reasoning, and impulse interpretation.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Vertical projectile motion
- **Key concepts:** free fall, equations of motion, displacement, velocity-time graphs, acceleration-time graphs
- **Skills / outcomes:** Use kinematics equations, sketch motion graphs, and describe upward/downward motion correctly.
- **Assessment angles:** Projectile calculations, graph interpretation, and sign-convention questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Organic molecules
- **Key concepts:** functional groups, homologous series, isomers, IUPAC naming, physical properties, addition, substitution, elimination and esterification reactions
- **Skills / outcomes:** Name compounds, identify isomers, relate structure to properties, and predict reaction type.
- **Assessment angles:** Naming questions, structural formulae, and reaction-type identification.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** SBA practical and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET sciences.
