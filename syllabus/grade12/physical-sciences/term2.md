# Grade 12 — Physical Sciences: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Work, energy and power
- **Key concepts:** work done, work-energy theorem, conservation of mechanical energy, average power, power from force and velocity
- **Skills / outcomes:** Calculate work and power, connect energy theorems to motion, and solve multi-step mechanics problems.
- **Assessment angles:** Work-energy calculations, sign-of-work questions, and power comparisons.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Doppler effect
- **Key concepts:** change in pitch, moving source, moving observer, ultrasound uses, red shift, expanding universe
- **Skills / outcomes:** Explain pitch changes and connect Doppler shifts to medical and astronomy applications.
- **Assessment angles:** Pitch scenarios, interpreting source/listener motion, and red-shift explanation items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Reaction rates, equilibrium and acids/bases
- **Key concepts:** collision theory, catalysts, Le Chatelier’s principle, equilibrium constant, Arrhenius and Brønsted-Lowry theories, pH, hydrolysis
- **Skills / outcomes:** Interpret energy profiles, predict equilibrium shifts, and solve acid/base calculation questions.
- **Assessment angles:** Equilibrium tables, pH calculations, and factor-affecting-rate questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** SBA practical and June examination or control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET sciences.
