## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Research project plus controlled test or June examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
Term 2 mixes interpretation-heavy topics with technical calculation work. Learners should link internal control, ethics and business decision-making to the figures they compute. Cost accounting (manufacturing) is CAPS Term 3 and VAT is CAPS Term 2.
