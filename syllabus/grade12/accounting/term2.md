# Grade 12 — Accounting: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Inventory, fixed assets and internal control
- **Key concepts:** inventory valuation, periodic and perpetual systems, fixed assets, asset age and replacement rate, internal control, ethics
- **Skills / outcomes:** value stock, interpret asset-management data and comment on control weaknesses and ethical risks
- **Assessment angles:** valuation calculations, stock-system comparisons, asset-interpretation comments, control recommendations

### Manufacturing costs and reconciliations
- **Key concepts:** cost accounting, production cost statement, unit cost, reconciliations, bank reconciliation, creditors reconciliation
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare manufacturing calculations and analyse reconciliation information accurately
- **Assessment angles:** cost calculations, break-even or unit-cost reasoning, reconciliation adjustments, June-exam style mixed questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Research project plus controlled test or June examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
Term 2 mixes interpretation-heavy topics with technical calculation work. Learners should link internal control, ethics and business decision-making to the figures they compute.
