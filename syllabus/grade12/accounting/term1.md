# Grade 12 — Accounting: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Companies: unique transactions and reporting
- **Key concepts:** share capital, share issues, buy-backs, directors, shareholders, final accounts, statement of comprehensive income, statement of financial position, cash flow statement, audit report
- **Skills / outcomes:** record company-specific transactions and prepare detailed financial statements with year-end adjustments
- **Assessment angles:** share transaction entries, statement layouts, cash-flow sections, audit-report interpretation

### Analysis, interpretation and ethics
- **Key concepts:** published financial statements, financial indicators, returns, liquidity, gearing, governance, ethics, GAAP
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate and interpret company indicators and comment on ethical and governance issues using evidence from statements
- **Assessment angles:** ratio calculations, recommendation questions, governance and ethics scenarios, comment-on-figures prompts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Written report and control test on company accounting and interpretation.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
Grade 12 Accounting is dominated by company reporting. Learners should connect statement preparation, cash flow and published-statement interpretation from the start of the year.
