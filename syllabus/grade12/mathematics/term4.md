# Grade 12 — Mathematics: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Paper 1 Consolidation
- **Key concepts:** patterns and sequences, finance, functions, algebra, calculus and probability
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise high-frequency exam methods and practise selecting efficient approaches under time pressure
- **Assessment angles:** common NSC routines, formula selection, calculator discipline, multi-step algebra/calculus questions
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Paper 2 Consolidation and NSC Preparation
- **Key concepts:** Euclidean geometry, analytical geometry, statistics and trigonometry
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine theorem recall, proof layout and problem-solving stamina for the final external exam
- **Assessment angles:** proof-writing quality, graph-reading accuracy, coordinate reasoning and full-paper strategy
- **Approximate time:** About 5 weeks including final examinations

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final NSC examination; no additional internal formal task beyond revision and exam readiness.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
