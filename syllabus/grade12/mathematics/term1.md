# Grade 12 — Mathematics: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Sequences, Series and Sigma Notation
- **Key concepts:** arithmetic and geometric sequences, sums of series, infinite geometric series and sigma notation
- **Skills / outcomes:** derive or apply formulas, evaluate sigma notation and solve contextual sequence problems
- **Assessment angles:** nth-term and sum formula choice, infinite-series conditions, sigma notation fluency
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Functions and Inverse Functions
- **Key concepts:** definition of a function, one-to-one relations, inverse functions and graph characteristics
- **Skills / outcomes:** restrict domains where needed, determine inverses and sketch graphs with correct features
- **Assessment angles:** inverse vs reciprocal confusion, domain restrictions, graph symmetry about y=x
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Exponential and Logarithmic Functions
- **Key concepts:** logarithm definition, logarithmic graphs and logarithms in financial contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret logarithms conceptually and use them to solve growth or decay questions
- **Assessment angles:** base interpretation, log graph features, solving for time in finance problems
- **Approximate time:** About 1 week

### Finance, Growth and Decay
- **Key concepts:** present and future value annuities, investment and loan analysis and long-term financial decision-making
- **Skills / outcomes:** use finance formulas confidently and compare options critically
- **Assessment angles:** annuity formula choice, loan vs investment reasoning, timeline analysis
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Trigonometry
- **Key concepts:** compound-angle identities, double-angle identities and trig equations
- **Skills / outcomes:** derive identities, simplify expressions and solve equations with formal working
- **Assessment angles:** identity derivation, proving trig statements, general solution errors
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Investigation or project, an assignment or test, and a second formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
