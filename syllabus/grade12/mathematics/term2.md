# Grade 12 — Mathematics: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Trigonometry in Two and Three Dimensions
- **Key concepts:** non-routine trig problems in 2D and 3D settings and extended trig proofs
- **Skills / outcomes:** model diagrams carefully, choose appropriate trig relationships and justify results
- **Assessment angles:** 3D sketches, angle interpretation, multistep trig proofs, choosing relevant ratios or rules
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Polynomial Functions
- **Key concepts:** factor theorem, remainder theorem and factorisation of cubic polynomials
- **Skills / outcomes:** find factors and roots of higher-degree polynomials and connect them to graph behaviour
- **Assessment angles:** factor theorem use, synthetic reasoning, cubic roots and graph links
- **Approximate time:** About 1 week

### Differential Calculus
- **Key concepts:** first principles, derivative rules, tangents, stationary points, inflection points, optimisation and rate of change
- **Skills / outcomes:** differentiate accurately, analyse cubic graphs and solve optimisation problems
- **Assessment angles:** first-principles setup, derivative-rule fluency, stationary-point classification, optimisation wording
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Analytical Geometry of the Circle
- **Key concepts:** equation of a circle, tangents to circles and coordinate geometry involving chords and diameters
- **Skills / outcomes:** derive circle equations, determine tangent equations and solve coordinate proof-style questions
- **Assessment angles:** centre-radius form, tangent gradient, diameter midpoint reasoning, circle-line intersections
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### June Examination Preparation
- **Key concepts:** integrated practice across Paper 1 and Paper 2 content covered to date
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise strategically and manage full internal examination papers
- **Assessment angles:** question selection, algebra-calculus integration, structured paper practice
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks including exams

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal assignment and the mid-year/internal examination.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
