# Grade 12 — Business Studies: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Human rights, inclusivity and responsibility
- **Key concepts:** human rights, inclusivity, environmental issues, social responsibility, CSR, CSI
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate responsible business behaviour and recommend responses to social and environmental issues
- **Assessment angles:** rights-and-inclusivity scenarios, CSR vs CSI comparisons, recommendation questions

### Presentation, data response and ownership
- **Key concepts:** presentation of business information, data response, forms of ownership, ownership case study or project
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret business information, respond to source-based questions and compare ownership forms at Grade 12 level
- **Assessment angles:** data-response items, ownership comparisons, project-style investigation questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Research project and trial-examination preparation/assessment.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
Term 3 is a key consolidation term before prelims. Learners should be comfortable moving from social-responsibility theory into data-based responses and ownership analysis.
