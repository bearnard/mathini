# Grade 12 — Business Studies: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Business sectors, quality and leadership
- **Key concepts:** business sectors and environments, quality concepts, quality circles, management, leadership styles, leadership theories
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse sector pressures, evaluate quality management and compare leadership and management approaches
- **Assessment angles:** quality-control scenarios, leadership-style applications, business-environment comparisons

### Investment, insurance and conflict management
- **Key concepts:** JSE and investment options, insurance concepts, team performance, conflict management
- **Skills / outcomes:** advise on investments and insurance and recommend ways to improve team performance and resolve conflict
- **Assessment angles:** investment comparisons, insurance underinsurance/overinsurance hooks, conflict-scenario responses

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Presentation, research-project issue and controlled test or June examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
Term 2 is dense and very examinable. Learners should practise distinguishing similar concepts such as leadership styles, quality tools and investment products.
