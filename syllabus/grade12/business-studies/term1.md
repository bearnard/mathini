# Grade 12 — Business Studies: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Legislation, HR and ethics
- **Key concepts:** Skills Development Act, Employment Equity Act, BBBEE, recruitment, selection, salary determination, professionalism, ethical behaviour
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply legislation to workplace scenarios, explain HR processes and judge ethical conduct in business situations
- **Assessment angles:** legislation scenario questions, HR procedures, ethics and professionalism case studies

### Creative thinking and business strategies
- **Key concepts:** problem solving, decision making, macro environment, business strategies, Porter’s Five Forces, strategic planning
- **Skills / outcomes:** use creative-thinking tools and apply strategic analysis to macro-environmental challenges
- **Assessment angles:** strategy application, Five-Forces analysis, recommendation and justification questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Case study and control test on legislation, HR and strategy content.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
Grade 12 Business Studies expects learners to apply legislation and strategy, not just define them. Short structured answers and longer explanations both matter.
