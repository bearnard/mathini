# Grade 12 — Economics: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 21–30

## Topics

### Growth, performance and contemporary issues
- **Key concepts:** growth and development policies, industrial development, economic indicators, inflation, tourism, environmental sustainability, current issues, preliminary-exam revision
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret graphs and tables, apply calculations, explain policy choices, analyse case studies, write structured responses
- **Assessment angles:** data-response questions, graph interpretation, calculations, policy paragraphs and short essays

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Case study, controlled test and preliminary examination.
- **SBA weighting:** 25% Programme of Assessment / 75% examinations in FET Economics.
