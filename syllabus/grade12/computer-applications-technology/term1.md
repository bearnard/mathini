# Grade 12 — Computer Applications Technology: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Systems technologies and social implications
- **Key concepts:** general concepts, hardware, software, storage, accessibility, environmental issues, user-centred design, system requirements
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate system choices, explain hardware/software trade-offs and discuss social implications of technology use
- **Assessment angles:** theory paper short questions, hardware-selection scenarios, digital-ethics items

### Advanced databases, spreadsheets and word processing
- **Key concepts:** database reports and queries, named ranges, nested IF, lookup functions, complex formulas, file management, professional documents, PAT information management
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve advanced practical tasks across applications and begin PAT phase 1 with structured information collection
- **Assessment angles:** database output questions, spreadsheet debugging, document-formatting corrections and PAT planning prompts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Theory test, practical test and PAT phase 1.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 CAT commonly uses 25% SBA + 25% PAT + 50% examinations.

## Notes
Grade 12 CAT assumes strong practical fluency. PAT planning begins early, and application integration is expected rather than optional.
