# Grade 12 — Life Orientation: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Social and environmental responsibility
- **Key concepts:** social issues, environmental health, intervention strategies, ethical responsibility, legislation awareness
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate current issues and design realistic responses that promote wellbeing and justice
- **Assessment angles:** issue-analysis tasks, intervention planning, structured source questions

### Community and social action
- **Key concepts:** volunteerism, advocacy, support services, leadership, accountability
- **Skills / outcomes:** assess how communities respond to challenges and identify where young adults can contribute meaningfully
- **Assessment angles:** case studies, action plans, reflection prompts

### Risk management and support
- **Key concepts:** violence, poverty, substance abuse, trauma, coping and referral
- **Skills / outcomes:** connect social problems to support systems and responsible intervention choices
- **Assessment angles:** problem-solution questions, referral-network mapping, discussion tasks

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** game participation, fitness goals, stress relief, active lifestyle habits
- **Skills / outcomes:** use physical activity as part of healthy stress management and sustained wellbeing
- **Assessment angles:** fitness reflections, participation rubrics, goal reviews

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A project, investigation or case study, a written task or test, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** FET Life Orientation uses 75% SBA and 25% PET across the year.
