# Grade 12 — Life Orientation: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Careers and career choices
- **Key concepts:** post-school pathways, admissions, bursaries, entrepreneurship, lifelong learning
- **Skills / outcomes:** finalise realistic transition plans for study, training, work or entrepreneurship after school
- **Assessment angles:** planning templates, opportunity comparison, oral presentation prompts

### World of work readiness
- **Key concepts:** CV refinement, interviews, workplace ethics, contracts, labour rights
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare job-seeking documents and explain key expectations in the workplace
- **Assessment angles:** mock interview tasks, document-editing items, labour-rights scenarios

### Transition and personal planning
- **Key concepts:** financial awareness, independence, support networks, resilience, future goals
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate readiness for adult responsibilities and identify supports for the first year after school
- **Assessment angles:** reflection paragraphs, action plans, decision-making scenarios

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** lifetime fitness, recreation choices, health routines, participation evidence
- **Skills / outcomes:** set sustainable activity goals for adult life and review the role of exercise in ongoing wellbeing
- **Assessment angles:** goal-setting tasks, participation records, healthy-lifestyle reflections

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A career portfolio or application task, final SBA moderation, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** FET Life Orientation uses 75% SBA and 25% PET across the year.
