# Grade 12 — Geography: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Climate, weather and geomorphology
- **Key concepts:** mid-latitude cyclones, tropical cyclones, anticyclones, valley climates, urban climates, drainage systems, fluvial processes, catchment management, mapwork
- **Skills / outcomes:** mapwork, GIS awareness, graph and data interpretation, fieldwork or case-study analysis
- **Assessment angles:** mapwork items, data-response questions, climate/settlement/resource essays, interpretation of graphs and images
- **Historical period / geographical region:** South Africa and the southern African region

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Data-handling task and test.
- **SBA weighting:** 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Geography.
