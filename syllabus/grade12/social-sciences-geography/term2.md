# Grade 12 — Geography: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 11–20

## Topics

### Rural and urban settlement geography
- **Key concepts:** site and situation, rural settlement patterns, rural depopulation, urban hierarchy, urban structure, informal settlements, GIS data
- **Skills / outcomes:** mapwork, GIS awareness, graph and data interpretation, fieldwork or case-study analysis
- **Assessment angles:** mapwork items, data-response questions, climate/settlement/resource essays, interpretation of graphs and images
- **Historical period / geographical region:** South Africa with global settlement examples

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Mapwork task and mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Geography.
