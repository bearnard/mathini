# Grade 12 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** final oral evidence, revision, listening recall, fluency maintenance
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate any remaining oral evidence and maintain fluency during final revision
- **Assessment angles:** oral checklists, listening quizzes, short prepared responses

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** final revision across prescribed literature, comprehension, summary and visual texts
- **Skills / outcomes:** review all key text types and exam strategies for the final assessment cycle
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-paper revision prompts, summary tasks, literature recall questions

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** final exam essays, transactional recall, editing, time management
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete writing tasks under pressure and apply planning and proofreading routines efficiently
- **Assessment angles:** timed-writing tasks, format recall, proofreading questions

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** revision of all major grammar points, editing, punctuation, style control
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate Paper 1 language knowledge for final examinations and post-school communication
- **Assessment angles:** grammar MCQs, sentence correction, editing tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of any remaining oral evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
