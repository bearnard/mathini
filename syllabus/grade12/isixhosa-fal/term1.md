# Grade 12 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** prepared speech, listening comprehension, discussion, oral response, register control
- **Skills / outcomes:** strengthen speaking and listening routines used in the Grade 12 SBA programme and classroom revision
- **Assessment angles:** speech rubrics, listening recall, discussion questions

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** prescribed literature, visual literacy, comprehension, summary, critical reading
- **Skills / outcomes:** read literary and non-literary texts analytically and revise the demands of Paper 1 and Paper 2
- **Assessment angles:** summary questions, contextual prompts, visual-text analysis

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** essay planning, reflective or narrative writing, transactional texts, drafting
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce exam-ready longer and shorter pieces with deliberate planning and editing
- **Assessment angles:** essay choice tasks, format features, paragraph development

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** advanced editing, concord, punctuation, style, vocabulary in context
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine accuracy and style in language-in-context tasks linked to examination formats
- **Assessment angles:** editing items, style questions, sentence correction

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Baseline or diagnostic work, a writing task, oral activity and a language or comprehension test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
