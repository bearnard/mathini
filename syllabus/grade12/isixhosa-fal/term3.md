# Grade 12 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** debate, prepared speech, seminar discussion, oral exam preparation
- **Skills / outcomes:** present sustained arguments and respond fluently to issues raised by literature and society
- **Assessment angles:** debate rubrics, oral evaluation tasks, discussion prompts

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** poetry, drama, short stories, trial-exam revision, contextual analysis
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret prescribed texts with examination-level precision and support answers with textual evidence
- **Assessment angles:** quotation prompts, contextual questions, literature revision tasks

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** argumentative or discursive essay, formal article, project or portfolio evidence, exam practice
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine extended and transactional writing for trial examinations and final revision
- **Assessment angles:** argument structure, format recall, timed writing rubrics

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** exam editing, style shifts, vocabulary refinement, punctuation, sentence control
- **Skills / outcomes:** polish language accuracy and stylistic control ahead of major assessments
- **Assessment angles:** editing items, vocabulary questions, sentence-improvement tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project submission and oral component, plus a formal language or writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
