# Grade 12 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** interviews, oral presentations, listening for attitude, formal responses
- **Skills / outcomes:** respond critically to spoken texts and maintain appropriate register in oral tasks
- **Assessment angles:** oral rubrics, listening notes, attitude and bias prompts

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** novel or drama study, media texts, perspective, theme, critical literacy
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare viewpoints across prescribed and media texts and justify interpretations with evidence
- **Assessment angles:** theme prompts, source comparison, character evidence

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** formal letter, review, article, speech, transactional precision
- **Skills / outcomes:** write formal pieces with clearer tone, organisation and audience awareness
- **Assessment angles:** format checks, tone questions, editing tasks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** connectors, passive voice, direct and indirect speech, cohesion, editing
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve coherence and control of more complex sentence patterns in context
- **Assessment angles:** connector items, speech conversion, proofreading tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Controlled test, oral presentation and a formal writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
