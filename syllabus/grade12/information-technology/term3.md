# Grade 12 — Information Technology: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Social implications and emerging technologies
- **Key concepts:** privacy, ethics, environmental impact, cyber security, AI, remote access, societal impact of digital systems
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate technology choices critically and write evidence-based responses to social-implication scenarios
- **Assessment angles:** essay-style theory prompts, ethical dilemmas, security recommendations

### Integrated solution development and PAT completion
- **Key concepts:** advanced OOP, advanced SQL, solution integration, testing, documentation, prelim preparation
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete substantial practical solutions and consolidate across the whole Grade 12 practical syllabus
- **Assessment angles:** integrated coding tasks, multi-part database questions, prelim-style paper practice

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PAT completion and preliminary practical/theory examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 IT commonly uses 25% SBA + 25% PAT + 50% NSC examinations.

## Notes
Term 3 is the major final consolidation phase before the NSC. Learners should expect highly integrated questions that mix theory and practical reasoning.
