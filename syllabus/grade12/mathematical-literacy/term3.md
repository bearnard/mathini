# Grade 12 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Finance: Taxation and Exchange Rates
- **Key concepts:** tax calculations and currency conversion in realistic scenarios
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret financial information and justify decisions involving local and foreign currency values
- **Assessment angles:** exchange-rate direction, tax calculation sequence, choosing the better option
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Maps, Plans and Models
- **Key concepts:** scale drawings, plans and models used for interpretation and problem solving
- **Skills / outcomes:** answer spatial questions using plans and models and explain reasoning clearly
- **Assessment angles:** scale, plan-view interpretation, model comparison, extracting dimensions
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Probability and Trial Preparation
- **Key concepts:** probability in real contexts plus integrated practice for the trial examination
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve probability questions and combine multiple topic areas in exam-style tasks
- **Assessment angles:** sample spaces, contextual risk questions, integrated case studies
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks including trial preparation

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Control test and the trial/internal examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
