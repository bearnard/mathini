# Grade 12 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Revision and final exam window

## Topics

### Integrated Revision
- **Key concepts:** full-paper revision across finance, measurement, maps, data handling and probability
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify weak areas, refine exam technique and apply Mathematical Literacy across unfamiliar but realistic contexts
- **Assessment angles:** case-study reading, selecting relevant information, showing workings clearly
- **Approximate time:** About 2–3 weeks

### NSC Examination Preparation
- **Key concepts:** Paper 1 and Paper 2 readiness, context interpretation and strategy for extended application questions
- **Skills / outcomes:** manage time, interpret source material accurately and structure answers to maximise marks
- **Assessment angles:** paper-style familiarity, context-heavy questions, common interpretation errors
- **Approximate time:** Final exam window

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** External NSC examination after revision and final consolidation.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
