# Grade 12 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Finance: Interest, Banking and Inflation
- **Key concepts:** savings, loans, banking products and inflation in extended real-life cases
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare financial choices and explain how time and inflation affect value
- **Assessment angles:** interest-table reading, banking-fee comparisons, inflation interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Maps, Plans and Scale
- **Key concepts:** interpreting maps, plans and scaled representations for practical decisions
- **Skills / outcomes:** use scale confidently and extract useful information from visual representations
- **Assessment angles:** actual-distance calculations, plan interpretation, route and layout questions
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Measurement
- **Key concepts:** length, weight, volume, temperature, perimeter, area and volume in applied contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve multi-step measurement problems linked to household, workplace or civic situations
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-unit calculations, perimeter-area-volume choice, contextual modelling
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks plus revision

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or investigation and the internal mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
