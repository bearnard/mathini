# Grade 12 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Measurement: Conversions and Time
- **Key concepts:** high-frequency unit conversions, time calculations and practical measurement contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** work quickly and accurately with conversions and interpret everyday measurement information
- **Assessment angles:** time formats, elapsed time, multi-unit conversions, realistic estimates
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Finance: Documents, Tariffs, Budgets, Cost Price, Selling Price and Break-even
- **Key concepts:** financial documents and decision-making across personal and business contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse tariffs and budgets, calculate pricing and identify break-even points
- **Assessment angles:** reading payslips or bills, break-even analysis, pricing questions, tariff comparisons
- **Approximate time:** About 4 weeks

### Data Handling
- **Key concepts:** reading, comparing and critiquing data displays and summaries
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret graphs critically, compare sets of data and communicate clear conclusions
- **Assessment angles:** data claims, misleading graphs, summary statistics in context
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment, investigation and a control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
