# Grade 12 — English Home Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Final NSC revision
- **Key concepts:** Paper 1, Paper 2, Paper 3, exam routines, time management
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise strategically for the national examination and switch confidently between paper demands
- **Assessment angles:** paper-format recognition, timing, summary and writing strategies

### Literature consolidation
- **Key concepts:** drama, novel, poetry, short revision sets, unseen response
- **Skills / outcomes:** recall core arguments, themes and quotations across the prescribed texts
- **Assessment angles:** theme linkage, quotation recall, unseen interpretation

### Writing recall and proofreading
- **Key concepts:** essay planning, transactional formats, editing checklist, register control
- **Skills / outcomes:** enter the final exam with secure control of required writing formats
- **Assessment angles:** format recall, editing, tone and audience questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final NSC examinations (Paper 1, Paper 2 and Paper 3); school-based oral work should already be completed.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 SBA counts 25% of the final mark; the external final examination counts 75%.

## Notes
Term 4 is dedicated to final revision and the national examination timetable.
