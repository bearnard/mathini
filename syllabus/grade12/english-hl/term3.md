# Grade 12 — English Home Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Prelims and high-level literature revision
- **Key concepts:** literature essay, contextual response, theme synthesis, quotation revision
- **Skills / outcomes:** answer literature questions with the depth and precision expected in NSC preparation
- **Assessment angles:** essay planning, evidence selection, contextual recall

### Advanced writing and editing
- **Key concepts:** transactional precision, essay refinement, style, coherence, exam editing
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine writing choices and avoid common exam errors
- **Assessment angles:** editing traps, paragraph cohesion, tone and register checks

### Oral completion and trial preparation
- **Key concepts:** oral portfolio, listening tasks, speaking confidence, paper strategy
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete SBA oral work and prepare strategically for trial examinations
- **Assessment angles:** oral criteria, exam planning, mixed-paper practice

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Completion of oral SBA, a formal writing or literature task, and the preparatory or trial examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 SBA counts 25% of the final mark; the external final examination counts 75%.

## Notes
Term 3 is usually the last full teaching term before the trial examination period.
