# Grade 12 — English Home Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Continuation of prescribed works
- **Key concepts:** drama, novel, seen and unseen poetry, interpretation, comparison
- **Skills / outcomes:** extend literature study toward exam-style essays and contextual responses
- **Assessment angles:** comparison questions, unseen poetry hooks, theme development

### Formal and persuasive writing
- **Key concepts:** argumentative and discursive essays, speeches, articles, formal letters, reviews
- **Skills / outcomes:** sustain a clear line of argument and adapt tone to task demands
- **Assessment angles:** thesis statements, tone, format and register questions

### Mid-year exam preparation
- **Key concepts:** Paper 1 routines, literature revision, oral continuation, timed writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare for the first major Grade 12 exam cycle across all papers
- **Assessment angles:** summary timing, literature recall, writing under time pressure

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal SBA tasks continue alongside the mid-year examination and oral programme.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 SBA counts 25% of the final mark; the external final examination counts 75%.

## Notes
Term 2 usually contains a major examination and strong emphasis on completing portfolio evidence to standard.
