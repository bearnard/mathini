# Grade 12 — English Home Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Orientation and prescribed texts
- **Key concepts:** baseline assessment, prescribed drama, novel, poetry, literary essay, contextual questions
- **Skills / outcomes:** start Grade 12 literature with confident use of evidence and exam terminology
- **Assessment angles:** quotation support, contextual questioning, literary essay prompts

### Paper 3 writing programme
- **Key concepts:** narrative, argumentative, discursive, reflective, descriptive essays, speeches, letters, reviews
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce Grade 12-level essays and transactional texts using the full writing process
- **Assessment angles:** essay-type selection, register, purpose and audience, planning quality

### Paper 1 language practice
- **Key concepts:** comprehension, summary, visual literacy, editing, oral preparation
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply exam-style reading and editing strategies from the start of the year
- **Assessment angles:** summary rules, inference, editing, visual-text hooks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** SBA writing and oral tasks, literature-based assessment, and a term test aligned to Paper 1, 2 and 3 expectations.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 SBA counts 25% of the final mark; the external final examination counts 75%.

## Notes
Grade 12 English HL is organised around Paper 1 (language in context), Paper 2 (literature) and Paper 3 (writing). Prescribed texts vary by DBE cycle and school.
