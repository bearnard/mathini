# Grade 12 — Life Sciences: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Genetics and inheritance
- **Key concepts:** genes, alleles, dominance, recessiveness, sex linkage, monohybrid crosses, dihybrid crosses, pedigrees, mutations
- **Skills / outcomes:** Solve inheritance problems, interpret pedigrees, and apply genetics to real-world contexts.
- **Assessment angles:** Punnett-square questions, pedigree analysis, and mutation or blood-group items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Responding to the environment in humans
- **Key concepts:** nervous system, reflex arc, neurons, human eye, human ear, sensory receptors
- **Skills / outcomes:** Trace nerve impulses, explain reflex actions, and interpret sense-organ diagrams.
- **Assessment angles:** Reflex-arc labels, eye and ear diagrams, and stimulus-response questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Endocrine system, homeostasis and plant responses
- **Key concepts:** endocrine glands, hormones, negative feedback, blood glucose control, thermoregulation, plant hormones, tropisms
- **Skills / outcomes:** Explain hormonal control, compare endocrine and nervous coordination, and interpret plant-response experiments.
- **Assessment angles:** Hormone-function tables, homeostasis scenarios, and tropism diagrams.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical task and June examination or controlled test.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 promotion is typically 25% SBA / 75% external examination.
