# Grade 12 — Life Sciences: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–4

## Topics

### Paper 1 consolidation
- **Key concepts:** human reproduction, nervous system, endocrine system, homeostasis, plant responses, applied diagrams
- **Skills / outcomes:** Revise systems-based biology and refine interpretation of diagrams and physiological scenarios.
- **Assessment angles:** System diagrams, homeostasis questions, and data-response items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Paper 2 consolidation
- **Key concepts:** DNA, meiosis, genetics, evolution, human evolution, scientific writing for NSC exams
- **Skills / outcomes:** Integrate molecular genetics and evolution content and answer extended questions accurately.
- **Assessment angles:** Genetic-cross questions, evolution evidence, and structured explanatory responses.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** NSC final examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 promotion is typically 25% SBA / 75% external examination.
