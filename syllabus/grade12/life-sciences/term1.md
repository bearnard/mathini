# Grade 12 — Life Sciences: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### DNA and protein synthesis
- **Key concepts:** DNA, genes, chromosomes, replication, RNA, transcription, translation, genetic code
- **Skills / outcomes:** Explain the flow of information from DNA to proteins and interpret molecular-biology diagrams.
- **Assessment angles:** DNA structure labels, sequence questions, and protein-synthesis flow diagrams.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Meiosis
- **Key concepts:** Meiosis I, Meiosis II, crossing over, reduction division, non-disjunction, genetic variation
- **Skills / outcomes:** Sequence stages of meiosis and explain its importance in sexual reproduction and variation.
- **Assessment angles:** Phase identification, comparison with mitosis, and abnormal-meiosis questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Reproduction in vertebrates and humans
- **Key concepts:** fertilisation strategies, ovipary, vivipary, parental care, puberty, menstrual cycle, implantation, gestation, placenta
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare reproductive strategies and describe human reproductive processes accurately.
- **Assessment angles:** System diagrams, process sequencing, and comparison questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical task and formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 promotion is typically 25% SBA / 75% external examination.
