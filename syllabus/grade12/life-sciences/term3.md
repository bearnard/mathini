# Grade 12 — Life Sciences: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Evolution by natural selection
- **Key concepts:** Darwinism, Lamarckism, natural selection, speciation, punctuated equilibrium, evidence for evolution
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare explanatory theories, interpret evidence, and explain how species change over time.
- **Assessment angles:** Theory comparisons, evidence-based questions, and speciation scenarios.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Human evolution
- **Key concepts:** hominid fossils, common ancestry, Out of Africa hypothesis, trends in human development
- **Skills / outcomes:** Interpret fossil evidence and explain major evolutionary trends in humans.
- **Assessment angles:** Fossil comparisons, timeline questions, and evidence-matching items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Revision and trial examinations
- **Key concepts:** integration of Paper 1 and Paper 2 content, data-response practice, extended biological explanations
- **Skills / outcomes:** Consolidate high-yield concepts and prepare for full trial papers under timed conditions.
- **Assessment angles:** Mixed-topic exam questions, data interpretation, and paragraph-style reasoning.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal assignment and trial examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 promotion is typically 25% SBA / 75% external examination.
