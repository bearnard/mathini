# Grade 12 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Trial-exam literature revision
- **Key concepts:** literature essay, contextual responses, theme synthesis, quotation revision
- **Skills / outcomes:** answer literature questions with the precision expected for the NSC examinations
- **Assessment angles:** essay planning, evidence selection, quotation recall

### Advanced writing and editing
- **Key concepts:** transactional precision, essay refinement, coherence, proofreading, style
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine writing quality and reduce avoidable errors before trial and final exams
- **Assessment angles:** editing traps, paragraph coherence, register checks

### Oral completion and exam strategy
- **Key concepts:** oral portfolio, listening tasks, speaking confidence, paper strategy
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete remaining oral SBA and prepare strategically for preliminary exams
- **Assessment angles:** oral criteria, mixed-paper practice, planning prompts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Completion of oral SBA, a formal writing or literature task, and the preparatory or trial examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 SBA counts 25% of the final mark; the external final examination counts 75%.

## Notes
Term 3 is usually the last substantial teaching term before the preliminary examination period.
