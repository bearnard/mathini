# Grade 12 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Prescribed-text programme
- **Key concepts:** prescribed drama, novel, poetry, contextual response, literary essay
- **Skills / outcomes:** start the Grade 12 literature programme with stronger exam awareness and textual support
- **Assessment angles:** literature essay prompts, quotation support, contextual questions

### Paper 3 writing programme
- **Key concepts:** essay types, speeches, letters, reviews, reports, process writing
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce Grade 12-level essays and transactional pieces for varied purposes and audiences
- **Assessment angles:** essay-type choice, register control, format recall

### Paper 1 language preparation
- **Key concepts:** comprehension, summary, visual literacy, editing, oral preparation
- **Skills / outcomes:** build strong routines for the language paper and oral portfolio tasks
- **Assessment angles:** summary rules, visual-text hooks, editing items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** SBA writing and oral tasks, literature-based assessment and a term test aligned to FAL paper expectations.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 SBA counts 25% of the final mark; the external final examination counts 75%.

## Notes
Grade 12 Afrikaans FAL follows the FET paper structure: Paper 1 language in context, Paper 2 literature, Paper 3 writing. Prescribed texts vary by DBE cycle and school.
