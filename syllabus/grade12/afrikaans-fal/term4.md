# Grade 12 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Final NSC revision
- **Key concepts:** Paper 1, Paper 2, Paper 3, time management, exam routines
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise strategically for the national examination and switch confidently between papers
- **Assessment angles:** paper-format recognition, timing, summary and writing strategies

### Literature consolidation
- **Key concepts:** drama, novel, poetry, theme recall, unseen response
- **Skills / outcomes:** retain core literary arguments and evidence across all prescribed texts
- **Assessment angles:** theme linkage, contextual recall, unseen interpretation

### Writing-format recall
- **Key concepts:** essay planning, transactional formats, proofreading checklist, register control
- **Skills / outcomes:** enter the final examination with secure control of required writing forms
- **Assessment angles:** format recall, editing, tone and audience items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final NSC examinations; school-based oral work should already be completed.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 SBA counts 25% of the final mark; the external final examination counts 75%.

## Notes
Term 4 is dedicated to final revision and the national examination timetable.
