# Grade 12 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Continuation of set works
- **Key concepts:** drama, novel, poetry, unseen response, comparison, interpretation
- **Skills / outcomes:** extend literature study toward exam-style contextual and essay responses
- **Assessment angles:** comparison questions, unseen-text hooks, theme development

### Formal and persuasive writing
- **Key concepts:** argumentative or discursive essay, article, formal letter, speech, review
- **Skills / outcomes:** write with sharper purpose and formal control in preparation for major exams
- **Assessment angles:** thesis statements, audience-purpose matching, format and tone

### Mid-year exam preparation
- **Key concepts:** summary, comprehension, oral continuation, timed writing, literature revision
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare for the first major Grade 12 exam cycle and moderate SBA work
- **Assessment angles:** time management, summary technique, literature recall

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal SBA tasks continue together with the mid-year examination and oral programme.
- **SBA weighting:** Grade 12 SBA counts 25% of the final mark; the external final examination counts 75%.

## Notes
Term 2 generally includes a major examination and a strong push to standardise portfolio evidence.
