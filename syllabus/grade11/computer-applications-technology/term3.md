# Grade 11 — Computer Applications Technology: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Software, communications and information management
- **Key concepts:** application software, online services, mobile internet, website accessibility, 4IR and 5IR, information management tasks, PAT phases
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate software and online platforms and collect, process and present information for PAT-style tasks
- **Assessment angles:** software-choice questions, communications theory, PAT evidence and information-quality questions

### Practical solution development
- **Key concepts:** database reports, spreadsheet integration, word-processing mail merge and referencing, HTML links and page design
- **Skills / outcomes:** build integrated digital products, import and export data and apply design principles across applications
- **Assessment angles:** practical integration tasks, report layouts, HTML design questions, formatting and data-processing fixes

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical test, alternative/theory test and PAT phase work.
- **SBA weighting:** CAT uses task-based SBA; Grade 12 commonly uses 25% SBA + 25% PAT + 50% exams, while Grades 10–11 follow the school POA with PAT-style practical work.

## Notes
The Grade 11 PAT becomes a major organiser of learning in term 3. Evidence of planning, processing and presentation matters, not just the final product.
