# Grade 11 — Computer Applications Technology: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Communication tools and final PAT work
- **Key concepts:** e-mail management, distribution lists, web-based mail, online risks, ransomware, clickjacking, AI, information management and PAT finalisation
- **Skills / outcomes:** manage online communication effectively, explain emerging risks and complete PAT outputs to examination standard
- **Assessment angles:** e-mail organisation tasks, online-risk scenarios, PAT moderation checks

### Application integration and exam preparation
- **Key concepts:** documents, spreadsheets, databases, integrated software use, case studies, final practical and theory papers
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve cross-application tasks and prepare for full examination papers under time pressure
- **Assessment angles:** integrated practical tasks, case-study theory items, end-of-year paper practice

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** PAT phase 3 completion and end-of-year practical plus theory examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** CAT uses task-based SBA; Grade 12 commonly uses 25% SBA + 25% PAT + 50% exams, while Grades 10–11 follow the school POA with PAT-style practical work.

## Notes
Term 4 is compact and exam-focused. Learners should finish PAT requirements before switching fully to case-study consolidation and examination papers.
