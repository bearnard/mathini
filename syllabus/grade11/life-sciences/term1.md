# Grade 11 — Life Sciences: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Micro-organisms and disease
- **Key concepts:** viruses, bacteria, protists, fungi, ecological roles, biotechnology uses, disease management
- **Skills / outcomes:** Classify micro-organisms, discuss their useful and harmful roles, and interpret disease-prevention information.
- **Assessment angles:** Classification questions, disease examples, and practical observation tasks.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Biodiversity of plants
- **Key concepts:** bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms, angiosperms, evolutionary trends, seeds and reproduction
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare plant groups and explain trends in adaptation and reproduction.
- **Assessment angles:** Group-comparison tables, life-cycle prompts, and structural-feature questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Biodiversity of animals
- **Key concepts:** invertebrate phyla, body plans, symmetry, cephalisation, germ layers, vertebrate overview
- **Skills / outcomes:** Recognise diagnostic features and compare major animal groups.
- **Assessment angles:** Phylum-identification items, diagram labeling, and body-plan comparisons.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical task and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
