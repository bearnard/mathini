# Grade 11 — Life Sciences: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–4

## Topics

### Human impact on the environment
- **Key concepts:** climate change, ozone depletion, water quality, food security, habitat destruction, loss of biodiversity, waste
- **Skills / outcomes:** Evaluate human pressures on ecosystems and suggest evidence-based mitigation strategies.
- **Assessment angles:** Case studies, data interpretation, and short evaluative responses.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Revision and examination practice
- **Key concepts:** biodiversity, life processes, ecology, drawings, graphs, data-response technique
- **Skills / outcomes:** Integrate content across Paper 1 and Paper 2 style questions and improve exam technique.
- **Assessment angles:** Mixed-topic questions, graph analysis, and biological terminology checks.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
