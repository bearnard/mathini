# Grade 11 — Life Sciences: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Gas exchange
- **Key concepts:** breathing system, alveoli, ventilation, gaseous exchange, homeostatic control of breathing
- **Skills / outcomes:** Describe structure and function of the respiratory system and interpret gas-exchange diagrams.
- **Assessment angles:** Alveolus diagrams, ventilation steps, and adaptation questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Excretion in humans
- **Key concepts:** kidney, nephron, filtration, reabsorption, excretion, dialysis, kidney failure
- **Skills / outcomes:** Explain urine formation and compare healthy kidney function with medical interventions.
- **Assessment angles:** Nephron labeling, sequence questions, and disease/treatment scenarios.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Population ecology
- **Key concepts:** population size, density, natality, mortality, immigration, emigration, competition, succession
- **Skills / outcomes:** Interpret population data and explain ecological interactions and succession.
- **Assessment angles:** Population graphs, interaction matching, and ecological case studies.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project/assignment and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
