# Grade 11 — Life Sciences: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Photosynthesis
- **Key concepts:** raw materials, stored energy, chlorophyll, starch tests, factors affecting photosynthesis
- **Skills / outcomes:** Describe the process, interpret experiments, and explain environmental conditions needed for photosynthesis.
- **Assessment angles:** Starch-test investigations, equation-style summaries, and graph interpretation.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Animal nutrition
- **Key concepts:** alimentary canal, digestion, absorption, assimilation, associated glands, blood sugar control
- **Skills / outcomes:** Trace food through the digestive system and relate organ structure to function.
- **Assessment angles:** System diagrams, process sequencing, and application to healthy eating.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

### Cellular respiration
- **Key concepts:** aerobic respiration, anaerobic respiration, ATP release, oxygen uptake, carbon dioxide release
- **Skills / outcomes:** Compare aerobic and anaerobic pathways and interpret respiration investigations.
- **Assessment angles:** Comparison tables, experiment questions, and energy-yield reasoning.
- **Knowledge strand:** Life & Living

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment/practical and mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
