# Grade 11 — Physical Sciences: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Momentum and vertical projectile motion
- **Key concepts:** momentum, impulse, conservation, collisions, vertical projectiles, motion graphs
- **Skills / outcomes:** Solve collision problems, analyse projectiles mathematically, and justify conservation assumptions.
- **Assessment angles:** Collision calculations, projectile graphs, and motion-diagram questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Electrostatics and electric circuits
- **Key concepts:** Coulomb’s law, electric fields, current, potential difference, resistance, series and parallel circuits
- **Skills / outcomes:** Calculate simple field or force values, analyse circuits, and compare resistor arrangements.
- **Assessment angles:** Circuit calculations, field sketches, and charge-interaction questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical task and June test or examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
