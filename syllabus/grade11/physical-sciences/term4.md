# Grade 11 — Physical Sciences: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–4

## Topics

### Physics consolidation
- **Key concepts:** mechanics, electricity, vectors, graphs, practical data interpretation
- **Skills / outcomes:** Integrate prior physics topics, revise formula use, and manage timed problem solving.
- **Assessment angles:** Mixed-paper calculations, graph interpretation, and experiment-style questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Chemistry consolidation
- **Key concepts:** organic chemistry, electrochemistry, structure-property links, exam response techniques
- **Skills / outcomes:** Synthesise chemistry concepts and present clear scientific explanations in exam conditions.
- **Assessment angles:** Structural questions, redox reasoning, and terminology checks.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
