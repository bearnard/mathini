# Grade 11 — Physical Sciences: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Vectors and forces
- **Key concepts:** resultant vectors, vector resolution, free-body diagrams, Newton’s laws, friction, net force
- **Skills / outcomes:** Resolve vectors, draw force diagrams, and explain motion using Newton’s laws.
- **Assessment angles:** Vector calculations, FBDs, and force-analysis questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

### Work, energy and power
- **Key concepts:** work done, net work, power, conservation of mechanical energy, transfer of energy
- **Skills / outcomes:** Calculate work and power, connect force and displacement, and interpret energy changes in systems.
- **Assessment angles:** Work-energy calculations, graph interpretation, and contextual mechanics problems.
- **Knowledge strand:** Energy & Change

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical investigation and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
