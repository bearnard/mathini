# Grade 11 — Physical Sciences: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Organic chemistry and intermolecular forces
- **Key concepts:** hydrocarbons, functional groups, IUPAC naming, homologous series, molecular shape, intermolecular forces, boiling point
- **Skills / outcomes:** Name and classify compounds, link structure to properties, and compare members of homologous series.
- **Assessment angles:** Naming questions, structural formulae, and property-prediction items.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

### Electrochemistry and chemical change
- **Key concepts:** redox, oxidation numbers, electrochemical cells, electrolysis, energy change in reactions
- **Skills / outcomes:** Identify oxidation and reduction, explain cell components, and interpret reaction-energy information.
- **Assessment angles:** Cell diagrams, half-reaction reasoning, and redox terminology questions.
- **Knowledge strand:** Matter & Materials

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Practical investigation and control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% exams in FET sciences.
