# Grade 11 — Business Studies: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Entrepreneurship and venture planning
- **Key concepts:** entrepreneurial qualities, business plan, action plan, starting a venture, venture implementation
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate entrepreneurial readiness, convert plans into action and justify venture decisions
- **Assessment angles:** entrepreneur-profile questions, action-plan steps, start-up recommendations

### Business information and team dynamics
- **Key concepts:** presentation of business information, team stages, team dynamics theories, conflict management
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare business communication and manage group processes and conflict in a workplace setting
- **Assessment angles:** presentation-method items, team-stage identification, conflict-handling case studies

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project and controlled test on entrepreneurial and teamwork content.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
This term integrates ventures and roles. Learners should be able to move from planning a business to managing the people dynamics inside it.
