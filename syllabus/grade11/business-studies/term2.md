# Grade 11 — Business Studies: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Creative thinking and change management
- **Key concepts:** creative thinking, problem solving, stress management, crisis management, change management
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply structured problem-solving tools and recommend ways businesses manage change and uncertainty
- **Assessment angles:** management scenarios, stress/crisis interventions, problem-solving sequences

### Operations, marketing and ethics
- **Key concepts:** marketing function, production function, quality, safety, professionalism, ethics
- **Skills / outcomes:** explain core operations and marketing activities and evaluate ethical and professional conduct in business contexts
- **Assessment angles:** function-specific case studies, quality indicators, ethics and professionalism questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Presentation and June examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
Term 2 brings Grade 11 closer to practical business management. Expect many scenario questions on operations, marketing and workplace conduct.
