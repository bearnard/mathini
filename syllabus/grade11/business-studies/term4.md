# Grade 11 — Business Studies: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Human resources function
- **Key concepts:** recruitment, selection, contracts, induction, salary determination, benefits, interviewing
- **Skills / outcomes:** explain HR processes and apply them to workplace scenarios and staffing decisions
- **Assessment angles:** HR process-ordering, interview and selection scenarios, labour-practice questions

### Revision and final examinations
- **Key concepts:** business environments, ventures, operations and roles across the Grade 11 year
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise paper 1 and paper 2 content in integrated case-study and essay formats
- **Assessment angles:** mixed structured questions, long responses, cross-topic comparisons

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination after HR revision.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
Term 4 opens with HR before switching rapidly into revision. This is a good point to consolidate action verbs and longer written responses.
