# Grade 11 — Business Studies: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Business environments and challenges
- **Key concepts:** influences on business environments, control factors, challenges of the environments, adapting to change, socio-economic issues
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse internal and external pressures on businesses and suggest practical responses to challenges
- **Assessment angles:** environment-analysis case studies, control-factor questions, adaptation strategies

### Business sectors, ownership and acquisition
- **Key concepts:** business sectors, company benefits versus other ownership forms, avenues of acquiring a business
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare ownership options, explain sector roles and identify ways entrepreneurs can enter or acquire businesses
- **Assessment angles:** ownership comparisons, sector applications, acquisition-method recommendations

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Case study and control test on term 1 business environments content.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Business Studies.

## Notes
Grade 11 sharpens the analytical side of Business Studies. Learners should explain not only what an environment is, but how firms respond strategically.
