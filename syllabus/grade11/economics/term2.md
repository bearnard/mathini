# Grade 11 — Economics: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 11–20

## Topics

### Elasticity, market relationships and growth
- **Key concepts:** price elasticity, income and cross elasticity, substitutes and complements, cost and revenue, market structures, Gini coefficient, redistribution
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret graphs and tables, apply calculations, explain policy choices, analyse case studies, write structured responses
- **Assessment angles:** data-response questions, graph interpretation, calculations, policy paragraphs and short essays

- **Formal tasks this term:** Project and mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** 25% Programme of Assessment / 75% examinations in FET Economics.
