# Grade 11 — Information Technology: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### PAT completion and revision
- **Key concepts:** integrated practical work, debugging, testing, documentation, full-year revision
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete practical deliverables cleanly and review for both practical and theory papers
- **Assessment angles:** trace-and-fix questions, integrated exam prompts, documentation checks

### Examination consolidation
- **Key concepts:** OOP, files, SQL, networks and theory revision
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare for end-of-year examinations using full-paper practice and targeted remediation
- **Assessment angles:** mixed-paper revision, timing strategy, common-error correction

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Year-end practical and theory examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** For Grades 10–11, 2023 ATP mediation indicates SBA converts to 40% and examinations to 60%; PAT work is built into the year programme.

## Notes
Term 4 should prioritise consolidation and exam fluency. Practical skill retention matters as much as theory recall at this stage.
