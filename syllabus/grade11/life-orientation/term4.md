# Grade 11 — Life Orientation: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Careers and career choices
- **Key concepts:** further study, bursaries, learnerships, skills development, employability
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare post-school options and identify entry requirements, costs and support opportunities
- **Assessment angles:** information-search tasks, comparison tables, oral presentations

### World of work and entrepreneurship
- **Key concepts:** work ethics, part-time work, entrepreneurship, labour trends, professionalism
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse labour-market trends and discuss entrepreneurial and employment pathways
- **Assessment angles:** case studies, opportunity mapping, short written responses

### Application documents and planning
- **Key concepts:** application letters, CVs, references, portfolios, interview readiness
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare stronger application documents and practise formal self-presentation
- **Assessment angles:** document-format checks, mock interview prompts, editing tasks

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** personal fitness review, lifelong activity, participation goals, recreation
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate physical activity habits and set sustainable goals for senior schooling and beyond
- **Assessment angles:** fitness reflections, target-setting tasks, participation rubrics

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A career portfolio or application task, final SBA moderation, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** FET Life Orientation uses 75% SBA and 25% PET across the year.
