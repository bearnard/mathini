# Grade 11 — Life Orientation: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Development of the self in society
- **Key concepts:** life roles, purpose, meaning, values, self-management
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse changing roles in family, school and community life and plan more intentional personal growth
- **Assessment angles:** role-mapping tasks, reflective writing, source-based questions

### Relationships, sexuality and health
- **Key concepts:** healthy relationships, sexuality, consent, reproductive health, communication
- **Skills / outcomes:** discuss relationships and sexuality responsibly and use accurate health knowledge in decision-making
- **Assessment angles:** case studies, myth-and-fact items, discussion prompts

### Study skills and coping strategies
- **Key concepts:** exam strategies, workload management, stress, resilience, support systems
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine study habits and coping strategies for a more demanding academic year
- **Assessment angles:** study-plan reviews, stress-management tasks, self-evaluation checklists

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** fitness development, technique, endurance, flexibility, participation
- **Skills / outcomes:** maintain a personal activity programme and show consistent participation and self-discipline
- **Assessment angles:** fitness logs, technique observations, participation rubrics

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A written task or assignment, a short test, and ongoing Physical Education Task (PET) evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** FET Life Orientation uses 75% SBA and 25% PET across the year.
