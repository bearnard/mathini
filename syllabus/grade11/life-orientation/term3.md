# Grade 11 — Life Orientation: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Democracy and human rights
- **Key concepts:** Bill of Rights, discrimination, diversity, social justice, advocacy
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse discrimination and explain how rights frameworks and advocacy can challenge injustice
- **Assessment angles:** source analysis, debate questions, rights-application tasks

### Democratic structures and participation
- **Key concepts:** government structures, elections, public participation, accountability, law
- **Skills / outcomes:** describe how democratic institutions function and how citizens influence decisions
- **Assessment angles:** matching activities, short essays, issue-based discussions

### Media, ideology and active citizenship
- **Key concepts:** bias, propaganda, values, civic voice, critical literacy
- **Skills / outcomes:** read media critically and use evidence-based arguments in civic conversations
- **Assessment angles:** media comparison tasks, argumentative prompts, reflection questions

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** athletics, conditioning, recreation, safe leadership, participation
- **Skills / outcomes:** show safe leadership and sustained effort in varied physical activities
- **Assessment angles:** leadership checklists, practical observations, participation records

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A source-based task or test, a reflective or practical project, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** FET Life Orientation uses 75% SBA and 25% PET across the year.
