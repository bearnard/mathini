# Grade 11 — Life Orientation: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Social and environmental responsibility
- **Key concepts:** environmental justice, sustainability, social justice, accountability, responsible action
- **Skills / outcomes:** evaluate environmental and social challenges and propose ethical responses for communities and institutions
- **Assessment angles:** research questions, issue-analysis grids, intervention ideas

### Community participation
- **Key concepts:** volunteering, leadership, campaigns, support services, collaboration
- **Skills / outcomes:** plan or assess community participation projects that address a real need
- **Assessment angles:** project proposals, reflection tasks, peer feedback prompts

### Risk and wellbeing
- **Key concepts:** substance abuse, trauma, violence, healthy coping, support networks
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify factors that increase risk and describe protective strategies for individuals and communities
- **Assessment angles:** case studies, prevention-message tasks, structured discussions

### Physical Education Task
- **Key concepts:** team sports, strategy, coordination, leadership, wellness
- **Skills / outcomes:** take part in group physical activity with stronger leadership and strategy awareness
- **Assessment angles:** team-role rubrics, skill checks, wellness records

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A project, investigation or case study, a written task or test, and ongoing PET evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** FET Life Orientation uses 75% SBA and 25% PET across the year.
