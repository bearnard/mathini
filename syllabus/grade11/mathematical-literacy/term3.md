# Grade 11 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Measurement: Perimeter, Area and Volume
- **Key concepts:** calculating size, capacity and material use in practical scenarios
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve measurement tasks linked to rooms, containers, packaging and construction-style contexts
- **Assessment angles:** surface vs floor area, volume interpretation, unit consistency
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Maps, Plans and Models
- **Key concepts:** models, plans and representation of physical space
- **Skills / outcomes:** move between drawings or models and real objects and answer applied spatial questions
- **Assessment angles:** top view vs side view, model scaling, reading annotations
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Finance: Taxation
- **Key concepts:** tax-related deductions and personal or business finance interpretation
- **Skills / outcomes:** read tax information and calculate relevant amounts in familiar contexts
- **Assessment angles:** deduction interpretation, tax tables or slips, finance vocabulary
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Probability
- **Key concepts:** probability in everyday and risk-related contexts
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare likelihoods, calculate simple probabilities and explain results in words
- **Assessment angles:** relative likelihood, sample spaces, probability statements in context
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or investigation and a control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
