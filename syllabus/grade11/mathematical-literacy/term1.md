# Grade 11 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Patterns, Relationships and Representations
- **Key concepts:** interpreting relationships through tables, graphs and contextual formulae
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare options and describe trends using multiple representations
- **Assessment angles:** graph interpretation, trend comparison, selecting useful representations
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Measurement: Conversions and Time
- **Key concepts:** unit conversions, practical measurement and time contexts revisited at greater complexity
- **Skills / outcomes:** work accurately with conversions and solve multi-step time or measure tasks
- **Assessment angles:** compound conversions, elapsed-time reasoning, practical estimates
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Finance: Documents, Tariffs, Budgets and Break-even
- **Key concepts:** financial documents, tariff systems, income and expenditure, cost price, selling price and break-even analysis
- **Skills / outcomes:** analyse and compare business or personal finance situations and make justified choices
- **Assessment angles:** break-even interpretation, markup questions, tariff comparisons, budget decision-making
- **Approximate time:** About 5 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or investigation and a control test.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
