# Grade 11 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Finance: Interest, Banking and Inflation
- **Key concepts:** banking products, interest growth and inflation effects on spending power
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare financial offers and explain how inflation changes real value
- **Assessment angles:** compound vs simple growth in context, fee comparisons, inflation interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Measurement
- **Key concepts:** measuring length, weight, volume and temperature in realistic settings
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve practical problems using appropriate units, conversions and measurements
- **Assessment angles:** temperature scales, mixed-unit conversions, reading measuring devices
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Maps, Plans and Scale
- **Key concepts:** scale drawings, maps and route-based interpretation
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate actual distances, compare routes and interpret plan views accurately
- **Assessment angles:** scale conversion, route choice, map reading and plan interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Revision and Mid-year Preparation
- **Key concepts:** integrating finance, measurement and map skills for examination readiness
- **Skills / outcomes:** synthesise term content in extended contextual questions
- **Assessment angles:** selecting relevant data, showing working, managing exam contexts
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks including exams

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or investigation and the mid-year examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
