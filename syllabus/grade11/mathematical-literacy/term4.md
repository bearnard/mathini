# Grade 11 — Mathematical Literacy: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Finance: Exchange Rates
- **Key concepts:** currency conversion and the effect of changing exchange rates
- **Skills / outcomes:** convert currencies, compare travel or trade options and interpret rate fluctuations
- **Assessment angles:** buying vs selling rates, currency-conversion errors, contextual decision making
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Data Handling
- **Key concepts:** data collection, representation and interpretation using graphs and summary measures
- **Skills / outcomes:** critique graphs, compare data sets and write short evidence-based conclusions
- **Assessment angles:** graph choice, trend explanation, central tendency and spread in context
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Revision and Exam Integration
- **Key concepts:** integrated revision across finance, measurement, maps, data and probability
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply Mathematical Literacy tools across extended case studies and exam-style papers
- **Assessment angles:** selecting relevant information, combining topics, exam pacing
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks including exams

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or investigation and the end-of-year examinations.
- **SBA weighting:** Mathematical Literacy FET CAPS: continuous assessment 25% and examinations 75%.
