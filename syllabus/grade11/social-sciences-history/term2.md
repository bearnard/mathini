# Grade 11 — History: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 11–20

## Topics

### Capitalism in the USA, 1900–1940
- **Key concepts:** Roaring Twenties, mass production, Wall Street Crash, Great Depression, New Deal, capitalism versus state intervention
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, chronology, comparing interpretations, paragraph and essay writing
- **Assessment angles:** source-based questions, causation essays, interpretation of cartoons/photos/maps, chronology prompts
- **Historical period / geographical region:** United States of America, 1900–1940

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Source-based or essay task, often linked to a mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET History.
