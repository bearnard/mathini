# Grade 11 — History: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 31–40

## Topics

### Nationalism in South Africa, Africa and the Middle East
- **Key concepts:** African nationalism, decolonisation, Zionism and Arab nationalism, liberation movements, identity, nation-building
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, chronology, comparing interpretations, paragraph and essay writing
- **Assessment angles:** source-based questions, causation essays, interpretation of cartoons/photos/maps, chronology prompts
- **Historical period / geographical region:** South Africa, Africa and the Middle East, 20th century

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** End-of-year examination and revision-based essay/source work.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET History.
