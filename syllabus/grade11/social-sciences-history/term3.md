# Grade 11 — History: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 21–30

## Topics

### Ideas of race in the late nineteenth and twentieth centuries
- **Key concepts:** scientific racism, Social Darwinism, eugenics, segregation, Nazism, apartheid, resistance to racist ideology
- **Skills / outcomes:** source analysis, chronology, comparing interpretations, paragraph and essay writing
- **Assessment angles:** source-based questions, causation essays, interpretation of cartoons/photos/maps, chronology prompts
- **Historical period / geographical region:** Europe, the USA, Australia and South Africa, c. 1850–1990

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Essay or source-based task plus controlled test; Grade 12 may also include trial-exam practice.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET History.
