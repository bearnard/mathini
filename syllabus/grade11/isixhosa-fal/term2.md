# Grade 11 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** interviews, panel discussion, oral summaries, listening for attitude and bias
- **Skills / outcomes:** identify attitude and purpose in spoken texts and adapt register in more formal oral tasks
- **Assessment angles:** oral rubrics, listening notes, register-awareness prompts

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** prose study, media texts, visual literacy, social themes, perspective
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare viewpoints in literature and media and justify interpretations with evidence
- **Assessment angles:** perspective questions, source comparison, theme prompts

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** review, report, email, speech writing, transactional control
- **Skills / outcomes:** write with more precision for formal contexts and shape responses to clear audiences
- **Assessment angles:** format checks, tone questions, report-writing tasks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** direct and indirect speech, connectors, passive voice, editing, cohesion
- **Skills / outcomes:** improve cohesion and formal language use across longer responses
- **Assessment angles:** speech conversion, connector tasks, editing exercises

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Controlled test, oral presentation and a formal writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
