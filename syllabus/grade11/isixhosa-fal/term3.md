# Grade 11 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** debate, prepared speech, seminar discussion, oral response to issues
- **Skills / outcomes:** sustain arguments orally and respond thoughtfully to literary or civic topics
- **Assessment angles:** debate rubrics, speech prompts, oral evaluation tasks

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** drama, poetry, short stories, critical reading, contextual analysis
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret texts more critically and support points with quotations and explanation
- **Assessment angles:** critical-reading questions, quotation tasks, contextual prompts

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** argumentative or discursive essay, formal letter, article, project presentation
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete a sustained project and write more persuasively in formal and reflective modes
- **Assessment angles:** project evidence, argument structure, format recall

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** complex sentences, style, punctuation, editing, vocabulary refinement
- **Skills / outcomes:** control sentence variety and polish writing for clarity, accuracy and effect
- **Assessment angles:** error correction, sentence-improvement items, vocabulary questions

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project submission and oral component, plus a formal language or writing task.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
