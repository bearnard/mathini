# Grade 11 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** prepared speeches, discussions, listening comprehension, oral response to literature
- **Skills / outcomes:** respond more confidently and analytically in spoken tasks and classroom discussions
- **Assessment angles:** speech rubrics, listening-for-detail items, discussion questions

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** novel, drama, poetry, media texts, theme, character, tone
- **Skills / outcomes:** continue sustained literature study and analyse how texts communicate meaning and values
- **Assessment angles:** theme questions, character evidence, media-analysis prompts

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** reflective essay, descriptive essay, friendly or formal texts, planning and drafting
- **Skills / outcomes:** write longer texts with stronger control of structure, tone and development
- **Assessment angles:** essay-type selection, paragraph flow, format checks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** editing, style, concord, punctuation, vocabulary in context, sentence control
- **Skills / outcomes:** apply grammar and style choices more deliberately in Paper 1-like tasks
- **Assessment angles:** editing items, style questions, concord and punctuation checks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Baseline or diagnostic work, a writing task, oral activity and a language or comprehension test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
