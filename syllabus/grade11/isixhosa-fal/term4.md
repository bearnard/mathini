# Grade 11 — isiXhosa First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Listening & Speaking
- **Key concepts:** revision of speeches, listening comprehension, reading fluency, presentation review
- **Skills / outcomes:** consolidate oral skills for final assessment and prepare for Grade 12 demands
- **Assessment angles:** oral recall, listening quizzes, fluency rubrics

### Reading & Viewing
- **Key concepts:** revision across literature, media texts, comprehension and summary
- **Skills / outcomes:** revise Paper 1 and Paper 2 skills through exam-style reading and viewing practice
- **Assessment angles:** summary tasks, literature revision prompts, visual-text questions

### Writing & Presenting
- **Key concepts:** exam essays, transactional recall, report and review writing, proofreading
- **Skills / outcomes:** complete timed writing tasks with stronger planning and control
- **Assessment angles:** planning checks, format recall, proofreading tasks

### Language Structures
- **Key concepts:** revision of all major grammar points, editing, sentence control, punctuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare for senior FET assessment by consolidating language structures in context
- **Assessment angles:** grammar MCQs, sentence correction, editing tasks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of any remaining oral evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not stated as a fixed term percentage in the ATP; tasks feed the annual SBA programme.
