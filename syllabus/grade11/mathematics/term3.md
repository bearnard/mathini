# Grade 11 — Mathematics: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Measurement
- **Key concepts:** selected measurement revision to support geometry and trigonometry
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve measurement problems accurately and use units consistently
- **Assessment angles:** unit consistency, diagram interpretation, link to geometry contexts
- **Approximate time:** About 1 week

### Euclidean Geometry
- **Key concepts:** circle geometry theorems, converses and riders involving tangents, chords and cyclic quadrilaterals
- **Skills / outcomes:** prove theorems and solve formal riders with clear reasons
- **Assessment angles:** circle-theorem identification, proof writing, tangent-chord theorem, cyclic quadrilateral reasoning
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Trigonometry
- **Key concepts:** sine rule, cosine rule and area rule in two-dimensional problems
- **Skills / outcomes:** select the correct rule and solve non-right-angled triangle problems
- **Assessment angles:** choosing the right rule, ambiguous information, calculator setup, proving trig results
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Finance, Growth and Decay
- **Key concepts:** compound growth, compound decay, depreciation, nominal and effective rates
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare financial options and solve multi-step investment or depreciation questions
- **Assessment angles:** growth vs decay, rate conversion, timeline interpretation, comparing offers
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Probability
- **Key concepts:** tree diagrams, dependent and independent events, complement rule and event relationships
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate compound probabilities and interpret whether events are independent
- **Assessment angles:** tree-diagram branching, dependent vs independent, complement and union calculations
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Assignment or test plus a second formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
