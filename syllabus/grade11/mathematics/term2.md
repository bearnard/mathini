# Grade 11 — Mathematics: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Functions
- **Key concepts:** effects of parameters on quadratic, hyperbolic, exponential and trigonometric graphs, average gradient and gradient at a point
- **Skills / outcomes:** sketch transformed graphs, interpret graph features and connect symbolic and graphical forms
- **Assessment angles:** parameter shifts, asymptotes, turning points, transformed trig graphs
- **Approximate time:** About 4 weeks

### Trigonometry
- **Key concepts:** identities, reduction formulae, general solutions and trigonometric equations
- **Skills / outcomes:** prove identities, simplify expressions and solve equations over general and restricted intervals
- **Assessment angles:** identity proof structure, reduction-formula choice, general solutions, equation solving
- **Approximate time:** About 4 weeks

### Examination Preparation
- **Key concepts:** integration of term 1 and 2 algebra, functions, analytical geometry and trigonometry
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare for full papers through mixed-topic problem solving and review
- **Assessment angles:** paper-structure familiarity, mixed-topic revision, time management under exam conditions
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal assignment and the mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
