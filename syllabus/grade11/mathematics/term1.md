# Grade 11 — Mathematics: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Exponents and Surds
- **Key concepts:** rational exponents, surd operations and equations involving surds
- **Skills / outcomes:** simplify and solve expressions with surds and rational exponents accurately
- **Assessment angles:** equivalent surd forms, exponent law application, solving simple surd equations
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Equations and Inequalities
- **Key concepts:** completing the square, quadratic equations, quadratic inequalities, equations in two unknowns and nature of roots
- **Skills / outcomes:** solve algebraic equations using multiple methods and interpret graphical meaning of solutions
- **Assessment angles:** quadratic formula use, inequality intervals, discriminant reasoning, system setup
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Number Patterns
- **Key concepts:** quadratic patterns, second differences and general terms
- **Skills / outcomes:** derive nth terms from structured patterns and model contextual sequences
- **Assessment angles:** second-difference recognition, pattern generalisation, table-to-formula questions
- **Approximate time:** About 2 weeks

### Analytical Geometry
- **Key concepts:** equations of lines, inclination, parallel and perpendicular lines, angle between lines
- **Skills / outcomes:** determine equations and solve coordinate geometry problems with justification
- **Assessment angles:** line-equation forms, angle between lines, inclination vs gradient, coordinate proof steps
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Investigation or project plus a formal test.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
