# Grade 11 — Mathematics: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–9

## Topics

### Statistics
- **Key concepts:** histograms, frequency polygons, ogives, variance, standard deviation, skewness and outliers
- **Skills / outcomes:** construct and interpret statistical displays and comment on distribution shape and spread
- **Assessment angles:** choosing summary measures, identifying skewness, reading ogives, outlier interpretation
- **Approximate time:** About 3 weeks

### Revision and Final Examination Preparation
- **Key concepts:** targeted consolidation across Papers 1 and 2, theorem recall and exam strategy
- **Skills / outcomes:** synthesise all Grade 11 topics and respond effectively to full-paper questions
- **Assessment angles:** paper-topic weighting, geometry proof recall, strategy for long-response items
- **Approximate time:** About 6 weeks including exams

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal test and the end-of-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** FET CAPS: school-based assessment 25% of the promotion mark and examinations 75%.
