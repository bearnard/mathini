# Grade 11 — English Home Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Prescribed prose or folklore and poetry
- **Key concepts:** prescribed novel or folklore, poetry analysis, literary devices, theme
- **Skills / outcomes:** engage with prescribed texts using more mature literary terminology and evidence
- **Assessment angles:** device identification, theme support, contextual literature responses

### Process writing for FET genres
- **Key concepts:** narrative, descriptive, argumentative, reflective, discursive essays, formal letters, CV, covering letter
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce extended and transactional texts with clearer purpose, structure and register
- **Assessment angles:** essay-type choices, format conventions, register, paragraph development

### Listening and language in context
- **Key concepts:** listening comprehension, transactional writing task, unprepared speech, language paper skills
- **Skills / outcomes:** respond accurately to spoken texts and edit language in context
- **Assessment angles:** listening recall, comprehension inference, grammar and editing items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Typical ATP schedule includes a listening task, transactional writing, an oral task and a language-in-context test.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Grade 11 usually formalises FET assessment patterns before Grade 12. Prescribed texts vary by DBE cycle and school.
