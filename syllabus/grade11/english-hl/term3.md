# Grade 11 — English Home Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Drama and poetry comparison
- **Key concepts:** prescribed drama, poetry comparison, stagecraft, tone, motif
- **Skills / outcomes:** write more analytical literature responses and compare texts with precision
- **Assessment angles:** comparison questions, stagecraft items, motif and theme prompts

### Literary and transactional responses
- **Key concepts:** literary essay, review, interview, report, prepared oral
- **Skills / outcomes:** move between analytical writing and practical communication with control
- **Assessment angles:** literary-essay hooks, transactional format, audience and register

### Language consolidation
- **Key concepts:** language in context, editing, oral delivery, exam-style tasks
- **Skills / outcomes:** maintain accuracy while preparing for end-of-year assessment
- **Assessment angles:** editing items, short comprehension hooks, oral effectiveness

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Prepared oral presentation, literature essay or test, and a language-in-context assessment.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 3 often shifts emphasis toward drama study and exam-style analytical writing.
