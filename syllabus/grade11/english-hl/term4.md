# Grade 11 — English Home Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Comprehensive literature revision
- **Key concepts:** novel, drama, poetry, thematic revision, quotation recall
- **Skills / outcomes:** synthesise literature knowledge across genres for final assessment
- **Assessment angles:** integrated literature questions, quotation recall, theme tracking

### Writing consolidation
- **Key concepts:** essay revision, transactional revision, editing, timing
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine structure and accuracy across all key writing forms
- **Assessment angles:** planning and editing hooks, format recall, topic suitability

### Exam readiness
- **Key concepts:** Paper 1, Paper 2, Paper 3 routines, oral evidence check
- **Skills / outcomes:** approach each FET paper strategically and confidently
- **Assessment angles:** paper-structure MCQs, revision checklists, common exam traps

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination covering the year’s literature, language and writing, with oral evidence completed as needed.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 is used to consolidate Grade 11 expectations before progression to Grade 12.
