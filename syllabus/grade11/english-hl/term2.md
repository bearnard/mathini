# Grade 11 — English Home Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Continued prose and poetry study
- **Key concepts:** seen and unseen poetry, prose continuation, visual literacy
- **Skills / outcomes:** compare texts more critically and sustain evidence-based discussion
- **Assessment angles:** comparison items, visual-literacy hooks, quote-based questions

### Advanced essay and functional writing
- **Key concepts:** discursive essay, reflective essay, agendas, minutes, reports, magazine articles
- **Skills / outcomes:** adapt writing for formal school and public contexts
- **Assessment angles:** text-type conventions, formal tone, task-purpose matching

### Mid-year exam preparation
- **Key concepts:** Paper 1 routines, Paper 2 revision, writing planning, oral continuation
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare for a full exam cycle across all three papers
- **Assessment angles:** summary technique, literature recall, writing under time limits

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Oral continuation, formal writing and the mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 usually includes a fuller exam and stronger visual-literacy integration.
