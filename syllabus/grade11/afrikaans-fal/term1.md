# Grade 11 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Baseline and literary genres
- **Key concepts:** baseline assessment, poetry, short stories, drama, literary terms
- **Skills / outcomes:** begin Grade 11 with stronger literary vocabulary and more independent response
- **Assessment angles:** genre identification, literary-term items, contextual questions

### Transactional and essay writing
- **Key concepts:** essays, reports, speeches, letters, process writing, grammar review
- **Skills / outcomes:** produce both extended and shorter texts with clearer purpose and structure
- **Assessment angles:** format conventions, topic suitability, paragraphing

### Language and oral programme
- **Key concepts:** language structures, listening, speaking, reading fluency, editing
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare for formal Grade 11 assessments across oral and written modes
- **Assessment angles:** listening recall, language editing, oral criteria

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** A test, a writing task and oral work are commonly scheduled in Term 1.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Grade 11 Afrikaans FAL strengthens FET assessment patterns before the Grade 12 year. Prescribed texts vary by DBE cycle and school.
