# Grade 11 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Visual literacy and interpretation
- **Key concepts:** charts, maps, advertisements, visual literacy, explanation
- **Skills / outcomes:** interpret information from visual texts and relate it to writing and oral tasks
- **Assessment angles:** visual-data questions, audience-purpose hooks, inference

### Extended writing set
- **Key concepts:** essays, reports, articles, formal writing, audience and register
- **Skills / outcomes:** write more sustained and formal Afrikaans for academic or public purposes
- **Assessment angles:** register choice, article and report format, coherence questions

### Continued literature study
- **Key concepts:** poetry, prose or drama continuation, theme, character, response
- **Skills / outcomes:** deepen literary response and prepare for mid-year assessment
- **Assessment angles:** theme support, character evidence, contextual items

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Formal writing, oral continuation and the mid-year examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 2 often combines visual literacy, extended writing and a larger exam cycle.
