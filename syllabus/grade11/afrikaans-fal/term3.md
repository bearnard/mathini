# Grade 11 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Advanced literature analysis
- **Key concepts:** prescribed poetry, short stories, novel or drama, comparison, interpretation
- **Skills / outcomes:** respond to literature with more precision and stronger textual support
- **Assessment angles:** comparison questions, contextual prompts, thematic links

### Sustained creative and transactional writing
- **Key concepts:** creative writing, transactional writing, minutes, emails, reports, speeches
- **Skills / outcomes:** switch flexibly between creative and practical texts while keeping format control
- **Assessment angles:** format recall, audience-purpose matching, editing tasks

### Prepared oral presentation
- **Key concepts:** oral presentation, listening, speaking confidence, exam practice
- **Skills / outcomes:** present ideas clearly and complete important oral evidence for the SBA programme
- **Assessment angles:** oral criteria, listening details, planning prompts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Prepared oral, formal writing and a literature or language assessment.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 3 usually balances literature analysis with formal oral performance and practical writing.
