# Grade 11 — Afrikaans First Additional Language: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–8

## Topics

### Revision of all prescribed work
- **Key concepts:** poetry, short stories, drama, prose, thematic revision
- **Skills / outcomes:** synthesise literature and language learning across the full year
- **Assessment angles:** theme linkage, quotation recall, integrated revision items

### Writing consolidation
- **Key concepts:** essay revision, transactional revision, proofreading, timing
- **Skills / outcomes:** refine writing across all major Grade 11 text forms
- **Assessment angles:** editing, format recall, topic suitability

### Exam readiness
- **Key concepts:** Paper 1, Paper 2, Paper 3 routines, oral evidence check
- **Skills / outcomes:** approach end-of-year examinations strategically and confidently
- **Assessment angles:** paper-format questions, checklist items, common exam traps

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination and completion of remaining SBA evidence.
- **SBA weighting:** Not usually expressed as a per-term percentage in the ATP; tasks contribute to the annual SBA programme.

## Notes
Term 4 consolidates Grade 11 expectations before learners move into Grade 12 NSC preparation.
