# Grade 11 — Geography: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 21–30

## Topics

### Development geography
- **Key concepts:** development indicators, development models, trade and development, globalisation, aid, gender, state and business roles
- **Skills / outcomes:** mapwork, GIS awareness, graph and data interpretation, fieldwork or case-study analysis
- **Assessment angles:** mapwork items, data-response questions, climate/settlement/resource essays, interpretation of graphs and images
- **Historical period / geographical region:** Local, regional and global contexts

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Research/essay task and test; Grade 12 may also include the trial examination.
- **SBA weighting:** 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Geography.
