# Grade 11 — Geography: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 31–40

## Topics

### Resources and sustainability
- **Key concepts:** resource use, soil formation, soil erosion, conventional and non-conventional energy, greener economies, GIS revision
- **Skills / outcomes:** mapwork, GIS awareness, graph and data interpretation, fieldwork or case-study analysis
- **Assessment angles:** mapwork items, data-response questions, climate/settlement/resource essays, interpretation of graphs and images
- **Historical period / geographical region:** South Africa and global sustainability challenges

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** End-of-year examination (Paper 1 and Paper 2 / mapwork).
- **SBA weighting:** 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Geography.
