# Grade 11 — Accounting: Term 3

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Budgeting
- **Key concepts:** budget concepts, cash budgets, debtors collections, creditors payments, projected statement of comprehensive income
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare cash budgets and projected income statements, explain shortfalls and propose adjustments
- **Assessment angles:** budget completion, cash-flow reasoning, recommendation questions, variance-style interpretation

### Inventory systems and cost accounting
- **Key concepts:** inventory systems, stock valuation, manufacturing costs, unit cost, break-even point, production cost statement
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate costs, compare inventory methods and solve basic manufacturing accounting problems
- **Assessment angles:** cost classification, break-even calculations, stock-system comparisons, interpretation of manufacturing figures

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Presentation and control test on budgeting and manufacturing content.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
Term 3 introduces more managerial/accounting-for-decision-making content. Calculations should be paired with comments on viability and control.
