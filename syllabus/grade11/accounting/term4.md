# Grade 11 — Accounting: Term 4

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–10

## Topics

### Value added tax
- **Key concepts:** purpose of VAT, input VAT, output VAT, VAT control, source-document implications, ethics and compliance
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate VAT, record VAT transactions and explain the business importance of compliance
- **Assessment angles:** VAT calculations, ledger effects, compliance and ethics questions

### Revision and final examination preparation
- **Key concepts:** reconciliation, partnerships, budgeting, inventory, manufacturing, VAT
- **Skills / outcomes:** integrate Grade 11 topics across paper practice and final revision
- **Assessment angles:** mixed end-of-year questions, multi-topic case studies, short calculations with comments

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Final examination after VAT and revision.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
Term 4 is brief and centres on VAT plus final-paper preparation. This is also where Grade 11 begins to resemble Grade 12 exam rhythm.
