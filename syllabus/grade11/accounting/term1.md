# Grade 11 — Accounting: Term 1

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Reconciliations and fixed assets
- **Key concepts:** bank reconciliation, creditors reconciliation, tangible or fixed asset register, depreciation, disposal, internal control
- **Skills / outcomes:** reconcile statements, update control records, record fixed-asset movements and explain supporting internal controls
- **Assessment angles:** bank recon formats, creditors errors, depreciation adjustments, internal-control comments

### Partnership accounting foundations
- **Key concepts:** partnership concepts, adjustments, partner current accounts, final accounts, accounting equation, ledgers
- **Skills / outcomes:** process partnership-specific transactions and prepare core accounts and statements
- **Assessment angles:** appropriation adjustments, partner-interest items, statement layouts, ledger postings

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Written report and control test on reconciliations, fixed assets and partnership basics.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
Grade 11 moves beyond sole traders into partnerships and stronger control systems. Ethics and internal control remain embedded in reconciliation and asset work.
