# Grade 11 — Accounting: Term 2

**CAPS phase:** FET Phase (Gr 10–12)
**ATP weeks:** Weeks 1–11

## Topics

### Partnership financial statements and notes
- **Key concepts:** statement of comprehensive income, statement of financial position, notes, appropriation account, partner capital and current accounts
- **Skills / outcomes:** prepare full partnership financial statements and related disclosures from trial-balance information
- **Assessment angles:** statement formatting, note completion, partner adjustments, narrative explanations of figures

### Analysis and interpretation of partnerships
- **Key concepts:** profitability, liquidity, solvency, return indicators, partner-specific interpretation
- **Skills / outcomes:** calculate indicators and comment on partnership performance, returns and financial position
- **Assessment angles:** ratio calculations, recommendation questions, interpretive comments tied to figures

## Assessment
- **Formal tasks this term:** Project on partnership financial statements and the June examination.
- **SBA weighting:** Typically 25% SBA / 75% examinations in FET Accounting.

## Notes
This term is statement-heavy. Learners must see links between preparation and interpretation, not treat ratios as isolated formula work.
