# Learning Aides — English Home Language (Gr 6–12)

## Subject Overview

English Home Language (HL) integrates four CAPS skill strands across all grades: **Listening &
Speaking**, **Reading & Viewing**, **Writing & Presenting**, and **Language Structures &
Conventions**. The curriculum progresses from foundational comprehension and narrative writing
in the Intermediate Phase, through literary analysis and transactional texts in the Senior Phase,
to the full Paper 1 / Paper 2 / Paper 3 FET examination structure in Grades 10–12.

Digital aides for English HL can uniquely support **close reading**, **process writing**,
**oral preparation**, and **language structure mastery** in ways that static textbooks cannot —
through interactive annotation, adaptive feedback, and multimodal input.

---

## Grade Band Overview

| Grade Band | Phase | Key Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Gr 6–7 | Intermediate | Vocabulary, comprehension, narrative/descriptive writing, core grammar |
| Gr 8–9 | Senior | Literature (novel/drama/poetry), transactional texts, essay writing, complex grammar |
| Gr 10–12 | FET | Literary analysis, critical writing, prescribed texts, Paper 1/2/3 exam skills |

## Recommended Aide Types

### 1. Text Annotation & Close Reading Tool
**Priority: 🔴 High**

A digital reader for comprehension passages or literature extracts that allows learners to
highlight, annotate, and tag text using colour-coded categories.

**Rationale:** Close reading is the most fundamental English HL skill, assessed in every comprehension
and literature question. Research in literacy education consistently shows that active annotation
improves both comprehension accuracy and analytical writing quality. The tool mirrors how expert
readers engage with texts.

**Component ideas:**
- Colour-coded highlight modes: character, theme, tone, figurative language, evidence
- Sticky-note marginal annotations with teacher-comment support
- Pop-up vocabulary definitions on long-press (grade-appropriate dictionary)
- Literary device detection mode: system highlights candidates; learner confirms and names the device
- Preloaded extracts per grade: editorials, speeches, poems, short story openings, drama scenes

---

### 2. Comprehension Practice Engine
**Priority: 🔴 High**

A passage-based question set with instant feedback, structured across the CAPS comprehension
question hierarchy: literal → inferential → critical/evaluative.

**Rationale:** Comprehension is assessed every term Gr6–12 and appears in Paper 1 for all FET
learners. Spaced practice with corrective feedback is among the most evidence-backed methods
for reading skill development. The tool mirrors the mark-allocation logic of CAPS question papers.

**Component ideas:**
- Passages calibrated to readability levels per grade (Gr6 through Gr12)
- Question types: factual retrieval, inference, vocabulary-in-context, tone/attitude, critical opinion
- Answer-reveal with model answer comparison and mark-allocation display
- Summary practice module: identify 5 key points, write in own words, follow CAPS summary rules
- Personal weakness tracker: "You tend to underperform on inference questions"

---

### 3. Process Writing Studio
**Priority: 🔴 High**

A stage-gated writing environment enforcing the CAPS process approach: brainstorm → plan →
draft → revise → edit → final copy.

**Rationale:** CAPS Writing & Presenting explicitly requires learners to move through a writing
process. Most learners skip planning entirely. A stage-gated digital tool enforces the process
with scaffolded prompts at each stage without being restrictive.

**Component ideas:**
- Gr6–9: mind-map brainstorm canvas with guided branching prompts
- FET: structured outline builder (thesis → topic sentences → evidence → conclusion)
- Text-type conventions panel per task: formal letter layout, speech conventions, essay structure
- Self-editing checklist: paragraph unity, capitals, punctuation, spelling, register
- Gr12 writing prompts bank across all Paper 3 text types (narrative, argumentative, reflective, etc.)

---

### 4. Grammar Drill & Language Structures Practice
**Priority: 🔴 High**

Adaptive grammar exercises aligned to the CAPS Language Structures & Conventions syllabus,
covering sentence transformation, parts of speech, tense, punctuation, and syntax.

**Rationale:** Language Structures & Conventions accounts for a substantial mark allocation in
every grade. The CAPS ATP for Gr6 lists nouns, pronouns, adverbs, reported speech, and direct
speech — all of which are systematically testable via drill-with-feedback.

**Component ideas:**
- Sentence transformation: active ↔ passive, direct ↔ reported speech, degrees of comparison
- Tense conversion with context sentences
- Punctuation placement (drag-and-drop colon, semicolon, comma, apostrophe)
- Parts-of-speech identification and labelling games (Gr6–8)
- Grade-banded question banks (Gr6–9 vs. FET difficulty tiers clearly distinguished)

---

### 5. Poetry & Literature Analysis Guides
**Priority: 🟡 Medium**

Guided analysis modules for poetry, drama extracts, and novel chapters, with scaffolded
prompts and worked examples aligned to the Paper 2 contextual-question format.

**Rationale:** Literary analysis is the area where most learners underperform relative to their
understanding of the content. They know the story but cannot write analytically about it.
Scaffolded prompts and worked examples build the skill progressively.

**Component ideas:**
- Poem reader with audio pronunciation for difficult or archaic words
- Stanza-by-stanza guided annotation with literal → figurative → thematic prompts
- Tone/mood selector: "Which word best describes the tone of this stanza?" (with justification)
- Theme identification card sort
- Drama scene analysis: character motivation map, conflict identification, stage-direction annotation

---

### 6. Oral Preparation & Presentation Coach
**Priority: 🟡 Medium**

A guided tool for preparing oral tasks (prepared speech, unprepared reading, debate,
role-play) with sample audio models and a rubric-linked self-assessment checklist.

**Rationale:** Oral tasks are a formal SBA requirement every term. The CAPS Gr12 ATP explicitly
includes oral preparation as an assessed activity. A digital preparation tool with modelled
examples reduces performance anxiety and improves delivery quality.

**Component ideas:**
- Task-type selector: prepared speech, reading aloud, debate, interview, role-play
- Sample audio recordings of model oral performances per task type
- Built-in rehearsal timer calibrated to CAPS time allocations per grade
- Rubric-linked self-checklist: eye contact, pace, pronunciation, volume, content
- Pronunciation audio for vocabulary flagged in the oral task

## Inspirational Examples

- **Actively Learn** — digital reading platform with embedded comprehension and annotation tools;
  widely adopted in English classrooms globally
- **CommonLit** — free literacy platform with levelled texts and CAPS-compatible question structures
- **NoRedInk** — adaptive grammar platform with skill-level tracking; directly inspires the Grammar
  Drill component
- **Newsela** — adjustable-readability text platform for differentiated comprehension practice
- **ReadWriteThink (NCTE)** — free interactive writing tools including story maps and graphic organisers
