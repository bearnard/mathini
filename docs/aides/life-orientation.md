# Learning Aides — Life Orientation / Life Skills (Gr 6–12)

## Subject Overview

Life Skills (Gr6) and Life Orientation (Gr7–12) cover four integrated CAPS domains:
**Development of the Self in Society**, **Social and Environmental Responsibility**,
**Democracy, Human Rights, and Citizenship**, and **Physical Education**. In FET (Gr10–12),
LO carries 75% SBA weighting with no external examination — making it uniquely SBA-driven.
Topics span personal growth, relationships, sexuality, study skills, career guidance, health, and
active citizenship.

LO is fundamentally **reflective, values-oriented, and decision-focused**. Digital aides must
support scenario analysis, self-reflection, and real-world information access without replacing
the relational and embodied elements of the subject. The goal is to make the SBA-dominant
assessment model more engaging and intellectually rigorous.

---

## Grade Band Overview

| Grade Band | Phase | Key Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Gr 6 | Intermediate Life Skills | Personal development, health basics, citizenship, PE |
| Gr 7–9 | Senior Phase LO | Relationships, career awareness, rights, democratic values, health |
| Gr 10–12 | FET LO | Self-management, sexuality/relationships, study skills, career planning, social issues |

## Recommended Aide Types

### 1. Scenario-Based Decision-Making Simulator
**Priority: 🔴 High**

Branching scenario simulations presenting realistic social situations — peer pressure,
relationship boundaries, substance use, online safety — where learners make choices and see
consequences unfold.

**Rationale:** Scenario and case-study analysis is the dominant LO assessment type from Gr6–12.
The CAPS Gr11 ATP explicitly lists "case studies, myth-and-fact items, discussion prompts" as
assessment angles. A digital branching scenario provides safe practice, realistic stakes, and
far greater engagement than text-only case studies.

**Component ideas:**
- Grade-calibrated scenarios:
  - Gr6–7: bullying, friendship, online safety, household responsibilities
  - Gr8–9: relationships, consent, substance pressure, social media, study stress
  - Gr10–12: career decisions, intimate relationships, substance abuse, civic participation
- Multiple decision points with branching outcomes (no single "game over" state)
- Reflection prompt after every branch: "Why did you choose that? What might you do differently?"
- SA-specific settings: township, urban, farm school, peri-urban contexts represented
- Teacher-visible reflection logs for SBA evidence

---

### 2. Career Exploration Compass
**Priority: 🔴 High**

An interactive career discovery tool linking learner interests, subject choices, and values
to career clusters and South African educational pathways.

**Rationale:** Career guidance is a core LO strand from Gr8 upward and is assessed as a major
written task in FET. The CAPS Gr12 LO ATP includes post-school planning as a formal assessment
topic. Many SA learners have extremely limited career exposure due to socio-economic context;
a digital tool can expose them to 50+ career paths with SA-relevant qualification and institution
information.

**Component ideas:**
- Short interest inventory → suggested career clusters (RIASEC-lite model)
- Career profile cards: job description, required Gr12 subjects, typical pathway, SA institutions,
  approximate graduate salary range
- Subject-to-career mapper: "If I take Physical Sciences and Maths, I can pursue…"
- NSFAS, bursary, and TVET information linked to FET career exploration
- Short audio or video testimonials: SA voices from diverse career paths

---

### 3. Study Skills & Self-Management Toolkit
**Priority: 🔴 High**

A practical toolkit of study planning, time management, and exam-preparation tools
directly aligned to the CAPS LO study skills strand.

**Rationale:** Study skills are explicitly assessed in LO every year at every phase. The CAPS Gr11
ATP lists "study-plan reviews, stress-management tasks, self-evaluation checklists" as formal
assessment angles. Beyond LO, this toolkit has direct value across every other PrepPals subject.

**Component ideas:**
- Drag-and-drop study timetable builder calibrated to CAPS term dates
- Exam countdown and subject-prioritisation planner
- Pomodoro-style focus timer with break reminders
- Note-taking method explainer: Cornell, mind map, outline, flashcard — each with interactive examples
- Stress thermometer self-assessment with coping-strategy suggestions
- Personal goal tracker with weekly reflection prompts

---

### 4. Health & Wellness Information Hub
**Priority: 🟡 Medium**

Age-appropriate, SA-contextualised health information covering reproductive health, STIs,
HIV/AIDS, mental wellness, and substance abuse — with a myth-busting component.

**Rationale:** Health content is mandated in CAPS LO from Gr8 upward and is listed in the CAPS
Gr11 ATP as covering "healthy relationships, sexuality, consent, reproductive health." Sensitive
topics are often under-addressed in classroom settings; a private, accurate digital resource fills
a genuine information gap.

**Component ideas:**
- Age-gated content (Gr6–7 age-appropriate vs. Gr8–12 version)
- SA statistics contextualised: HIV prevalence, teen pregnancy, substance abuse trends
- Myth-busting quiz format for common health misconceptions
- Mental health section: anxiety, depression, grief — signs, coping strategies, where to get help
  in SA (SADAG helpline, Childline, school counsellor guidance)
- Anonymous question prompt for sensitive topics (pre-moderated response library)

---

### 5. Rights & Citizenship Explainer
**Priority: 🟡 Medium**

An interactive module covering the South African Constitution, Bill of Rights, democratic
structures, and active citizenship pathways.

**Rationale:** Democracy, Human Rights, and Citizenship is a mandatory CAPS LO strand. Many
learners hold strong values but lack constitutional knowledge to contextualise them. The Bill of
Rights is directly assessable as a case-study and source-based task topic.

**Component ideas:**
- Bill of Rights explorer: click any right → plain-language explanation → SA case study example
- "Is this a rights violation?" case-study challenge with multiple SA scenarios
- SA government structure: three spheres (national/provincial/local), three arms (executive/
  legislative/judicial) — visual explainer
- How to vote and register: practical guide for Gr12 learners approaching voting age
- Youth activism case studies: #FeesMustFall, Fridays for Future SA, community action examples

---

### 6. Physical Education Reflection Log
**Priority: 🟢 Low**

A digital PE participation diary where learners log activity, set fitness goals, and reflect
on progress — aligned to the CAPS Physical Education Task (PET) evidence requirements.

**Rationale:** The PET is assessed throughout the year across Gr10–12 using participation rubrics
and fitness logs (CAPS Gr11 ATP: "fitness logs, technique observations, participation rubrics").
A digital log removes paper-log friction and generates summaries ready for teacher review.

**Component ideas:**
- Activity type selector with SA-relevant options: soccer, netball, running, aerobics, swimming, dance
- Fitness goal setter with weekly progress tracking graph
- Weekly reflection prompt linked to CAPS PET self-assessment criteria
- Export/print PET evidence summary for teacher submission

## Inspirational Examples

- **Soul City Institute (iNguvu)** — SA-specific health and life skills digital content for secondary
  learners; directly relevant content model for the health hub
- **Mindset Learn (SA)** — SA nonprofit producing CAPS-aligned LO video content widely used
  in under-resourced schools; demonstrates high demand for digital LO resources
- **Khan Academy College & Career** — career exploration and planning tools that inspire the
  Career Compass design
- **FunDza Literacy Trust** — SA digital storytelling platform; scenario-based stories model the
  branching narrative approach used in the decision-making simulator
- **SADAG (SA Depression and Anxiety Group)** — SA mental health resources that can be integrated
  directly into the health hub component
