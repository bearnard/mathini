# Grade 12 English HL listening and speaking (Paper 4): teacher-check list

- **Paper 4 marks.** Prepared speech 2 × 10 = 20, unprepared speech 15, listening for comprehension 15, total 50 of the 300 end-of-year marks (CAPS 4.x, "Oral Assessment Tasks: Paper 4"). Confirm this is still the NSC split.
- **Oral tasks by term.** T1 Task 1 listening and Task 4 speech, T2 Task 7 speech, T3 Task 9 prepared speech introducing a speaker or a vote of thanks, T4 moderation. Schools may schedule differently.
- **Suggested durations** (CAPS table): prepared speech 4 to 5 minutes, unprepared speech 2 to 3, introducing a speaker or vote of thanks 4 to 5, dialogue 6 to 8, interview 10 to 15, debate, panel or group discussion 20 to 30. Confirm against the school's oral guidelines.
- **Listening task.** The lesson says Grade 12 uses a text of about 400 words or an audio clip of about three minutes, read or played at least twice (CAPS table). Confirm.
- **Debate procedure** is taught as the CAPS states it (affirmative begins and ends, burden of proof, a 5 to 10 minute break before rebuttals, two rebuttals each).
- **Examples** use made-up names (a nurse called Sister Dlamini, a school litter topic) and no real people.
- The existing text-only lesson `gr12-oral-trial-preparation-t3` is unchanged.
