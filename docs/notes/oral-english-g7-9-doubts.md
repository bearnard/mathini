To confirm with a teacher:
- The terms are placed to spread the oral skills across the year; the CAPS term for each oral task was not available in the audit, so a teacher should confirm them (a move later keeps ids and URLs).
- Rubric criteria are general (content, structure, language, delivery, courtesy, time); no marks, weightings or official rubric wording are given.
- Pace figures (120 to 150 words a minute) and time limits (a 3 or 4 minute speech, a 14 minute debate, a 24 minute panel) are teaching examples, not CAPS durations.
- Poetry reading aloud is left to the G8/G9 poetry strand (bron); the reading-aloud lesson uses prose and general passages only.
- The written interview (G8 T2), the written speech (G7 T4) and the written minutes (G7 T3) stay separate; these lessons cover the spoken skills.
