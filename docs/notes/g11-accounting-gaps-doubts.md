# Grade 11 Accounting gaps: doubts and decisions

## GAAP principles (T1), Non-profit organisations and Analysis Cash Book (T2), Statement of Receipts and Payments (T2)
- CAPS (Grade 11 Accounting): Term 1 lists the GAAP concepts historical cost, prudence, materiality, business entity, going concern and matching. Term 2 lists clubs: accumulated fund, membership, entrance and affiliation fees, honorarium, surplus and deficit, the Analysis Cash Book, posting to the ledger, the Trial Balance and the Statement of Receipts and Payments, with receipts against income and payments against expenses.
- All figures belong to an imaginary Riverside Netball Club (`club11.ts`). Opening: bank 8 000, equipment 4 000, creditors 1 000, so accumulated fund 11 000. Receipts 19 000, payments 12 000, closing bank 15 000. Trial balance Dr = Cr = 30 000; closing fund 22 000 equals bank 15 000 plus equipment 7 000. A test recomputes every total independently.
- Statement of Receipts and Payments is taught as a cash summary; the Income and Expenditure Statement and Balance Sheet of clubs are not in this branch (not in the audit gap).
- GAAP: stock at the lower of cost and net realisable value; matching shown with a prepaid expense; materiality shown as a simple percentage-of-turnover rule of thumb (1%), explicitly marked as a guide and not a legal threshold.
- Wave B (nothing moved): Partnership Financial Statements and Notes (our T2, CAPS T1); FIFO and weighted average (Grade 12).
