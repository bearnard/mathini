# Grade 9 Technology: doubts

## Mechanical control systems (Term 2)
- CAPS Gr 9 T2 only says "Investigate: learners find out about the following mechanical control systems: ratchet and pawl, disc brake, bicycle brake, cleat." The depth is mine. The lesson stays at "what it does, how it works, one calculation each" and does not go into friction coefficients or cleat types.
- Ratchet and pawl: the picture and text say the wheel turns freely clockwise and is locked anticlockwise. The saw-tooth direction is a drawing choice; the opposite handedness is equally correct. The teeth-to-degrees sum (360 / teeth) is exact.
- Disc brake: the example uses a master piston of 2 cm² and pad pistons of 6 cm² with 100 N. A real brake has a lever or pedal first, so these figures are a simplified model of Pascal's principle (the same idea as the hydraulics lesson), not a real brake spec.
- Bicycle brake: "cable rim brake" is what CAPS means by "bicycle brake"; the lever mechanical advantage of 4 (8 cm effort, 2 cm load) is an illustration, since real levers vary. I also mention hydraulic disc brakes on bicycles only in the mistake box, to separate the two.
- Cleat: the picture is a simplified sketch of a horn cleat with the rope wrapped once, a figure of eight and a hitch; real cleating has a specific last turn that locks. Please check the wording for the hitch against a sailing source. The picture is described in words in its aria label.
- Mini-PAT 2 (integrated systems) and evaluating everyday mechanisms (can opener, egg beater, ratchet spanner) are separate CAPS items and are not in this lesson.
