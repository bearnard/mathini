# Grade 8 English HL poetry strand: points for a teacher to confirm

Source: DBE CAPS English Home Language Senior Phase Grades 7-9 (sp_eng_b): poetry (key features; internal structure: figures of speech and imagery, rhyme, rhythm; external structure: lines, stanzas, typography; figurative meaning; mood). Four lessons were added beside the umbrella lesson "Poetry and Close Reading". No prescribed or post-1900 poem is used. Every poem is either an original poem written for the lesson (labelled "Original poem written for this lesson") or a public-domain poem with author, title and year, and the words are asserted in `src/g8Poetry.test.ts`. Questions in the banks use only short original poems or lines written for the question, so no lesson poem is repeated in a question.

| 5 | William Blake, "The Tyger" (1794), first stanza | Sound and structure | The spelling "Tyger" and the original punctuation. |

## Other points

| 6 | Sound and structure | "Eye" and "symmetry" are called a rhyme pair in Blake's stanza (AABB). | It is a near (historical) rhyme: the word "symmetry" was pronounced to rhyme with "eye" in Blake's time. A teacher may prefer to call it a near rhyme. |
