# Grade 8 English HL transactional texts: points for a teacher to confirm

Source: DBE CAPS English Home Language Senior Phase Grades 7-9, Grade 8 teaching plans; `audit/caps-alignment.md`. All model texts are original, with invented names, places and figures.

## Branch A (Terms 1 and 2)
| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Friendly letter | Layout puts the sender's address and the date at the top right, "Dear [name]," as the greeting and "Your friend," as the closing. | Textbooks vary on commas, address layout and closings. |
| 2 | Friendly letter | Writing from a visual stimulus is taught as look, choose, plan, write, check. | CAPS gives the task without steps. |
| 3 | Investigative report | Parts: title, introduction, method, findings, conclusion, recommendation. Findings use invented figures (22 + 10 + 8 = 40; 55%). | Textbooks name the parts slightly differently. |
| 4 | Investigative report | Impersonal style and the past tense for findings. | CAPS asks for an investigative report without a format. |
| 5 | Instructions | Imperative verbs, numbered steps, sequence words, a warning. | None. |
| 6 | Directions | Turns, positions, landmarks and distances. | None. |
| 7 | Dialogue | Play-style layout (speaker name and colon, directions in brackets). | Some textbooks use quotation marks in a narrative dialogue. |
| 8 | Interview | A written interview uses Q and A with open questions. | CAPS says "written interview" without a format. |

## Branch B (Terms 3 and 4)
| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Letter to the press | Layout includes the editor's address, "Dear Editor," a "Re:" heading and "Yours faithfully". | Newspapers and textbooks differ on the heading. |
| 10 | Article | Headline, by-line, lead, body in order of importance, quotations. | CAPS names the text type without a format. |
| 11 | Diary entry | Date and time, "Dear Diary," events, feelings, reflection. | "Dear Diary" is optional. |
| 12 | News report | Objective, facts first, named sources, both sides. The difference from an article is explained in a short table. | Textbooks draw the line differently. |
