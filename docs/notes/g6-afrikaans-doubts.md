# Grade 6 Afrikaans FAL text types (Wave A): items for a first-language teacher to check

Nothing here has been checked by a first-language speaker. Every Afrikaans word, phrase and sentence shown in a lesson is listed with its English gloss (a test fails if a lesson shows an Afrikaans phrase that is not on this list). The Afrikaans is kept to very simple, standard sentences for Grade 6; anything uncertain is listed below rather than taught. Source for the text types: the CAPS Afrikaans First Additional Language Grade 6 plans (`ip_afr`) and the audit. Names and places are invented.

## Points that need a teacher's opinion

| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story | `Daar was eens ’n seun.`, `Hy het Sipho geheet.`, `Op ’n dag het hy sy skoen verloor.`, `Gelukkig het sy vriend die skoen gekry.` | Regular het + ge- perfect; please confirm these read naturally for Grade 6. |
| 2 | Factual report | `slot` for the ending of a report and `inleiding` for the introduction | Textbooks may use `gevolgtrekking` or `afsluiting`; `slot` was chosen because it is shorter. |
| 3 | Factual report | `Die biblioteek is oop van agt tot drie.` | Standard; confirm the time wording. |
| 4 | Process | `Eerste sny ek die brood.` (verb second after an order word) and `toebroodjie` for sandwich | `Eerste` versus `Eerstens`; `toebroodjie` is the usual word. |
| 5 | Process | `Die brood is op die bord.` | `bord` means a plate; some would say `snyplank` for a board. The English gloss says plate. |
| 6 | Diary | `Liewe Dagboek,` and `Môre sal ek my boeke saamneem.` | Standard; `saamneem` is one word. |
| 7 | Opinion | `Ek dink sport is belangrik.` (no `dat`) and `Ek stem nie saam nie.` | The short word order without `dat` is common; a textbook may prefer `dat`. |
| 8 | Play | The layout `Thandi: Goeie môre!` and the word `gesprek` for conversation | Stage directions (`toneelaanwysings`) are not taught here. |
| 9 | News report and poster | `skoonmaakdag`, `Kom saam!`, `Almal is welkom.`, `Die skool hou ’n skoonmaakdag.` | Standard. |
| 10 | All | The question prompts `Watter Afrikaanse woord beteken ...?` and `Watter sin sê ...?` | Simple standard wording copied from the Grade 8 lessons. |
