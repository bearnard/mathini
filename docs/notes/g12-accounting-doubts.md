# Grade 12 Accounting: close corporations, internal control, internal audit — teacher check

- **Close corporations are no longer registered.** Since the Companies Act 2008 took effect (1 May 2011) no NEW close corporations can be registered, but existing CCs carry on. This is why CAPS still teaches them. The lesson says so in its first slide. A teacher should confirm that wording.
- **Terminology.** The lesson uses CAPS terms: Income Statement and Balance Sheet for the CC (not the company statement names), members' interest, members' contribution, distribution to members. Confirm the term pairs (company ↔ CC) match the textbook the class uses.
- **Tax.** The income tax amount is given in the worked example; no tax rate or table is taught. Confirm that the examiners give the tax amount in the same way.
- **Member limits and registration rules** are deliberately not taught (not in CAPS Grade 12 depth).
- **Audit sampling.** Random and systematic sampling, error rate and estimated errors are taught with a simple example (3 of 20, 200 invoices). CAPS wording is brief; confirm the depth.
- **Internal control** examples (cash count shortage R50, division of duties) are illustrative, not from a past paper.
- Later PARTIAL gaps offered, not built: GAAP principles, debtors age analysis, cash budget.
