# Grade 10 Life Orientation: plan (approved by the orchestrator, msg 834)

Owner of this wave item: scholar-bron. Date: 2026-11-16.

## Source

- **CAPS FET Life Orientation Grades 10-12** (DBE): Section 2.4 "Weighting of topics" and Section 3.2 "Annual Teaching Plan, Grade 10" (Terms 1 to 4).
  https://www.education.gov.za/portals/0/cd/national%20curriculum%20statements%20and%20vocational/caps%20fet%20_%20life%20orientation%20_%20gr%2010-12%20_%20web_e6b3.pdf
  Extracted with `pdftotext -layout` (40 pages); every topic list below is taken from that text, not from the repo's older `syllabus/grade10/life-orientation/*.md` notes, which are generic and do NOT match the CAPS plan (they invent topics such as "Risk behaviour and media influence" and "Wellbeing and study skills" in Term 1; the real CAPS plan has Study skills in Term 2 and Democracy in Terms 1 and 4).
- Grade 10 weighting (hours of 66 contact hours): Development of the self in society 8, Social and environmental responsibility 4, Democracy and human rights 7, Careers and career choices 11, Study skills 3, Physical Education 33 (practical; theory only).
- Formal assessment: T1 written task + PET, T2 mid-year exam + PET, T3 project + PET, T4 end-of-year exam + PET. The sequence of topics within a term is not fixed.

- Generated files regenerated by the repo scripts: `taskIndex.generated.ts`, `topicNames.generated.ts`, `curriculum-content.generated.json`, site stats.
- Pictures: existing LO diagram components only (`src/components/diagrams/lo/`: StepFlow, Branches, Hub, Zones, Compare, Tiers, LinkedPairs, MatchColumns, CountBars, TimeBar, PlanBar ...). - No end-of-term tests and no exam-practice scope exist for Life Orientation in any other grade (Grade 11 LO has neither), so none are added; see "Questions for you".

## Accuracy rules for this subject

- Only CAPS-stated content plus facts I can check against a primary source. Legal and institutional facts (Constitution, Bill of Rights section numbers, CRC 1989, African Charter 1990, CEDAW 1979, SAQA, NQF with 10 levels, tax, ages of consent) are listed in `docs/notes/g10-life-orientation-doubts.md` with their source, and anything I cannot confirm from a primary source goes in that file for a teacher review pack and NOT in the bank.
- Sexuality and health topics (topics 2, 11, 12): CAPS-aligned, factual, age-appropriate (Grade 10, about 15 to 16 years), no graphic detail; CAPS lists abstinence as a value, so it is taught as the CAPS values list and alongside accurate STI/contraception facts only as far as the CAPS list asks. These three topics get a teacher review entry.
- Religions (topic 16): descriptive only, neutral, no ranking; facts (founders, scriptures, places of worship, major festivals) kept to what is uncontroversial and checked.
- Current facts (legal ages, grants, tax thresholds, scarce skills lists) are NOT stated as numbers unless dated and sourced; otherwise written generally.
- No invented statistics, quotes or case studies with real people; scenario names are SA names, invented and labelled as examples.
- Decimal comma wherever a decimal appears; currency in R.
