# Grade 9 Afrikaans FAL transactional texts (Wave A): items for a first-language teacher to check

Nothing here has been checked by a first-language speaker. Every Afrikaans word, phrase and sentence shown in a lesson is listed with its English gloss (a test fails if a lesson shows an Afrikaans phrase that is not on this list). Source for the text types: DBE CAPS Afrikaans FAL Grades 7-9 (sp_afr_b). Sentences are short and regular on purpose.

## Points that need a teacher's opinion

| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diary | `Liewe Dagboek,` as the opening, and the order `Gister het ek gehardloop.` (verb second after the time word). | Standard; please confirm that the opening is natural for a Grade 9 diary. |
| 2 | Diary | The past is taught as `het` + ge- participle only (gespeel, gekyk, gewerk). | Simplification: some verbs form the participle differently (for example eindig: geëindig; begin: begin). The lesson uses only regular ones. |
| 3 | Constitution | Headings Naam, Doel, Lidmaatskap, Vergaderings, Wysigings; `Aangeneem op 3 Februarie.` for the date of adoption. | CAPS lists name, membership, terms of reference, date, signature; the Afrikaans headings are the author's choice. |
| 4 | Constitution | `moet` for a duty and `mag` for a permission; `Die grondwet kan verander word.` | Standard; `gewysig` is the more formal word. |
| 5 | Policy | `Selfoonbeleid` and `Selfone moet gedurende lesse afgeskakel wees.` | Please confirm the compound and `afgeskakel wees`. |
| 6 | Will | `bemaak` (leave, bequeath), `aanstel` (appoint) as in `Ek stel my broer as eksekuteur aan.`, and the key words testament, erfgenaam, eksekuteur, boedel, getuies. | Standard legal vocabulary; no legal detail is taught and the lesson says a real will is written with an attorney. |
| 7 | CV and covering letter | Headings Persoonlike besonderhede, Kwalifikasies, Werkondervinding, Verwysings (referees). | Textbooks differ; `Verwysings` is widely used for referees. |
| 8 | Covering letter | Greeting `Geagte Meneer/Mevrou` and closing `Vriendelike groete` (the same forms as the existing formal-letter lesson). | The closings `Die uwe` and `Hoogagtend` are also taught in some schools; they are not used here. |
| 9 | Both | Names and numbers are invented (`082 000 0000`, `Hillcrest Hoërskool`, `Mev. N. Dlamini`). | Placeholders so that no real person is named. |
| 10 | Application letter | `u` is taught as the formal `you` (with `jou` as the informal wrong choice in a formal letter), and the three-part plan: opening (the post and where it was advertised), middle (experience and qualities), ending (CV attached, interview, thanks). | The plan is the author's; CAPS names the text type only. |
| 11 | E-mail | Lines `Aan`, `Kopie`, `Onderwerp` (To, CC, Subject); formal `Geagte Mev. Dlamini` / `Vriendelike groete` against informal `Hallo Zinzi` / `Groete`; `Donderdag` and `Mag ons asseblief ... gebruik?`. | Standard; please confirm that `Kopie` is the usual label for CC in Afrikaans mail programs (some show `KK`). |

## Wave A gap: Brief van waardering (letter of appreciation, Quarter 1)

Nothing here has been checked by a first-language speaker. CAPS Grade 9 Quarter 1 names "write a letter of appreciation for help or advice received" with correct format, purpose, main and supporting ideas, register, logical order and conjunctions.

| # | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | `Baie dankie vir u hulp.` and `Ek wil u bedank vir u advies.` as the opening, `u` for the reader. | Standard; please confirm that `bedank` is natural here for a Grade 9 learner. |
| 18 | `Geagte Mev. Pillay` as the greeting of a letter to a teacher and `Vriendelike groete` as the closing, as in the other formal lessons. | A warmer `Liewe Mev. Pillay` and `Groete` are also used for a letter of thanks; this lesson keeps to the polite forms. |
| 19 | `omdat` and `daarom` as the reason and result words, `Ek is dankbaar omdat u my gehelp het.` | Standard; the word order after `omdat` (verb at the end) is not taught here. |
| 20 | `My punte het beter geword.` and `U het my gewys hoe om 'n leerrooster te maak.` as the supporting ideas. | `leerrooster` (revision timetable) is a compound that some teachers would write as `studierooster`; please confirm. |
| 21 | `Dankie, maat` is shown as the informal wrong choice for a teacher. | Standard colloquial; please confirm the register note. |
| 22 | The name `Mev. Pillay` is invented (no real person), as are the model letter details. | None. |
