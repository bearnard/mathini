# Grade 11 History, Topic 5 (Term 4): points for a teacher to check

Two lessons added by `feat/g11-his-t4-resistance` for CAPS Grade 11 Topic 5 (resistance to apartheid, 1948 to 1964): "Resistance to Apartheid 1948 to 1960" and "Sharpeville, the Armed Struggle and the Rivonia Trial" (14 questions each). Every source box is an original or paraphrased description written for the lesson; the only quotation is the first line of the Freedom Charter.

## Dates and numbers (also asserted independently in `src/g11HisResistance.test.ts`)

Youth League 1944; Programme of Action 1949; Group Areas Act and Suppression of Communism Act 1950; Defiance Campaign 26 June 1952; Freedom Charter 26 June 1955; Treason Trial arrests December 1956, acquittal 1961, 156 accused; women's march 9 August 1956 (about 20 000); PAC formed 1959; Sharpeville 21 March 1960 (69 killed); state of emergency 30 March 1960; ANC and PAC banned 8 April 1960; Umkhonto we Sizwe first attacks 16 December 1961; Sabotage Act 1962; Liliesleaf raid 11 July 1963; Mandela's statement 20 April 1964; verdict 12 June 1964 (eight men, life imprisonment).

## Points a teacher should confirm

1. **Death toll at Sharpeville**: 69 is the figure in the standard record; some sources give a different count of the wounded, so the lesson gives no number for the wounded.
2. **Who called the Sharpeville protest**: the PAC. The ANC ran its own anti-pass campaign in the same month, which the Mistake box mentions.
3. **Women's march numbers**: "about 20 000" is the usual figure; accounts vary.
4. **Treason Trial**: "all were cleared" (found not guilty, 1961). The trial began with 156 accused and the number fell during the trial; the lesson uses the 156 who were arrested.
5. **Rivonia**: the lesson says "eight men sentenced to life imprisonment" and "the state asked for the death sentence". It does not name the accused or the prison, because the accused were held in different prisons.
6. **Umkhonto we Sizwe's first targets**: the lesson says state targets chosen to avoid loss of life "at the start". Later phases are not covered.
7. **Mandela's statement**: the Source B paraphrase keeps the three ideas (against white domination and against black domination; a free society in harmony; prepared to die for it). It is a paraphrase, not a quotation.
8. **Scope**: nothing here is Grade 12 content (no Black Consciousness, no 1976, no UDF). The old "Resistance to Racism" and "Liberation Movements" lessons are hidden in this branch, with 308 redirects.
