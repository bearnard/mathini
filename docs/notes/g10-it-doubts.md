# Grade 10 Information Technology, 2024 amendment, branch A: points for a teacher to confirm

Source: the 2024 CAPS amendment, Grade 10 teaching plan (DBE Circular S9 of 2025: it applies to Grades 10 to 12 from January 2026). Comparison: `audit/it-2024-amendment-check.md`.

| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Computers and software | Shareware, freeware, FOSS and proprietary are each one line. | Terms are used loosely; the amendment lists them without definitions. |
| 2 | File management | File specifications use Windows paths (`D:\School\Maths\Homework.docx`). | Other systems use other separators. |
| 3 | Algorithms | IPO table, flow chart shapes (oval, rectangle, parallelogram, diamond) and trace table examples are recomputed in `g10ItAmendmentA.test.ts`. | Textbooks vary in flow chart shapes. |
| 4 | Licences | Copyleft: "free to use and change if the changed version stays free". | Simplified. |
| 5 | Digital divide | Reasons are cost, no power or signal, lack of skills and language. | The amendment names the divide and its reasons without a list. |
| 6 | Hardware | A laser printer is "fast and sharp, used in offices"; an ink printer is "cheaper to buy". | General comparison, not fixed. |
| 7 | Operating systems | Windows, Linux, iOS and Android are named as the amendment names them. | None. |
| 8 | Networks | Wired is "often more secure and stable"; wireless is "more open to interference". | Depends on set-up. |
| 9 | E-mail | Cc is "visible to all"; Bcc is "hidden". | None. |
| 10 | Polya | The four steps follow the amendment (Polya, 1957). | None. |
| 11 | Ergonomics | The 20-20-20 rule is given as a common guideline. | Not CAPS wording. |

Term move in branch A (no content change): Networks and Data Handling to Term 2.
