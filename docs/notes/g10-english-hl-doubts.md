## Not covered on purpose

- **Prescribed (set) works.** Not named, not quoted. Novel, drama and poetry lessons teach the technique with original extracts.
- **Oral tasks (Paper 4).** The app cannot mark delivery; later lessons teach the features and conventions only.
- **Afrikaans and isiXhosa.** Nothing in this subject needs a native-speaker check (English only).

## Texts

All passages, the poem and the letters are original (written for these lessons). The three annotated opening lines and the descriptive sentences are original. No public-domain text has been used yet; when poetry lessons use one, the author and date will be listed here.
