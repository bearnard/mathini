## Care and accuracy

- **First aid and health** are the highest-risk content. I give only widely taught, simple steps (cool a burn under running water for at least 10 minutes; press on a bleeding cut with a clean cloth; never make a person vomit after poisoning; for a snake bite keep still and get to a hospital; for choking, call for an adult and emergency help) and no medicine names or doses. Each lesson says "tell an adult and call for help". Every step is listed in `docs/notes/g6-life-skills-doubts.md` for a nurse or first-aid teacher to confirm. Nothing is presented as a replacement for training.
- **Rites of passage** are written respectfully and generally (examples from several traditions, "families differ"), and listed in the doubts file for a teacher or cultural reviewer.
- **Dates and facts** (national days, symbols, emergency numbers) sit in one facts file and are asserted in tests: Human Rights Day 21 March, Freedom Day 27 April, Africa Day 25 May, Women's Day 9 August, Heritage Day 24 September, Reconciliation Day 16 December, Mandela Day 18 July; the flag adopted on 27 April 1994 and the coat of arms of 2000 with its motto `!ke e: /xarra //ke`. CAPS also lists Children's Day, which has no fixed public-holiday date, so no date is given.
- No names of real children; no scary or graphic detail.
