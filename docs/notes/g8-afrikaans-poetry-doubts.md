# Grade 8 Afrikaans FAL poetry (Wave A): items for a first-language teacher to check

Nothing here has been checked by a first-language speaker. Every Afrikaans word, phrase and sentence shown in a lesson is listed with its English gloss (a test fails if a lesson shows an Afrikaans phrase that is not on this list). All poems are ORIGINAL simple Afrikaans poems written for the lessons and labelled "Oorspronklike gedig, vir hierdie les geskryf"; no published Afrikaans poem is used. Source: DBE CAPS Afrikaans FAL Grades 7-9 (sp_afr_b), poetry strand (key features, figures of speech and imagery, rhyme, rhythm, stanzas, mood, message; own poem: 2 verses in Grade 7, 2-3 in Grade 8, 3-6 in Grade 9). Each lesson has 4 slides and 12 questions.

## The poems, line by line, with an English meaning

| Poem | Afrikaans lines | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Gedig A | Die markplein brul soos 'n leeu, / 'n duisend stemme skree my naam, / die motors toet en die remme gil, / en die hele stad wag vir my. | The market square roars like a lion, a thousand voices shout my name, the cars hoot and the brakes scream, and the whole city waits for me. |
| Gedig C | Die nag is 'n swart kombers oor die dorp, / die maan kyk stil op ons neer. | The night is a black blanket over the town, the moon looks down quietly at us. |
| Gedig B | Tik-tak, tik-tak, die horlosie praat, / drup-drup, die reën val op die dak, / en die kat skrik wakker: miaau! | Tick-tock, tick-tock, the clock talks, drip-drip, the rain falls on the roof, and the cat wakes with a fright: meow! |
| Gedig D | Die berge staan so stil en hoog, / die rivier loop stadig deur die dal, / die voëls sing saam met die son, / en die wind waai sag oor al. | The mountains stand so still and high, the river flows slowly through the valley, the birds sing along with the sun, and the wind blows softly over everything. |
| Gedig E | Ek sit onder 'n groot, groen boom, / en daar begin ek rustig droom. / Die voëls vlieg hoog in die lug, / en my gedagtes maak 'n vlug. | I sit under a big green tree, and there I begin to dream calmly. The birds fly high in the sky, and my thoughts take flight. |
| Gedig F | Reën, reën, gaan weg, / kom weer 'n ander dag. / Reën, reën, gaan weg, / die kinders wil speel. | Rain, rain, go away, come again another day. Rain, rain, go away, the children want to play. |
| Gedig G | Die laaste dag van die vakansie breek aan, / my tas staan gepak by die deur. / Ek tel die ure soos geld wat val, / en wens daar was nog meer. | The last day of the holiday dawns, my bag stands packed by the door. I count the hours like money that falls, and wish there were more. |
| Gedig H | My ouma se hande is warm soos brood, / haar lag is 'n ketel wat sing. /  / In die aand sê sy die sterre moet slaap, / en die donker is nie so lank nie. | My gran's hands are warm like bread, her laugh is a kettle that sings. In the evening she tells the stars to sleep, and the dark is not so long. |
