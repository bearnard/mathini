# Grades 7 to 9 Afrikaans FAL oral skills: items for a first-language teacher to check

Nothing here has been checked by a first-language speaker. Every Afrikaans word, phrase and sentence shown in a lesson is listed with its English gloss (a test fails if a lesson shows an Afrikaans phrase that is not in this file). Orchestrator instruction: simple standard Afrikaans in the existing house style, every phrase on this list.

## Points that need a teacher's opinion

| # | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `hardoplees` for reading aloud, `spoed` (pace), `pouse` (pause), `toon` (tone), `oogkontak` (eye contact) | Standard words; please confirm `toon` for tone of voice. |
| 2 | `stelling` for a debate motion; `voorstanders` and `teenstanders` for the two sides; `weerlegging` for rebuttal; `tydhouer` for timekeeper | Please confirm the usual classroom terms for a debate (some books say `mosie`, `voor` and `teen`). |
| 3 | `skooldrag` for school uniform; `Skooldrag moet verpligtend wees.` | Please confirm `skooldrag` and `verpligtend` for compulsory. |
| 4 | `Val niemand in die rede nie.` for do not interrupt anyone | Standard idiom `in die rede val`; please confirm the word order. |
| 5 | `retoriese vraag`, `oproep tot aksie`, `herhaling`, `kernwoorde`, `kaartjies` (cue cards) | Please confirm the usual terms; `kaartjies` could also be `aantekenkaartjies`. |
| 6 | `onderhoudvoerder` (interviewer), `oop vraag`, `geslote vraag`, `opvolgvraag`; `Dankie dat u tyd vir my gemaak het.` | Please confirm the compound words and the formal `u`. |
| 7 | `besonderhede`, `hoofgedagte`, `opsomming`, `standpunt`, `vooroordeel`, `verskriklik` | Standard; please confirm. |
| 8 | `oortuig`, `feite`, `emosie`, `geloofwaardigheid` and the examples about recycling | Please confirm that the examples read naturally. |
| 9 | `Mevrou die Voorsitter`, `punt van orde`, `Ek stel voor dat ...`, `12 stem vir, 7 teen en 3 weerhou.`, `Die voorstel is aanvaar.`, `weerhou` for abstain | Please confirm the meeting language. |
| 10 | `paneelbespreking` and `bewyse` | Please confirm. |
| 11 | All | Names and numbers are invented; timings (120 to 150 words a minute, a 24 minute panel) are teaching examples, not CAPS durations; the lessons carry no marks. The questions are asked in Afrikaans, with English meanings in the explanations. |
