# Grade 10 IT: the 2024 amendment (plan, two branches)

Source: `audit/it-2024-amendment-check.md` section 4; the amendment's Grade 10 teaching plan (txt lines 163-530). Applies to Grade 10 from January 2026 (DBE Circular S9 of 2025). Our Grade 10 has 8 broad lessons; the amendment lists about 22 sub-topics, so most work is additive.

## Branch A (Terms 1 and 2): 10 new lessons x 14 questions = 140, ids prefixed `gr10-`
| Id | Term | Amendment content |
|---|---|---|
| `gr10-computers-software-ict-systems-t1` | T1 | Types of computers (desktop, laptop, tablet, smartphone, server, embedded), hardware vs software, system and application software, shareware, freeware, FOSS, proprietary, ICT system and the information processing cycle |
| `gr10-file-management-t1` | T1 | File specification (drive, path, name, extension), folders and hierarchy, file manager, reasons for a structure, copy, move, rename |
| `gr10-algorithms-ipo-trace-tables-t1` | T1 | What an algorithm is, IPO tables, flow charts, trace tables, swap, smallest and largest, aggregates, even, factor, VAT |
| `gr10-licences-digital-divide-t1` | T1 Social | Licences (creative commons, copyleft), piracy and copyright, the digital divide, digital citizenship and footprint, economic reasons for computers |
| `gr10-hardware-devices-t2` | T2 | Input, output, storage and input/output devices, system unit, ports and connectors, primary vs secondary memory, desktop vs mobile |
| `gr10-operating-systems-utilities-drivers-t2` | T2 | Role and types of operating system, examples, utility programs, device drivers |
| `gr10-networks-overview-t2` | T2 | Reasons, components, media, PAN/HAN/LAN/WAN, Internet as a WAN, client-server vs peer-to-peer |
| `gr10-e-communication-email-netiquette-t2` | T2 | E-communication tools, e-mail (To, Cc, Bcc, attachments, subject), netiquette, accuracy, time, distance and cost |
| `gr10-problem-solving-polya-user-stories-t2` | T2 | Polya's four steps, user stories, noun-verb analysis, acceptance tests |
| `gr10-ergonomics-green-health-t2` | T2 Social | Ergonomics, green computing, health issues |

Term move: Networks and Data Handling T3 to T2 (the amendment's networks overview is Term 2).
