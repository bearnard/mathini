# CAT 2024 amendment: additive theory lessons (plan, three branches, in this order)

Source: `audit/cat-2024-amendment-check.md`; DBE Circular S9 of 2025. No hides, no term moves. Same lesson pattern and gates as the IT work.

## Branch 1, Grade 12 (4 lessons x 14 = 56)
- `gr12-cloud-grid-streaming-t2` (T2): grid and cloud computing, streaming vs downloading, cloud applications, permissions and sharing
- `gr12-malware-kinds-t2` (T2): viruses, Trojans, ransomware, spyware, adware, keyloggers (described, never instructed), safeguards
- `gr12-social-media-digital-footprint-t3` (T3): social media platforms, legal and ethical issues, digital footprint and cyber profile, fake news, misinformation, disinformation, defamation, cyberbullying
- `gr12-blogs-vlogs-podcasts-wearables-t3` (T3): blogs, vlogs, podcasts and vodcasts, wikis, personal mobile devices and wearables

## Branch 2, Grade 11 (6 lessons x 14 = 84)
AI and big data (T2); cloud storage as SaaS and reading adverts (T2); IoT, 4IR, VR/AR and fake news (T3); hotspots, Bluetooth, NFC, GPS, 4G/5G and mobile apps (T3); gig economy, phishing and pharming (T3); malware categories and e-commerce/e-banking safety (T4).

## Branch 3, Grade 10 (4 lessons x 14 = 56)
Wearables, RFID, bar and QR codes, NFC, GPS and communication channels (T2); licences including creative commons (T2); ergonomics and green computing (T1); digital citizenship and footprint, netiquette, blogs, vlogs and web applications (T3).

Doubts go in `docs/notes/cat-doubts.md`. Per branch: tsc, full tests, phone check, question-sync dry run, two screenshots.
