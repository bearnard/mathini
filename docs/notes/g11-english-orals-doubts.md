# Grade 11 English HL listening and speaking (orals): points for a teacher to check

Eight lessons added by `feat/g11-eng-orals` (14 questions each, 112 in all), from the CAPS Grade 11 oral slots in the audit: prepared and unprepared speech (T1), debate (T1), dialogue and interview (T2), panel and informal discussion (T2), meetings and meeting procedures (T3), storytelling and praise poems (T3), introducing a speaker and vote of thanks (T4), oral report and review (T4). Nothing existing was edited, moved or hidden. Questions are written to "you".

## Taken from the CAPS text (section 3.1)

Listening and speaking features: speaking process, prepared and unprepared speech, interview phases (introduction, rapport, questions, summary, closure), introducing a speaker, vote of thanks, panel discussion and chairperson duties, informal discussion, debate procedure (resolution, affirmative and negative teams, burden of proof, the 5 to 10 minute break, two rebuttals each). Durations: debates, forum, group and panel discussions 20 to 30 minutes; dialogues 6 to 8; interviews 10 to 15; introducing a speaker and vote of thanks 4 to 5; prepared speeches, report, review 4 to 5; unprepared speeches 2 to 3; storytelling 8 to 10; meetings and procedures 10 to 15.

## Not in the CAPS text, written from general knowledge (please check)

1. **Meetings and procedures**: the CAPS names the slot ("features and conventions; planning, researching, organising, practising and presenting") but lists no features. The lesson teaches the usual formal-meeting set: chairperson, secretary, members, quorum, agenda order (welcome, apologies, minutes of the last meeting, matters arising, general, closure), propose, second, discuss, vote by majority, minutes in the past tense. Schools may use other terms (for example "any other business").
2. **Storytelling**: features (opening, few clear characters, order of events, repetition, ending, voice, pace, pause, gesture) are general oral-storytelling advice, not CAPS wording.
3. **Praise poems**: the lesson describes features only (praise names, repetition and parallel lines, exaggeration, imagery from nature, history and family, performance by an imbongi). It quotes no existing praise poem and no copyrighted text. A first-language teacher should check that "imbongi" and the description of the form are right, and that the isiXhosa tradition is not treated as the only one (the lesson says "in many African traditions").
4. **Oral report and review**: the CAPS gives the duration (4 to 5 minutes) and calls it a prepared speech; the structures of a report and a review are the usual school forms.
5. **Meetings and minutes**: the CAPS writing strand covers "agenda and minutes of meeting" in Terms 1, 3 and 4; this oral lesson only mentions minutes briefly and does not duplicate a writing lesson.
6. **Marks and Paper 4**: no mark allocations are taught in these lessons, because the Grade 11 oral mark split and the Paper 4 weighting for Grade 11 are not stated in the CAPS text read for this work.
