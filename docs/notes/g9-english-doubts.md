# Grade 9 English HL transactional texts (Wave A): points for a teacher to confirm

Source: DBE CAPS English Home Language, Senior Phase Grades 7-9 (Grade 9 teaching plans and the text-type table). Every model text is an **original model written for the lesson** with invented names, places and organisations.

## Term 2 branch (diary entry, constitution and policy)

| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Both | Placement in term 2 follows the CAPS writing task ("Diary entry/constitution/policy"). | CAPS plan tables extract poorly; confirm the term against the printed CAPS. |
| 2 | Diary | "Dear Diary," is shown as optional; the date at the top is stressed. | CAPS does not say; widespread practice. |
| 3 | Constitution | The model parts (name, aims, membership, meetings, amendments, adoption date, signature) extend the CAPS list (name, membership, terms of reference, date of adoption, signature, "etc."). The aims clause is taught as the terms of reference. | CAPS says "etc."; a teacher may prefer other headings. |
| 4 | Constitution | "Must" for a duty and "may" for a permission. Older legal texts use "shall". | Style choice; plain-language convention. |
| 5 | Policy | The policy is taught as one short model (purpose, rule, exception, adoption). | CAPS names "policy" with the constitution but gives no separate format. |
