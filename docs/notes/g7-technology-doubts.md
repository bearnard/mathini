# Grade 7 Technology: doubts

## Graphical communication (Term 1)
- Scope is the Grade 7 list in CAPS Technology Gr 7-9, "Communication skills" (Grade 7, Term 1): purpose of graphics; conventions (outlines thin/dark, construction lines thin/feint, hidden detail dashed, scale, dimensioning); free-hand sketching; working drawing = 2D drawing of ONE face; 3D oblique (front view with depth at 45°, squared quadrant paper); 3D artistic single vanishing point perspective with colour, texture and shading. Isometric, double vanishing point and first-angle orthographic projection belong to Grades 8 and 9 and are NOT taught here.
- CAPS says the oblique depth is drawn "at 45°" and does not give the depth length. The lesson shows the depth at half size, the usual way (cabinet oblique) to stop the box looking stretched. If the class teacher uses full depth, the picture and the 10 mm example would change.
- CAPS words the line as "thin/dark" for outlines; the lesson says "thick and dark" (dark and heavier than the construction lines). Please check the wording against the textbook used.
- CAPS Grade 7 uses single vanishing point only as an artistic drawing; the Grade 9 lesson (planned) goes further on texture, shading and an interior view. Grade 7 only teaches the idea (one point on the horizon, back face smaller).
- The scale examples (1:10, 1:20, 1:5, 2:1) are mine; CAPS lists "scale" without ratios. The table top, cupboard, shelf and bolt are made-up sizes.
- Not covered: the free-hand sketching practical and the Mini-PAT drawing tasks (out of scope).
