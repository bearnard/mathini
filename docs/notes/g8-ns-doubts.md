# Grade 8 Natural Sciences: doubts and decisions

## Term 2 (feat/g8-ns-t2)
- Scope follows the Grade 8 CAPS text (read from the official PDF, Term 2, Matter and Materials). Density is qualitative in Grade 8 ("the amount of mass in a given volume"); there is no density formula and no calculation. The one numeric example compares the mass of equal volumes using rounded teaching values (water about 1 g per cm³, cooking oil about 0,9 g per cm³, ice about 0,92 g per cm³, iron about 7,9 g per cm³, wood about 0,6 g per cm³): they are labelled "about" and are not stated as exact. Questions that use them quote the values.
- Gas pressure: only the CAPS idea (more particles in the same container, more collisions, higher pressure). The effect of heating on pressure is stated by the CAPS as not for Grade 8 and is not taught.
- Atoms: the picture shows the first 20 elements with electron shells 2, 8, 8, 2 (the CAPS model task). Neutron numbers and isotopes are Grade 10 and are not taught. Atoms are drawn as a simple model, not to scale.
- Reactions: reactants, products, rearranged atoms and bonds breaking and forming, with word and symbol equations. Balancing is Grade 9 CAPS: the equations shown are already balanced and the learner is only asked to identify reactants and products and to count atoms.
- Solar System is not rebuilt here (the existing lesson is in the wrong term, Wave B).
- Not covered: the practical activities (ball and ring, ether and potassium permanganate diffusion, popit-bead models, electrolysis of copper chloride).
