# Grade 8 Afrikaans FAL transactional texts (Wave A): items for a first-language teacher to check

Nothing here has been checked by a first-language speaker. Every Afrikaans word, phrase and sentence shown in a lesson is listed with its English gloss (a test fails if a lesson shows an Afrikaans phrase that is not on this list). Source for the text types: DBE CAPS Afrikaans FAL Grades 7-9 (sp_afr_b). Sentences are short and regular on purpose.

## Points that need a teacher's opinion

| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Friendly letter | Greeting `Liewe Thandi,` and closings `Jou vriend,` / `Jou vriendin,` / `Groete`; `Ek hoop jy is gesond.` | Standard; please confirm these are natural for a Grade 8 letter. |
| 2 | Friendly letter | `Ons het na die strand gegaan.` as the model past sentence. | Regular het + ge- perfect. |
| 3 | Investigative report | Headings `bevindinge`, `gevolgtrekking`, `aanbeveling`; `vraelys` for questionnaire. | Textbooks differ in the headings. |
| 4 | Investigative report | `loop skool toe` (walk to school) and `ry met die taxi / bus`. | Colloquial but standard. |
| 5 | Instructions | Command form without a pronoun (`Vou die papier.`) and the order words eerste, dan, daarna, laastens. | Standard. |
| 6 | Directions | `links`, `regs`, `reguit`, `langs`, `teenoor`, `verby`. | `teenoor` for opposite is standard. |
| 7 | Dialogue | Layout `Thandi: ...` and `toneelaanwysing` for stage direction. | The word is the author's choice; textbooks may say `speelaanwysing`. |
| 8 | Interview | The polite `u` for an older person or a stranger; `Dankie vir u tyd.` | Standard. |
| 9 | All | Names and places are invented. | Placeholders so that no real person is named. |

| 10 | Letter to the press | `Geagte Redakteur,`, `Eerstens ... Tweedens ...`, `Ek stem nie saam nie.`, `Ek vra die raad om ... oop te hou.` and the closing `Vriendelike groete`. | Formal letter conventions differ; the closing follows the existing formal lessons. |
| 11 | Newspaper article | `opskrif`, `skrywer`, `aanhaling`; the question words wie, wat, waar, wanneer, hoekom; `Hy het gesê: ...` for a quotation. | Textbooks use `skrywer` or `verslaggewer` for the writer. |
| 12 | Diary entry | Same forms as the Grade 9 diary lesson, simpler. | Standard. |
| 13 | News report | `feite`, `mening`, `objektief`, `bron` and `Volgens die munisipaliteit ...`. | Standard. |
