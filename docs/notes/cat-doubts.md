# Computer Applications Technology, 2024 amendment: points for a teacher to confirm

Source: the 2024 CAPS amendment (DBE Circular S9 of 2025). Comparison: `audit/cat-2024-amendment-check.md`.

## Grade 12 (Terms 2 and 3)
| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cloud, grid, streaming | Grid computing is "many computers share one large task"; cloud is "a service you use". | Textbooks vary on how the two overlap. |
| 2 | Cloud sharing | View, comment and edit permissions are generic. | Products name them differently. |
| 3 | Malware kinds | Described only; the list follows the amendment (viruses, Trojans, ransomware, spyware, adware, keyloggers) plus worms. | None. |
| 4 | Social media | Misinformation is "by mistake", disinformation is "on purpose". | Common distinction; the amendment gives the words without definitions. |
| 5 | Social media | Defamation is described in everyday terms, not as a legal test. | A teacher may add the legal detail. |
| 6 | Blogs, vlogs, podcasts | A vodcast is "a series of video episodes". | The term is now rare. |
| 7 | Wearables | Etiquette examples (silence in lessons) are common courtesy, not CAPS wording. | None. |

## Grade 11 (Terms 2 to 4)
| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI and big data | AI is "software that learns from data". Big data is described by volume, variety and velocity. | The amendment gives "what is big data in simple terms" and "how it relates to AI" without a list. |
| 2 | Cloud storage and adverts | SaaS is "software you use online that the provider runs". The advert example compares two laptops. | Examples are invented, not from a past paper. |
| 3 | IoT, 4IR, VR/AR, fake news | VR and AR are defined in one line each. | Textbooks differ at the edge of AR and MR. |
| 4 | Wireless | NFC is "very short range, tap to pay or share"; hotspots are personal or public. | General descriptions. |
| 5 | Gig economy | Described neutrally with a benefit and a drawback. | None. |
| 6 | Phishing and pharming, malware | Described only; nothing is instructed. | None. |
| 7 | E-banking | SSL, https and extra verification are mentioned as habits. | The amendment says "for example SSL / https lock". |

## Grade 10 (Terms 1 to 3)
| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ergonomics and green computing | The 20-20-20 rule is a common guideline. | Not CAPS wording. |
| 2 | Wearables, RFID, QR, NFC, GPS | A bar code is "a number the system looks up". | Simplified. |
| 3 | Licences and creative commons | Four common conditions are listed (attribution, non-commercial, no derivatives, share alike). | Real licences combine them in set ways. |
| 4 | Digital citizenship and blogs | A wiki is "a site that many people can edit". | None. |
