# Computer Applications Technology, 2024 amendment: points for a teacher to confirm

Source: the 2024 CAPS amendment (DBE Circular S9 of 2025). Comparison: `audit/cat-2024-amendment-check.md`.

## Grade 12 (Terms 2 and 3)
| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cloud, grid, streaming | Grid computing is "many computers share one large task"; cloud is "a service you use". | Textbooks vary on how the two overlap. |
| 2 | Cloud sharing | View, comment and edit permissions are generic. | Products name them differently. |
| 3 | Malware kinds | Described only; the list follows the amendment (viruses, Trojans, ransomware, spyware, adware, keyloggers) plus worms. | None. |
| 4 | Social media | Misinformation is "by mistake", disinformation is "on purpose". | Common distinction; the amendment gives the words without definitions. |
| 5 | Social media | Defamation is described in everyday terms, not as a legal test. | A teacher may add the legal detail. |
| 6 | Blogs, vlogs, podcasts | A vodcast is "a series of video episodes". | The term is now rare. |
| 7 | Wearables | Etiquette examples (silence in lessons) are common courtesy, not CAPS wording. | None. |
