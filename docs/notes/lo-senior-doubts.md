# Senior Phase Life Orientation: teacher-check list

Every health, legal and religion fact below should be confirmed by a teacher or a health professional before release. Rules followed: ages 12-15; factual and non-graphic; the victim is never blamed ("it is never your fault"); no stigma; help sources only Childline 116, GBV Command Centre 0800 428 428, National AIDS Helpline 0800 012 322, SAPS 10111; questions ask the learner directly and never mention learners or CAPS.

## Grade 8 (feat/lo8-gaps)
| # | Lesson | Item to check | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sexuality | Whole lesson | Values-based only: feelings, influences, respect, consent as respect, saying no, where to ask. No anatomy or sexual activity. "A person who is too young cannot give consent to an adult" is stated at a general level. |
| 2 | Sexuality | Childline described as "a free helpline for children" | Confirm current description and that 116 is free from all networks. |

| 5 | HIV and AIDS | Definition of HIV and AIDS; transmission fluids (blood, semen, vaginal fluids, breast milk); routes (sex without a condom, shared needles and sharp objects with blood, mother to baby); non-routes (hugging, sharing food or plates, mosquitoes, toilets, pools, coughing). | Confirm wording with a health professional. |
| 6 | HIV and AIDS | Prevention (wait to have sex, condom used correctly, never share needles, gloves or covering cuts); testing at a clinic with counselling; antiretroviral treatment taken every day keeps the virus under control and people healthy. U=U and PrEP are deliberately not taught. | Confirm wording and that testing and treatment at public clinics are described correctly. |
| 7 | HIV and AIDS | "AIDS is a later stage"; "untreated for a long time". | Confirm. |

| 10 | Human rights violations | Types and counter-strategies; the South African Human Rights Commission described as dealing with human rights complaints. | Confirm. |
