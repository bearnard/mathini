# Senior Phase Life Orientation: teacher-check list

Every health, legal and religion fact below should be confirmed by a teacher or a health professional before release. Rules followed: ages 12-15; factual and non-graphic; the victim is never blamed ("it is never your fault"); no stigma; help sources only Childline 116, GBV Command Centre 0800 428 428, National AIDS Helpline 0800 012 322, SAPS 10111; questions ask the learner directly and never mention learners or CAPS.

## Grade 8 (feat/lo8-gaps)
