# Grade 8 English HL: the transactional texts we do not teach (plan)

Status: PLAN, sent to the orchestrator before any lesson is written. ADDITIVE ONLY: no existing lesson is deleted, moved, renamed or rewritten. Same approach as Grade 9 (`g9-english-transactional-plan.md`).

## Sources
DBE CAPS English Home Language, Senior Phase Grades 7-9 (`sp_eng_b`), Grade 8 teaching plans and formal tasks; `audit/caps-alignment.md`, "Grade 8 English" (the transactional gaps and the wrong-term list).

## Shape: 8 lessons x 14 questions = 112, two branches
| Branch | CAPS term | Topic id | Title | Elements taught |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| feat/g8-eng-transactional-a | 1 | `gr8-friendly-letter-t1` | Friendly (informal) letter | purpose, address and date, greeting, paragraphs, informal register, closing, writing from a visual stimulus |
| | 1 | `gr8-investigative-report-t1` | Investigative report and questionnaire | question and aim, a questionnaire, findings, headings, impersonal style, conclusion and recommendation |
| | 2 | `gr8-instructions-directions-t2` | Instruction text and directions | goal, materials, numbered steps, imperative verbs, sequence words, giving directions, landmarks |
| | 2 | `gr8-dialogue-and-interview-t2` | Dialogue and written interview | format of a dialogue (speakers, turn-taking, stage directions), paragraph conventions, interview questions and answers, introduction and closure |
| feat/g8-eng-transactional-b | 3 | `gr8-letter-to-the-press-t3` | Letter to the press | reacting to an article, address to the editor, a clear view, reasons, polite and persuasive tone, closing |
| | 4 | `gr8-newspaper-magazine-article-t4` | Newspaper and magazine article | headline, by-line, lead (who, what, where, when, why), body in order of importance, quotations, facts and opinion |
| | 4 | `gr8-diary-entry-t4` | Diary entry | date, first person, events and feelings, informal register, reflection |
| | 4 | `gr8-journalistic-report-t4` | Journalistic (news) report | objective tone, facts first, sources, past tense, headline and lead, difference from an article |

Each lesson: 4 slides (rule, an ORIGINAL model text with invented South African names, a labelled parts list, common mistake, a "Check yourself") and 14 server-markable questions (layout, which part belongs where, register, spot the error, which sentence suits the purpose). Placement follows the CAPS term of the writing task. Existing lessons are untouched; the English wrong-term issues in the audit are left for a Wave B decision.

## Afrikaans FAL and isiXhosa FAL (second and third part)
- **Afrikaans FAL (Grade 8):** same shape after English is merged: the CAPS Grade 8 transactional texts of the audit (directions, e-mail or letter, diary, and so on) with a doubts file for a mother-tongue teacher (`docs/notes/g8-afrikaans-doubts.md`).
- **isiXhosa FAL (Grade 8):** no learner-facing lessons or questions. A teacher-review pack only (CAPS topic list, model skeletons with English glosses, what a first-language reviewer must write or check), as for Grade 9.

## Accuracy and care
All model texts are original (invented names, places and organisations). SA spelling and decimal comma. Anything that depends on a textbook convention (for example "Yours sincerely" vs "Yours faithfully", how a headline is capitalised) goes in `docs/notes/g8-english-doubts.md` for a teacher.
