# Grade 7 Afrikaans FAL text types (Wave A): items for a first-language teacher to check

Nothing here has been checked by a first-language speaker. Every Afrikaans word, phrase and sentence shown in a lesson is listed with its English gloss (a test fails if a lesson shows an Afrikaans phrase that is not on this list). Source for the text types: DBE CAPS Afrikaans FAL Grades 7-9 (sp_afr_b) and the Grade 7 audit (informal letter, poem and dialogue, instructions and directions, advertisement and poster, forms and questionnaires, notice, agenda and minutes, newspaper article and interview, diary, e-mail and flyer). Sentences are short and regular on purpose, in the house style of the Grade 8 and Grade 9 Afrikaans lessons. Each lesson has 4 slides and 12 questions (the same as the Grade 8 and 9 Afrikaans lessons; the plan said 14).

## Points that need a teacher's opinion

| # | Lesson | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Informal letter | Greeting `Liewe Sipho,` and closings `Jou vriend,` and `Groete`; `Hoe gaan dit met jou?`, `Ek hoop jy is gesond.`, `Skryf gou terug.` | Standard; please confirm they are natural for a Grade 7 letter. `Hoogagtend` and `Geagte Meneer` appear only as the formal forms that are wrong for a friend. |
| 2 | Informal letter | `Ek het 'n nuwe fiets gekry.` and `Wat doen jy in die vakansie?` as model sentences. | Regular, simple. |
| 3 | Poem and dialogue | Rhyme pairs `kat` and `mat`, `hond` and `mond`, `huis` and `muis`; `reël` (line), `vers` (stanza), `rym` (rhyme). | Standard words; please confirm `vers` for stanza (some books say `strofe`). |
| 4 | Poem and dialogue | Dialogue layout `Thandi: Waar is my tas?` with `dubbelpunt` (colon) and `vraagteken` (question mark). | Standard. |
| 5 | Instructions and directions | Commands without a pronoun (`Vou die papier.`, `Sny die papier.`, `Plak dit op die kaart.`) and the order words eerste, dan, daarna, laastens; `Eerste vou jy die papier.` | The verb comes second after the order word; please confirm the word order. |
| 6 | Directions | `links`, `regs`, `reguit`, `Gaan reguit.`, `Draai links by die robot.` (`robot` for a traffic light). | `robot` is the South African word. |
| 7 | Advertisement and poster | `nuut`, `spesiaal`, `goedkoop`, `gratis`, `Gratis roomys!`, `Net R100.`, `Almal is welkom!` | `Net` is used for only; please confirm. |
| 8 | Forms and questionnaires | `vorm`, `vraelys`, `naam` (first name), `van` (surname), `geboortedatum`, `handtekening`, `blokletters`, and the commands `Vul die vorm in.`, `Skryf in blokletters.`, `Merk een blokkie.`, `Onderteken die vorm.` | `Merk een blokkie` (tick one box) and `Onderteken` (sign) are the author's choices; textbooks differ. |
| 9 | Forms and questionnaires | `Loop jy skool toe?` (Do you walk to school?) as the sample question. | Colloquial but standard. |
| 10 | Notice, agenda and minutes | `kennisgewing`, `agenda`, `notule`, `vergadering`, `verskonings`, `besluit`, `teenwoordig`, `Welkom`, `Sluiting`; `Die vergadering het om 14:00 begin.`; `Besluit: Die basaar is op 20 Junie.` | Textbooks differ in the agenda headings; `basaar` is used for a school fete. |
| 11 | Newspaper report and interview | `koerantberig`, `opskrif`, `Deur Thandi Mokoena`, the five questions Wie? Wat? Waar? Wanneer? Hoekom?, `onderhoud`, `Vraag` and `Antwoord`. | `Laerskool Wyk 12` is used for Ward 12 Primary School; please confirm the wording. |
| 12 | Newspaper report and interview | `Hoekom het julle bome geplant?` and `Ons wil die skool groen maak.` | Simple past with het and ge-. |
| 13 | Diary, e-mail and flyer | `Liewe Dagboek,`, `Gister het ons bome geplant.`, `Ek was bly.`; `Aan`, `Onderwerp`, `Geagte Mev. Pillay,`, `Vriendelike groete`; `strooibiljet`. | The same forms as the Grade 9 diary and e-mail lessons. |
| 14 | All | Names and numbers are invented (Thandi Mokoena, Mev. Pillay, Mnr. Dlamini, 082 000 0000). | Placeholders so that no real person is named. |
| 15 | All | The questions are asked in Afrikaans, with English meanings in the explanations. The wrong options are Afrikaans words that are clearly different in meaning (for example the options for a meaning question are all text-type words from the same lesson). | Please check that no wrong option could be a correct answer in standard Afrikaans. |
