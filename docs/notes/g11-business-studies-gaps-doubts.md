# Grade 11 Business Studies gaps: doubts and decisions

## Intellectual property rights (T1), Trade unions and the Labour Relations Act (T1), Citizenship roles and responsibilities (T3)
- CAPS (Grade 11 Business Studies): Term 1, socio-economic issues: piracy and the nature and definition of copyright, patent and trade mark; industrial relations: strikes, go-slows and lockouts, the Labour Relations Act (nature and purpose), what a trade union is, history, roles and functions. Term 3 week 2: citizenship roles and responsibilities: the role of citizens and the business community, giving back, and the role of business, civil society, NGOs and CBOs.
- CAPS-level only, as approved: no Act section numbers, no wage or membership thresholds, no registration fees or patent term in years ("a limited period"). The lessons are qualitative, so there are no computed numbers to test; the test checks the standard lesson shape, 14 valid curated questions each, and that key words of every answer appear in the lesson.
- History is kept to two safe facts: many black workers were not legally recognised with union rights until the late 1970s, and COSATU was formed in 1985. If the orchestrator prefers no dates, drop the 1985 question and the history card.
- The CCMA and workplace forum are mentioned in one line each because CAPS lists workplace forums (conflict management) and disputes; they are not developed further.
- Case scenarios in the citizenship lesson use imaginary businesses (a bakery, a supermarket); no real company is named as a donor.
- Wave B list (nothing moved) is unchanged in `g11-commerce-gaps-plan.md`.
