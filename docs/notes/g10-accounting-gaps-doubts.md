# Grade 10 Accounting gaps: doubts

Branch `feat/g10-acc-vat`: 4 lessons x 14 questions (VAT concepts T2, VAT calculations and the VAT Control account T2, internal control components T1, cost concepts and budget types T4).

- VAT rate: 15% (current standard rate). If the rate changes, `VAT_RATE` in `acc10.ts` and the bank text (R30, R57 500, R4 500 and so on) must change together; the tests recompute in BigInt cents.
- Zero-rated and exempt lists are general (brown bread, maize meal, milk, exports; financial services, home rent, education). No registration thresholds, no section numbers, no VAT 201 form detail.
- Budget horizons (short, medium, long term) are named without fixed year counts, because CAPS gives none.
- All businesses and figures are imaginary teaching data.
