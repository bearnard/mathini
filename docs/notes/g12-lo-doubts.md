# Grade 12 Life Orientation Wave A: teacher-check list

Every health fact below should be confirmed by a teacher or health professional.

- **Hypertension** "silent": often has no symptoms; only a blood pressure check shows it.
- **TB** spreads through the air when a sick person coughs; crowded, badly ventilated rooms spread it more easily; curable if the full course of medicine is taken; stopping early can make it harder to treat.
- **HIV**: treatment (ARVs) lets a person stay healthy and lowers the chance of passing HIV on. The lesson says that public clinics in South Africa test for HIV and TB and give treatment free of charge; confirm the wording.
- **Smoking** is a major cause of lung cancer and heart disease.
- **Physical activity**: the example target is 150 minutes of moderate activity a week (the adult guideline). Young people are advised more, about 60 minutes a day. The lesson uses 150 as an example target only; confirm.
- **Unemployment rate** is taught as unemployed ÷ labour force (the narrow definition); confirm it matches the textbook.
- **SARS tax**: concept only (SARS collects tax, PAYE, tax number, annual return, evasion is a crime). No rates, brackets or threshold amounts are taught. Confirm the depth.
- **Corruption reporting**: the lesson names the police and the National Anti-Corruption Hotline and says a caller may stay anonymous; no hotline number is printed. Confirm.
- The four non-CAPS Grade 12 LO lessons (Community and Social Action, Risk Management and Support, Active Citizenship and Leadership, Transition and Personal Planning) are NOT hidden yet; they are hidden only after these five lessons are live and orch has been told.
