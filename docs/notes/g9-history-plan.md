## Open points (the doubts file will carry them)
1. Figures for the internment and for Japanese atrocities in China are given only as "more than 100 000" and "very large numbers"; contested figures are left out.
2. CAPS says people descended from ancestors in Africa "as recently as 100 000 years ago"; the lesson uses the CAPS wording and notes that scientists give earlier dates for the first modern humans.
