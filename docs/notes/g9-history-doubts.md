# Grade 9 History additions (Wave A): points for a teacher to confirm

Source: DBE CAPS Social Sciences Grades 7-9, Grade 9 History (Term 1 p. 40, Term 3 p. 43, the project p. 14). All sources in the lessons are original or paraphrased descriptions, labelled as such. Dates and numbers are in `src/curriculum/grade9/history/_shared/facts.ts` and asserted in `g9HistoryAdditions.test.ts`.

## World War II in the Pacific (Term 1)

| # | What | Why it is uncertain |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The camps for Japanese Americans: "more than 100 000 people, about two thirds of them American citizens", Executive Order 9066 on 19 February 1942, the apology and compensation in 1988. | Standard figures; the totals vary slightly between sources, so the lesson gives a floor ("more than"). |
| 2 | Japanese atrocities in China are described with Nanjing (December 1937) as one example and **no casualty figures**. | Casualty figures for Nanjing are disputed; the lesson says "very large numbers". |
| 3 | The attack on Pearl Harbor is described as coming "without a declaration of war"; the reasons for the attack are given in three short points. | A simplification of a debated topic. |
| 4 | Prisoners of war: "treated very harshly, with forced labour", no figures and no detail of mistreatment. | Kept brief and age-appropriate on purpose. |
| 5 | The end of the war in the Pacific (the atomic bombs) is not repeated here: it is in the Term 2 lesson. | Avoids overlap with an existing lesson. |
